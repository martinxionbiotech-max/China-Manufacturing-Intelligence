## Grounding Evidence (Phase 1 anchors)

- **MIIT 45 National Advanced Manufacturing Clusters** (工业和信息化部, Nov 2022)
  — Source: 新华网 / 工信部官网. 2021 combined leading-industry output ≈ ¥19 trillion.
  Category split: New-gen IT ×13, High-end equipment ×13, New materials ×7,
  Biomedical & medical devices ×5, Consumer goods ×4, NEV & ICV ×3.
- **MIIT 35 additional National Advanced Manufacturing Clusters** (Dec 2024)
  — Source: 中国政府网 / 工信部. Adds aerospace, shipbuilding, agri-machinery,
  machine tools (工业母机), scientific instruments, PV (photovoltaic), rare earth,
  special steel, textile/garment, sports products, ICV/NEV clusters.
- **China Integrated City Index 2024 — "China City Manufacturing Influence"**
  — Top 10 manufacturing cities = 42.4% of national exports, 21.8% of mfg workforce.
  9 of top 10 in Yangtze River Delta + Pearl River Delta. Suzhou #2, Ningbo #5.

## Rules (hard constraints, inherited from prompt)

1. Never write from memory when verification is possible.
2. Never invent manufacturers, locations, markets, production numbers, rankings,
   market share, certifications.
3. Never convert "supplier" → "manufacturer", or "located in city" → "factory there".
4. Distinguish FACT / INFERENCE / OPINION explicitly.
5. Evidence hierarchy: primary (gov/customs/associations/company) → strong secondary
   (industry press/academic) → discovery (B2B platforms/blogs; discovery only).
6. Quality gate before publish: Research ≥8, Evidence ≥8, Originality ≥8 (of /10).
7. Quantity is secondary to information density. Network effect over page count.
