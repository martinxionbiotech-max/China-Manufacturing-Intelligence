## B. GEOGRAPHIC HIERARCHY (地理层级)

Model: **Province (省/自治区/直辖市) → City (地级市) → District/County
(区/县/县级市) → Town (镇) → Cluster (产业集群/专业镇)**.

### Manufacturing provinces (31 province-level units, ranked by mfg weight)

**Core manufacturing belts (P0):**
- **Guangdong (广东)** — electronics, appliances, furniture, lighting, textiles, toys, batteries, EVs
- **Jiangsu (江苏)** — electronics, machinery, textiles, chemicals, PV, shipbuilding, machine tools
- **Zhejiang (浙江)** — hardware, small appliances, textiles, pumps, valves, commodity, machine tools
- **Shandong (山东)** — machinery, chemicals, tires, home appliances, agri-machinery, shipbuilding
- **Fujian (福建)** — footwear, garments, ceramics, stone, sports products, batteries (Ningde)

**Strong manufacturing provinces (P1):**
- **Anhui (安徽)** — home appliances (Hefei), EVs, PV, intelligent voice
- **Hubei (湖北)** — automotive (Wuhan), optoelectronics, steel

- **Henan (河南)** — food, aluminum, machinery, textiles, superhard materials
- **Sichuan (四川)** — electronics (Chengdu), aerospace, energy equipment

**Secondary manufacturing provinces (P2):**
- Liaoning (辽宁), Jiangxi (江西), Shaanxi (陕西), Guangxi (广西),
- Chongqing (重庆·直辖市), Tianjin (天津·直辖市), Beijing (北京·直辖市),
- Shanghai (上海·直辖市), Yunnan (云南), Guizhou (贵州), Inner Mongolia (内蒙古),
- Xinjiang (新疆), Gansu (甘肃), Shanxi (山西), Jilin (吉林), Heilongjiang (黑龙江),
- Hainan (海南), Qinghai (青海), Ningxia (宁夏), Tibet (西藏)

## E. TOP 200 PRODUCT CATEGORIES (draft taxonomy)

Products are the highest-value SEO layer. Grouped by industry. This is a
**taxonomy skeleton** — each product becomes a "Where is [Product] manufactured
in China?" research page in Phase 2/5. Full 200-item list maintained in
`data/product-taxonomy.md` (to be expanded); below is the P0/P1 shortlist.

### Electronics (P0 products)
Smartphones, PCBs (printed circuit boards), semiconductors/IC, displays/LCD-OLED,
LED lighting chips, connectors, cables & wire harnesses, power adapters, audio
devices (earbuds/speakers), cameras/CCTV, drones, IoT modules, batteries (Li-ion
cells), chargers.

### Electrical Equipment (P0)
Electric motors, transformers, circuit breakers, switchgear, relays, power tools,
cables & wires, solar inverters, UPS, generators, windings/magnets.

### Machinery (P0)
CNC machine tools, injection molding machines, die-casting machines, pumps,
valves, bearings, gearboxes, compressors, agricultural machinery, construction
machinery (excavators/loaders/cranes), textile machinery.

### Automotive / EV (P0)
EV battery packs, EV motors, EV charging stations, auto parts (brakes/axles/
suspension), tires, automotive electronics.

### Hardware & Tools (P1)
Hand tools, fasteners (screws/bolts), locks, hinges, plumbing fixtures, drill bits,
saws, wrenches, cutting tools.

### Home Appliances (P1)
Air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines, rice cookers, water heaters,
microwave ovens, small kitchen appliances, fans.

### Lighting (P1)
LED bulbs, LED panels, chandeliers, street lights, solar lights, strip lights,
downlights, desk lamps.

### Furniture & Home (P1)
Sofa, office furniture, mattresses, cabinets, ceramic tiles, sanitary ware,
curtains, carpets, doors & windows.

### Textiles & Garments (P1)
Yarn, fabric, socks, underwear, knitwear, down jackets, workwear, bedding.

### Footwear & Sports (P1)
Sneakers, leather shoes, sports equipment, outdoor gear, yoga mats.

### Toys & Commodities (P2)
Plastic toys, plush toys, Christmas decorations, stationery, giftware, umbrellas.

### Chemicals & Materials (P2)
Plastics (pellets), rubber, coatings/paints, adhesives, rare earth magnets,
nano materials, carbon fiber, aluminum profiles, steel products.

### Medical (P2)
Medical devices, PPE, masks, gloves, diagnostic equipment, surgical instruments.

## F. Relationship Model (high-level)

```
Province ──contains──> City ──contains──> District ──contains──> Town/Cluster
Cluster ──specializes_in──> Industry
Industry ──produces──> Product
Product ──requires──> Component ──requires──> Raw Material
Cluster ──hosts──> Manufacturer ──produces──> Product
Cluster ──hosts──> Industrial Park
City ──served_by──> Port
Cluster ──exports_to──> Export Market
Product ──maps_to──> HS Code
Manufacturer ──holds──> Certification
Manufacturer ──has──> Export Evidence
```
