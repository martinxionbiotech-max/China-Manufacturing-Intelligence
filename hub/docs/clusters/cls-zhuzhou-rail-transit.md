## Verified facts

- 2023 cluster scale exceeded RMB 160B, >30% of national industry output; products exported to 100+ countries across six continents.  
  *Source: src-gxt-hunan-20241224, src-rednet*
- World's largest rail transit equipment manufacturing base; China's most complete rail-transit industry chain; first 100-billion rail transit cluster globally.  
  *Source: src-gxt-hunan-20240826*
- 5 CRRC first-tier subsidiaries + 400+ upstream/downstream enterprises; 80%+ local supply rate; 80%+ of components within 5 km radius.  
  *Source: src-sasac, src-hunan-gov-20240816*
- Founded 1936 (Yuehan Railway works); China's first mainline electric locomotive 1958; cumulative 10,000+ electric locomotives, 60+ models.  
  *Source: src-sasac-en-20230509*
- CRRC ZELC can assemble 18 electric locomotives simultaneously; delivered a ~10,000-part European train in 18 months (half of international peers).  
  *Source: src-sasac-en-20230509*
- Fuxing traction & network control systems developed by CRRC Zhuzhou Institute; metro traction/inverter systems world #1 market share (reported).  
  *Source: src-rmlt-20240708, src-gxt-hunan-20240826*
- 84 international standards + 112 national standards led/participated by Zhuzhou firms.  
  *Source: src-hnzy-2023*
