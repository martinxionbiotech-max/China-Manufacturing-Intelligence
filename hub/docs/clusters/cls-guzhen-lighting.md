## Verified facts

- Guzhen (<50 km2) holds ~70% of China's domestic lighting market and ~50% of the global market; exports to 130+ (some sources 180+) countries.  
  *Source: src-xinhua-20241122, src-oeeee-20251023*
- Cluster centered on Guzhen, radiating 3 cities/11 towns, with annual output value exceeding RMB 100B.  
  *Source: src-oeeee-20251023, src-stcn*
- 30,000+ lighting business entities in the town (some sources 45,000+ registered); 200,000+ industry workers.  
  *Source: src-xinhua-20241122, src-stcn*
- Originated 1982 with the first self-made lamp copied from a Hong Kong sample; 2002 named 'China Lighting Capital'.  
  *Source: src-zsdag, src-stcn*
- China's overall lighting industry reached RMB 600B annual sales; 2024 export US$56.1B; 2025 Jan-Aug export US$34.4B (-6.7% YoY).  
  *Source: src-oeeee-20251023*
- Guzhen Lighting Fair (since 1999) is the Asia-Pacific's largest lighting exhibition; 33rd edition drew ~160,000 visitors, 4,800+ overseas buyers from 144 countries.  
  *Source: src-zsnews-20251026, src-gdtoday*
