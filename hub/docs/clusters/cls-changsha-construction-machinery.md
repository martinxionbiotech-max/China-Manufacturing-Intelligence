## Verified facts

- 2021 cluster output exceeded RMB 280B (~1/3 national); 2022 fell to ~RMB 200B (industry downturn); 2024 recovered to >RMB 250B (>30% national share).  
  *Source: src-hunan-gov-20250427, src-stcn-2024*
- World's 3rd largest construction machinery agglomeration (after US Illinois and Japan Tokyo); asset/revenue/profit #1 nationally for 12+ consecutive years.  
  *Source: src-stcn-2024*
- 5 companies in global top-50 (Sany, Zoomlion, Sunward, CRCHI, Sinoboom); Changsha is the world's 2nd city with 5 top-50 firms (after Tokyo).  
  *Source: src-chinadaily-20230301*
- 2024 cluster export RMB 29.06B (+22.9%); overseas revenue CAGR >50%; Sany/Zoomlion/Sunward overseas revenue share >50%.  
  *Source: src-chinadaily-20250515, src-hunan-gov-20250427*
- Cluster produces 12 categories, 100+ sub-categories, ~500 model specs — 70% of national construction machinery variety.  
  *Source: src-hunan-gov-202105*
- Local supply rate rose from 16.3% (2019) to >22% (2022); in-province rate 32.2%.  
  *Source: src-stcn-2024, src-hunan-gov-202105*
- 2022: key enterprises' average R&D intensity >6.5%, R&D spend >RMB 20B; 20+ national & 100+ provincial innovation platforms.  
  *Source: src-scol-20240407*
