## Verified facts

- 2023 Foshan ceramics industry total output value RMB 101.97B (超千亿); ceramic tile output 745M m2 (peak 1.22B m2 in 2010).  
  *Source: src-taocixinxi-20240902*
- Foshan ceramic tile output historically 30-40% of China's national total (peak ~50% in 2006); 2023 export RMB 11.45B (~32.97% of national).  
  *Source: src-fstcwy, src-jiemian*
- 8,000+ registered ceramics entities, ~2,000 brands; 152 high-tech firms; listed firms include Dongpeng, Keda, Monalisa, Arrow.  
  *Source: src-taocixinxi-20240902*
- Origin 1983: Shiwan Lihua imported Italy's glazed-tile line (start of modern Chinese building ceramics); 2002 China Ceramics City opened.  
  *Source: src-nfnews-20220601*
- Ceramic machinery: ~80% of China's ceramic presses and 85% of polished-tile presses made in Foshan (Lital, Keda).  
  *Source: src-baike*
- 'Foshan Ceramics' collective trademark registered 2020 (after 15 years, 3 rejections).  
  *Source: src-taocixinxi-20240902*
