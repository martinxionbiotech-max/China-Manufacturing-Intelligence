## Verified facts

- 2022 electronics manufacturing output was RMB 2.48 trillion, about 1/6 of China's national total, ranked #1 among mainland cities for years.  
  *Source: src-people-sz-20220407*
- 2024: Shenzhen industrial output above designated size reached RMB 5.4 trillion; total industrial added value RMB 1.24 trillion; #1 nationally in both for 3 consecutive years.  
  *Source: src-sz-gov-2024-annual*
- 2024: computer/communication/electronic-equipment manufacturing added value grew 11.0% YoY.  
  *Source: src-sz-gov-2024-annual*
- More than 4,100 electronics manufacturers above designated size; 5 enterprises >RMB 100B; 27 >RMB 10B; ~400 >RMB 500M; 21 in China's top-100 electronics list.  
  *Source: src-people-sz-20220407*
- Network communication + smart terminal segments ~RMB 1 trillion; UHD display + semiconductor/IC segments >RMB 100 billion.  
  *Source: src-people-sz-20220407*
- ~1 in 7 smartphones globally is 'made in Shenzhen'; Shenzhen produces ~1/2 of global communication base stations.  
  *Source: src-c114-20240123*
- Huaqiangbei (Futian District) named 'China's No.1 Electronics Street' in 2008; world's largest electronics trading hub.  
  *Source: src-stcn-20230727*
- 2024: Shenzhen total trade RMB 4.5 trillion (+16.4%), exports RMB 2.81 trillion (+14.6%) — 32 consecutive years #1 among mainland cities; returned to 'No.1 foreign-trade city'.  
  *Source: src-sz-customs-2024*
- 2024: electromechanical product exports RMB 2 trillion (+12.1%), 71% of total; traditional electronics (phones/PCs/home appliances) RMB 377.43B (+6.8%); 'new three' (lithium batteries/EV/PV) RMB 99.69B (+11.5%); drones RMB 14.57B (+19.6%, national #1).  
  *Source: src-sz-customs-2024*
- 2024: Shenzhen port container throughput exceeded 30M TEU (+13.7%); Yantian port alone >15.03M TEU.  
  *Source: src-sz-jtys-20241128, src-people-hwb-20250122*
- Designated a MIIT National Advanced Manufacturing Cluster in 2022.  
  *Source: src-miit-2022*
- 2024: >24,700 national high-tech enterprises in Shenzhen.  
  *Source: src-sz-gov-2024-annual*
