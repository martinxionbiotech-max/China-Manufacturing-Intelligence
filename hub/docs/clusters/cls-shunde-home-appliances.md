## Verified facts

- Shunde home appliance output ~15% of China's national total; export ~10% of national; 80% local sourcing rate; 3,000+ production & component firms.  
  *Source: src-gdeacc, src-time-weekly*
- Shunde annual output value passed RMB 400B (2023-24); world's largest rice cooker and microwave oven supply base; national largest AC/fridge/water-heater/disinfecting-cabinet base.  
  *Source: src-foshanplus, src-cifnews*
- Foshan microwave & water-heater output >40% of global; range hoods ~1/3 global; rice cookers ~1/4 global.  
  *Source: src-sina-20260115, src-stcn*
- China microwave output ~116M units in 2024 (~67% of global); Galanz ~38M units/yr, historically 40-44% global share.  
  *Source: src-faxiangongchang*
- Beijiao: 92 km2, ~200 above-scale appliance firms, >200M units/yr, ~10% of national appliance output value; Midea HQ.  
  *Source: src-xinhua-20220121*
- Midea Shunde microwave plant was Foshan's first WEF 'Lighthouse Factory' (2021); Midea kitchen-heat plant is world's largest dishwasher base (6M units/yr, 145 countries).  
  *Source: src-nfnews, src-cb*
