## Verified facts

- World's largest textile distribution center; ~1/4 of global fabrics traded here; sold to 192-206 countries.  
  *Source: src-xinhua-20250106, src-rmrb-20250430*
- 2024 market turnover RMB 400.99B (first time >400B, +11.09%); 2025 turnover RMB 441.39B (+10.07%).  
  *Source: src-xinhua-20250106, src-zjic-20260325*
- 8,000+ textile enterprises, ~140,000 workers; above-scale textile output value RMB 151.28B.  
  *Source: src-zjic-20260325, src-xinhua-20250514*
- Dyeing & printing capacity ~40% of China's national total; nearly all fabric types covered.  
  *Source: src-rmrb-20250430, src-zjic-20260325*
- Market founded 1988 (first indoor textile market); 1992 renamed China Textile City (first 'China'-named market); grew from 'water cloth market'.  
  *Source: src-gmw-20230209*
- Dyeing consolidation: 212 firms cut to 109, relocated to Lanyin Fashion Town; peak dyeing 18B+ meters/year.  
  *Source: src-gmw-20230209, src-cfej-20240108*
