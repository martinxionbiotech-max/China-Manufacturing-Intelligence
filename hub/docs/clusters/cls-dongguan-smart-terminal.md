## Verified facts

- Designated a MIIT National Advanced Manufacturing Cluster in 2019; ranked #4 on the 2023 China Top-100 Industrial Clusters list.  
  *Source: src-dg-gov-20240105*
- 2022: Dongguan electronics designated-scale output RMB 947.02B; added value RMB 167.34B.  
  *Source: src-dg-gov-20240105*
- 2023: electronics manufacturing output exceeded RMB 1.1 trillion, ~1/4 of Guangdong's total.  
  *Source: src-dg-gov-invest*
- 2022: smartphone production 196 million units — 1 in 6 smartphones made in China came from Dongguan.  
  *Source: src-dg-gov-20240105*
- 2024: Dongguan smartphone shipments ~24% of global total (1 in 5 worldwide); 1 in 8 global phones from Chang'an Town.  
  *Source: src-ourchinastory*
- 2023: Huawei/OPPO/vivo combined output value grew 13.3%, reversing three years of decline.  
  *Source: src-gzdaily-20240410*
- Dongguan has ~220,000 industrial enterprises, ~14,000 above designated scale.  
  *Source: src-dg-gov-invest*
- Chang'an Town 2025 GDP RMB 110B (2nd consecutive '100-billion town'); electronics output RMB 200B+, >3/4 of town's industrial output.  
  *Source: src-ourchinastory*
