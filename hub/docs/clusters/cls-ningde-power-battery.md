## Verified facts

- CATL 2024: global power-battery usage share 37.9% (#1 for 8 straight years); energy-storage battery shipments 36.5% global (#1 for 4 straight years).  
  *Source: src-catl-ar2024*
- CATL 2025: global power-battery share 39.2% (#1 for 9 straight years); energy-storage shipments 30.4% global (#1 for 5 straight years).  
  *Source: src-catl-ar2025*
- CATL 2025 revenue RMB 423.7B (+17%), net profit RMB 72.2B (+42%); lithium battery sales 661 GWh (+39%).  
  *Source: src-catl-ar2025*
- CATL 2025 global production capacity 772 GWh, with 321 GWh under construction.  
  *Source: src-catl-ar2025*
- Ningde city: 330 GWh lithium capacity installed/under construction, plus 170 GWh planned; 90+ upstream/downstream companies clustered.  
  *Source: src-fujian-gov-20260116*
- 2025: Ningde lithium battery + stainless steel cluster output exceeded RMB 300B and RMB 240B respectively.  
  *Source: src-fujian-gov-20260116*
- Ningde power battery cluster is Fujian's ONLY national advanced manufacturing cluster; city named 'China's New Energy Battery Capital'.  
  *Source: src-fjdaily-20221219*
- Fuding CATL base is CATL's largest single project globally — 120 GWh planned, output >RMB 100B.  
  *Source: src-xinhua-fuding*
