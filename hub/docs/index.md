## Methodology

This hub is the structured interface to the China Manufacturing Knowledge Graph. It complements the editorial main site (research, analysis, and comparisons). Facts are classified by evidence level (High) and each carries a source reference. No production, market-share, ranking, or certification figure is invented; where a figure cannot be verified it is omitted.
