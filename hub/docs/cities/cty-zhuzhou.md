## Verified facts

- 2023 株洲轨道交通集群规模超 1600 亿元, 占全国 30%+, 全球最大轨道交通装备制造基地.  
  *Source: [cls-zhuzhou-rail-transit](../clusters/cls-zhuzhou-rail-transit.md)*
- 株洲是全国唯一的中小航空发动机研制基地, 创造新中国航空工业多个第一.  
  *Source: [cls-zhuzhou-aero-engine](../clusters/cls-zhuzhou-aero-engine.md)*
- 2024 industry = 39.9% and manufacturing = 37.4% of GDP — both #1 in Hunan .  
  *Source: 21经济网, "株洲'破'与'立'：一座工业老城的制造新变" (2025).*
- 3 national advanced-manufacturing clusters (rail transit, small aero-engines, UHV power-equipment) + 3 national SME characteristic clusters (ceramics, cemented carbide, power semiconductors) .  
  *Source: 21经济网, "株洲'破'与'立'：一座工业老城的制造新变" (2025).*
- Rail cluster: 425 firms (273 above-scale), anchored by CRRC Zhuzhou Electric Locomotive and CRRC Zhuzhou Motor .  
  *Source: 21经济网, "株洲'破'与'立'：一座工业老城的制造新变" (2025).*
- National advanced-manufacturing top-100 city: from outside top-50 (2020) to #37 (2024) .  
  *Source: 21经济网, "株洲'破'与'立'：一座工业老城的制造新变" (2025).*
