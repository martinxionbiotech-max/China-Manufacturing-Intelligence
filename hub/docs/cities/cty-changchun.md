## Verified facts

- 长春是中国汽车工业摇篮, 1956 年第一辆解放卡车下线; 奥迪 PPE 项目约 50% 供应商布局在工厂 30 公里内.  
  *Source: [cls-changchun-auto](../clusters/cls-changchun-auto.md)*
- 1,034 auto-parts enterprises form the chain's "skeleton": 395 above-scale, 200 with RMB 100M+ revenue, 31 at RMB 1B+, 3 at RMB 5B+, 1 at RMB 10B+ .  
  *Source: "汽势如虹驰骋产业赛道——长春汽车产业集群发展综述" (2024).*
- 6 whole-vehicle plants, 7 auto brands; nearly half of the world's top-100 auto-parts firms have a Changchun presence .  
  *Source: "汽势如虹驰骋产业赛道——长春汽车产业集群发展综述" (2024).*
- 11 national key auto labs (simulation/control), 6 national enterprise tech centers, 21 universities with auto majors .  
  *Source: "汽势如虹驰骋产业赛道——长春汽车产业集群发展综述" (2024).*
- First-batch national "vehicle-road-cloud integration" ICV pilot city (July 2024) .  
  *Source: "汽势如虹驰骋产业赛道——长春汽车产业集群发展综述" (2024).*
