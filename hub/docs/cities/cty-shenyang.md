## Verified facts

- 中科院沈阳自动化所近 50 年机器人研究史, 诞生我国第一台工业机器人样机; 新松(SIASUN)2000 年成立.  
  *Source: [cls-shenyang-robotics](../clusters/cls-shenyang-robotics.md)*
- The robotics cluster is led by 7 academicians and gathers 40+ national experts from the CAS Shenyang Institute of Automation, Institute of Metal Research, and China Machinery Institute .  
  *Source: 工业和信息化部, "2024年国家先进制造业集群名单" (2024).*
- Liaoning added 3 national clusters in Dec 2024 — Shenyang aviation, Shenyang-Dalian machine tools, Dalian-Panjin green petrochemical — raising the province to 4 .  
  *Source: 工业和信息化部, "2024年国家先进制造业集群名单" (2024).*
- Northeast Food Sci-Tech Park: RMB 10.5 billion investment, 3.24 km² (Sujiatun) — a food-industry diversification track .  
  *Source: 工业和信息化部, "2024年国家先进制造业集群名单" (2024).*
