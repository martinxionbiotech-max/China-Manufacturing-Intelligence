## Verified facts

- 2021 张江集成电路产业销售收入 1702.56 亿元, 占上海全市比重超六成; 张江集聚产业链上下游企业约 500 家.  
  *Source: [cls-shanghai-ic](../clusters/cls-shanghai-ic.md)*
- 特斯拉上海超级工厂总投资 500 亿元, 2021 年入库工业产值 1319 亿元.  
  *Source: [cls-shanghai-nev](../clusters/cls-shanghai-nev.md)*
- 张江累计获批一类新药超 20 个, 占全国近 1/5; 在研创新品种超 800 个.  
  *Source: [cls-zhangjiang-biomed](../clusters/cls-zhangjiang-biomed.md)*
- 5 national advanced-manufacturing clusters during the "14th Five-Year" plan (IC, Zhangjiang biopharma, NEVs as first-batch, plus two added in 2024) .  
  *Source: "国家级集群拔节，新兴支柱产业蓄势" (2025).*
- Biopharma: 4,130 resident firms (830 high-tech, 592 specialized), and 18 of the global top-20 pharma + 18 of the top-20 medical-device MNCs each have China HQ or R&D here .  
  *Source: "国家级集群拔节，新兴支柱产业蓄势" (2025).*
- Port: 51.506M TEU throughput (2024), #1 world for 15 straight years; ~350 international routes to 700+ ports in 200+ countries .  
  *Source: "国家级集群拔节，新兴支柱产业蓄势" (2025).*
