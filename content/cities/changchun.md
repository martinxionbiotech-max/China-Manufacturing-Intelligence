# Manufacturing in Changchun

Changchun is China's automobile cradle — where the first Jiefang truck rolled off the line in 1956 —
and now the anchor of FAW's near-site NEV supply chain.

## City role

Changchun is the **domestic-brand NEV pole** of China's auto industry, complementing Shanghai's
foreign-anchored Tesla pole. One is self-reliance, the other is open-ecosystem scale.

## What the city makes

The Audi PPE project locates ~50% of its suppliers within 30 km of the plant, with the local government
zoning dedicated supplier industrial parks — a deliberate near-site (近地化) supply-chain buildout
[cls-changchun-auto]. FAW-Fudi (一汽弗迪), the FAW-BYDB battery JV, plans 45 GWh capacity and RMB 13.5
billion investment to support Hongqi's "All-in-NEV" strategy.

## Data deep-dive

- **1,034 auto-parts enterprises** form the chain's "skeleton": 395 above-scale, 200 with RMB 100M+
  revenue, 31 at RMB 1B+, 3 at RMB 5B+, 1 at RMB 10B+ [1].
- **6 whole-vehicle plants, 7 auto brands**; nearly half of the world's top-100 auto-parts firms have a
  Changchun presence [2].
- **11 national key auto labs** (simulation/control), 6 national enterprise tech centers, 21
  universities with auto majors [1].
- **First-batch national "vehicle-road-cloud integration" ICV pilot city** (July 2024) [2].

## Cross-cluster position

Changchun's battery layer (FAW-Fudi) is a *regional* cell source — a second source to [CATL in
Ningde](/cities/ningde/), which anchors the national battery chain. Its powertrain engineering also
connects to Weifang's diesel-engine base via the adjacent engine supply chain.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Changchun is building a **self-contained NEV ecosystem** — FAW brand + near-site suppliers +
a captive battery JV, reinforced by the densest auto R&D base (11 labs, 21 university programs). The
30-km supplier radius is a structural choice to shorten and localize the chain, mirroring (at smaller
scale) Zhuzhou's 5-km rail radius.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Changchun signals *domestic-brand* cost structures and a growing near-site parts
ecosystem. It is not the export-grade volume pole — that is Shanghai. Match the brand-tier and
export/compliance requirement to the right city.

## Buyer notes

- Domestic-brand NEV and chassis/powertrain → Changchun (FAW/Hongqi).
- Export/compliance-oriented NEV → [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/).
- Regional battery cells → FAW-Fudi (a second source to CATL).
