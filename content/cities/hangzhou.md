# Manufacturing in Hangzhou

Hangzhou is a two-speed manufacturing city: a high-tech digital-security duopoly (Hikvision, Dahua) and
a share of the Hangzhou Bay textile belt. It is also China's #1 city on all three private-enterprise
500 lists.

## City role

Hangzhou is the **surveillance-technology anchor** of China and a co-member of the Hangzhou Bay textile
cluster (with Shaoxing, Ningbo, Jiaxing).

## What the city makes

Hikvision and Dahua, born in Hangzhou's Binjiang core, hold a combined ~65% market share
[cls-hangzhou-digital-security]. Hikvision posted RMB 41.2 billion H1-2024 revenue (+9.68%), pivoting to
AIoT; Dahua posted RMB 14.867 billion (+1.59%).

## Data deep-dive

- **Above-scale industrial value-added: RMB 290.3B (2015) → RMB 440.9B (2024)**, now #5 among
  sub-provincial cities (surpassing Wuhan by RMB 30B) [1].
- **#1 on all three 2025 private-enterprise 500 lists**; manufacturers are 60% of its listees, with 5
  thousand-yuan-level industrial "aircraft carriers" (Rongsheng, Geely, Hengyi, Wanxiang, Fuye) [1].
- **Digital-economy core manufacturing value-added +10.5%** (Jan–Nov 2024) [1].
- **"296X" cluster system:** 2 (AI + visual intelligence) + 9 (robotics, IC, biopharma, network
  comms, smart-connected autos, new-energy equipment, new materials, high-end equipment, textiles) [2].

## Cross-cluster position

On textiles, Hangzhou is a **co-member** of the Hangzhou Bay cluster (RMB 1.0135 trillion, ~1/5 of
national) — but the fabric core (Keqiao) and sock core (Datang) live in [Shaoxing](/cities/shaoxing/),
not Hangzhou. Hangzhou's textile role is garment-adjacent and brand-side, not the raw-fabric floor.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Hangzhou is a **dual-economy city** — a knowledge-economy tech cluster (surveillance/AIoT)
coexisting with a traditional textile-cluster membership. The 5-thousand-yuan-manufacturer count and
#1-across-all-three private-500 lists reveal a *manufacturing* depth beneath its "internet city" image.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Hangzhou is the correct anchor for **video-surveillance hardware and AIoT
platforms** (Hikvision/Dahua — a two-vendor category). For textiles, use Hangzhou for garment/brand
work but anchor raw fabric and dyeing in [Shaoxing](/cities/shaoxing/) (Keqiao).

## Buyer notes

- Surveillance cameras and AIoT platforms → Hangzhou (Hikvision, Dahua, ~65%).
- Evaluate the AIoT/analytics software as the differentiator, not camera hardware.
- Raw fabric/dyeing → [Shaoxing](/cities/shaoxing/) (Keqiao), not Hangzhou.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Hangzhou is a dual-economy city — a knowledge-economy tech cluster coexisting with a traditional textile-cluster membership — and the two halves route differently.*

For video-surveillance hardware and AIoT platforms (Hikvision and Dahua, a two-vendor category), Hangzhou is the anchor. For textiles, use Hangzhou as one node in the broader Hangzhou Bay belt, not the whole chain.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
