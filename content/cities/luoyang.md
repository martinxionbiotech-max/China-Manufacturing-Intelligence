# Manufacturing in Luoyang

Luoyang anchors China's agricultural-machinery industry — YTO Group (中国一拖) leads the cluster that
gave Henan province its first national advanced-manufacturing-cluster designation in 2024. It is also a
39-industry-class old-industrial city now majority high-tech.

## City role

Luoyang is the **single-champion anchor** of China's agricultural machinery — one city, one champion
(YTO), one national cluster.

## What the city makes

The Luoyang cluster entered the national list in 2024 — Henan's "zero-to-one" breakthrough — led by YTO
with 300+ linked firms and RMB 60 billion output [cls-luoyang-agri-machinery]. YTO's large-wheel tractor
line and the 8568 multi-function combine harvester were developed by the National Agricultural Machinery
Equipment Innovation Center.

## Data deep-dive

- **GDP: RMB 581.86 billion (2024), +4.9%; industrial value-added +6.6%** [1].
- **39 industrial classes; 7 national single champions, 56 national "little-giants"** [1].
- **High-tech industry share: >55% of industrial output (first time)** [1].
- **Luoyang Bearing (洛轴): high-end bearings = 70% of its output** [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Luoyang's tractor-and-harvester chain is adjacent to — but distinct from — Weifang's diesel-engine
(powertrain) chain. A buyer sourcing complete machines anchors on Luoyang; a buyer sourcing engines
anchors on [Weifang](/cities/weifang/).

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Agricultural machinery is a **single-champion industry** — one anchor firm, one city. That
concentration means a deep, stable supply chain but less competitive pricing pressure than multi-brand
sectors. The >55% high-tech share shows an old SOE-heavy city successfully re-pivoting toward
advanced manufacturing rather than being stuck in legacy industry.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Luoyang is the correct anchor for **complete tractors and combine harvesters**
(YTO and its 300-firm chain). If the need is engines/powertrain rather than whole machines, route to
Weifang instead — the engine and tractor chains are adjacent but separate. The bearing specialization
(洛轴) is a credible secondary source for high-end bearings.

## Buyer notes

- Complete tractors and combine harvesters → Luoyang (YTO).
- High-end bearings → Luoyang (洛轴).
- Engines/powertrain → [Weifang](/cities/weifang/) (Weichai).
- Distinguish the engine chain from the tractor chain.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, agricultural machinery is a single-champion industry — one anchor firm, one city — and that concentration means a deep, stable supply chain but less competitive tension.*

For complete tractors and combine harvesters (YTO and its 300-firm chain), Luoyang is the correct anchor. If the need is engines or powertrain rather than complete machines, route to Weifang (Weichai) instead.
