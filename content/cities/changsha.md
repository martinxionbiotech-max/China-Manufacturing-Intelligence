# Manufacturing in Changsha

Changsha is the world's third-largest construction-machinery agglomeration — the only city besides one
US metro with five firms in the global top-50. It has held the national #1 construction-machinery
output ranking for over a decade.

## City role

Changsha is the **multi-brand pole** of China's construction-machinery industry, complementing Xuzhou's
single-champion (XCMG) model.

## What the city makes

Five global top-50 firms — Sany, Zoomlion, Sunward, CRCHI, and Sinoboom — call Changsha home
[cls-changsha-construction-machinery]. The cluster produces 12 categories, 100+ sub-categories, and ~500
model specs — 70% of national construction-machinery variety. Output swung with the cycle: RMB 280B
(2021) → ~RMB 200B (2022) → >RMB 250B (2024).

## Data deep-dive

- **>70% of China's construction-machinery product varieties and ~30% of national output value** come
  from Changsha — #1 for 10+ consecutive years [1].
- **Manufacturing value-added = 24.5% of GDP**; above-scale industrial value-added grew 9.6% in 2024, a
  decade high [2].
- **Zoomlion overseas revenue: RMB 23.38 billion (2024), 51.41% of total** [3].
- **National advanced-manufacturing top-100 city ranking: #10 → #5** over the "14th Five-Year" period [2].

## Cross-cluster position

Changsha and [Xuzhou](/cities/xuzhou/) are the industry's two-horse race. Changsha = competition and
variety (local sourcing rate rising 16.3% → 22%); Xuzhou = integration and full lineage. 2024 Changsha
cluster exports hit RMB 29.06 billion (+22.9%), with Sany/Zoomlion/Sunward overseas revenue share above
50%.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Changsha's five-firm structure makes it a **competitive cluster** — the co-location of
rivals drives both variety (70% of national) and export aggressiveness (overseas >50% share for the top
three). Its key enterprises average >6.5% R&D intensity, an unusually high number for heavy machinery.
The 70%-variety / 30%-output split is the tell: Changsha wins on *breadth*, not just scale.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Changsha is the correct floor for **multi-vendor negotiation leverage** and niche
spec coverage. It is not the single-account scale play — that is Xuzhou. Sourcing strategy, not quality,
should decide between the two, and the >50% overseas-revenue share signals export-grade, globally
proven machines.

## Buyer notes

- Multi-vendor sourcing, niche specs, export-ready variety → Changsha.
- Single-vendor scale and full lineage → [Xuzhou](/cities/xuzhou/).
- Factor the industry cycle (output swung RMB 280B→200B→250B) into contract timing.
