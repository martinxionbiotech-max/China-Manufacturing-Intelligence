# Manufacturing in Xi'an

Xi'an is the cradle of China's large-aircraft program — 30+ aircraft types including the Y-20 transport
flew from here. It is also Shaanxi's industrial capital with six pillar industries spanning aviation,
NEVs, and machine tools.

## City role

Xi'an is China's **large-aircraft cradle** and the home of Yanliang (阎良), the country's only complete
aviation-industry-chain location.

## What the city makes

Xi'an gathers ~1/4 of China's aviation research-and-production strength [cls-xian-aviation]. Yanliang
aviation base is China's only complete aviation chain; 2023 aviation output was RMB 35.8 billion, with
Yanliang's 2024 total above RMB 40 billion, and the city targeting RMB 280 billion aerospace scale by
the end of the 14th Five-Year Plan.

## Data deep-dive

- **2024 GDP RMB 1,331.78 billion; 2,115 above-scale industrial firms with RMB 969.28 billion output,
  value-added +6.5%** (6th of 15 sub-provincial cities) [1].
- **Six pillar industries: RMB 767.13 billion output (+4.6%), 79.1% of above-scale industry** [1].
- **Xi'an aviation, Yu-E-Ning coal-chemical, Bao-Han-Tian machine-tool clusters all entered the national
  list**; advanced manufacturing = 43.9% of Shaanxi's above-scale output [1].
- **Geely Xi'an NEV base: 902 welding robots on one body line, leading a 23-firm NEV-parts chain** [2].

## Cross-cluster position

Xi'an's aviation cluster is a **national-strategy sector** — unlike consumer-goods or electronics
clusters, it has no direct competitor city in this dataset and no matching product/industry page yet (the
content layer has no aero-engine or aircraft entity). It connects to Changzhou's aerospace-grade carbon
fiber and Zhuzhou's small aero-engines.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Xi'an is a **defense-and-national-strategy cluster** — its value is strategic (large
aircraft, Y-20) rather than commercial, which is why it is the least "buyer-facing" cluster in this
dataset. Its aviation output (RMB 35.8B) is small next to consumer clusters, but its strategic weight is
disproportionate. The 79.1% six-pillar concentration shows a genuinely diversified industrial base
(aviation + NEVs + machine tools) beneath the aviation headline.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Xi'an is relevant mainly for **aerospace-adjacent supply** (structures, materials,
testing) rather than commodity procurement. Its downstream carbon-fiber link to Changzhou and aero-engine
link to Zhuzhou are the actionable commercial entry points. General commercial buyers will find little
here; aerospace buyers should approach via the strategic-industrial channel.
