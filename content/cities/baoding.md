# Manufacturing in Baoding

Baoding anchors China's power transmission and new-energy equipment — the "China Electric Valley"
(中国电谷), north China's largest high-end power-equipment base. It is also Hebei's manufacturing-quality
leader for 11 straight years.

## City role

Baoding is a **single-champion city**: one national cluster, one anchor function — the
transmission-and-distribution layer of China's grid-equipment supply chain.

## What the city makes

Baoding's cluster gathers 6,300+ enterprises with RMB 166 billion in 2024 output
[cls-baoding-power-equipment]. Its "one core, six zones, three bases" layout follows a
"wind-solar-hydrogen-storage-transmission" (风光氢储输) industrial thread, targeting RMB 250 billion by
2025 with 8 firms above RMB 10 billion.

## Data deep-dive

- **Manufacturing quality-competitiveness index: 90.13 (2024)** — #1 in Hebei for 11 consecutive years
  [1].
- **8 thousand-yuan (RMB 100B) clusters** across the city [2].
- **Two pillars: auto & parts + new energy**, plus 37 county-level characteristic clusters [1].
- **Yingli Group: ~40% global market share in BIPV (building-integrated photovoltaics)**, RMB 1 billion
  R&D in the field [3].

## Cross-cluster position

Baoding occupies the **high-voltage** end of the power-equipment industry. The complementary
**low-voltage** end lives in [Yueqing](/cities/wenzhou/) (Wenzhou electrical, 65% share). Together they
span the full grid-equipment voltage range — a buyer wiring a complete electrical BOM touches both.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Baoding's "wind-solar-hydrogen-storage-transmission" thread means it is positioning itself
as the equipment backbone of China's renewables buildout — transformers and switchgear for wind/solar
farms, not just conventional grid gear. The 11-year quality-index leadership is a *process-maturity*
signal, uncommon for a north-China prefecture city.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Baoding is the correct anchor for utility-scale transmission/distribution
equipment, especially renewables-linked projects. Do not expect it to be the cheapest source for
low-voltage apparatus — that is Yueqing's lane. Route by voltage class, and use the quality-index
leadership as a signal for process consistency rather than a per-supplier guarantee.

## Buyer notes

- Utility/renewables transmission & distribution equipment → Baoding.
- Low-voltage breakers/contactors → [Yueqing](/cities/wenzhou/).
- Watch the RMB 250B/2025 target as a capacity-expansion signal.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Baoding is positioning itself as the equipment backbone of China's renewables buildout, not a diversified manufacturing hub — its "wind-solar-hydrogen-storage-transmission" thread is a single coherent bet.*

That makes it the correct anchor for utility-scale transmission and distribution equipment, especially renewables-linked projects. But do not expect it to be a generalist electronics or consumer-goods play — that is not what this city is.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
