# Manufacturing in Wenzhou

Wenzhou anchors China's low-voltage electrical industry through Yueqing (乐清) — the "China Electrical
Capital" and home to Chint (正泰). It is also the purest expression of Zhejiang's private-economy model:
a city that crossed the RMB 1-trillion GDP line in 2025 on a foundation of self-grown township firms.

## City role

Wenzhou (Yueqing) is the **low-voltage apparatus pole** of China's power-equipment industry, complementing
[Baoding](/cities/baoding/)'s high-voltage transmission pole.

## What the city makes

Yueqing's cluster entered the MIIT advanced-manufacturing list in 2022 and holds 65%+ national share,
covering 200+ series and 25,000+ models [cls-wenzhou-electrical]. Chint Group, founded 1984 by Nan
Cunhui, reached US$22.1 billion in 2024 revenue. Beyond electricals, Wenzhou runs a **"5+5+N" industrial
system** — five traditional strengths (electrical, footwear, apparel, auto parts, pumps/valves) plus
five emerging tracks (digital economy, smart equipment, life-health, new energy, new materials) [1].

## Data deep-dive

- **Industrial output value > RMB 1.5 trillion** (2025); the city entered China's "trillion-yuan GDP
  club" as its 28th member [1][3].
- **R&D intensity: 2.76% of GDP (2024)** — the highest in Zhejiang province [2].
- **Private enterprises: 90.8% of above-scale industrial value-added** — 1.564 million registered
  private firms and individual businesses as of Nov 2025 [3].
- **4,772 high-tech enterprises**; industrial value-added topped RMB 300 billion [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Wenzhou and Baoding are **complementary voltage layers**: Wenzhou owns low-voltage apparatus (breakers,
contactors, relays); Baoding owns transmission/distribution (transformers, switchgear). A full electrical
BOM spans both.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Wenzhou is a **self-grown (草根) cluster** — it emerged from township enterprises and
traders, not a state anchor. That origin explains its extreme category breadth (25,000+ models) and its
"one champion (Chint) + long tail" structure. The 90.8% private share and 2.76% R&D intensity show a
private-economy city that is *also* climbing the innovation ladder — not just a cost floor.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Wenzhou is the correct anchor for **low-voltage apparatus**, with the caveat that
certification tiers vary widely across the long tail. Chint-tier suppliers carry full international
certs; the tail does not. Verify per-model CCC/CE/UL. The "5+5+N" diversification is a resilience signal
— but electricals remain the single deepest specialty.

## Buyer notes

- Low-voltage breakers/contactors/relays → Yueqing (65% share).
- Chint-tier for export-grade certified apparatus.
- High-voltage transmission equipment → [Baoding](/cities/baoding/).
