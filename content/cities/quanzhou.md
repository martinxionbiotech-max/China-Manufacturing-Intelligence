# Manufacturing in Quanzhou

Quanzhou anchors China's sports-footwear industry — Jinjiang's Chendai town packs more sneaker capacity
into a sub-40 km² area than anywhere on earth.

## City role

Quanzhou is the **sports-goods capital**, with Jinjiang (a county-level city) as the manufacturing core
and Quanzhou (the prefecture) carrying the national-cluster designation.

## What the city makes

Chendai town hosts 3,000+ shoe firms producing 1 billion+ sports shoes a year [cls-jinjiang-footwear].
Quanzhou's 2024 textile-and-footwear output exceeded RMB 700 billion, with sports shoes at 40% of
national and 20% of global production [cls-quanzhou-sports]. Anta's 2022 revenue was RMB 53.651 billion,
#1 in China's sportswear market.

## Data deep-dive

- **70,000+ industrial enterprises, 7,000+ above-scale** — the "Jinjiang experience" (晋江经验) of
  private-economy industrial clusters [1].
- **5 clusters each above RMB 300 billion output** (textile & apparel, footwear, etc.) [1].
- **Machinery & equipment > RMB 260 billion**; its "four wheels, one belt" (四轮一带) products = 50%+ of
  national exports [2].
- **355 city-level leading enterprises contribute ~20% of GDP** [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Quanzhou is a **city-and-metro pair** — Jinjiang (manufacturing) + Quanzhou (national designation). The
supply chain is hyper-local: R&D, design, and component matching are findable within a 50 km radius. In
2024 Anta led a "sports-goods innovation consortium" to set global standards.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Quanzhou's edge is **supply-chain density** — a 50-km self-contained radius covering R&D to
component matching, which lets Anta, Xtep, and 361° all compete from the same geography. The "Jinjiang
experience" is a *model* (private-economy + market-driven cluster), not a sector: it now spans footwear,
machinery, and building materials. It is mid-upgrade: still volume (1B+ pairs), but the anchors now set
global standards.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Quanzhou serves both **commodity OEM** (volume, price) and **branded ODM**
(Anta/Xtep tier) — but those are different supplier tiers. Match deliberately: Chendai's 3,000-firm base
for volume, the anchor brands for ODM/global-standard work.

## Buyer notes

- Commodity-volume OEM sneakers → Chendai's 3,000-firm base.
- Branded/ODM sportswear → Anta, Xtep, 361° tier.
- Match the supplier tier to the requirement (volume vs. brand/standard).

## The Author's Take

So it serves both commodity OEM (volume, price) and branded ODM (the Anta and Xtep tier) — but those are different supplier tiers. Match deliberately, and do not treat them as one market.
