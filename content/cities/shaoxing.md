# Manufacturing in Shaoxing

Shaoxing is China's textile capital — home to the world's largest fabric-distribution hub (Keqiao, 中国
轻纺城) and the sock town of Datang (70% of China's socks).

## City role

Shaoxing is the **fabric-and-dyeing core** of China's textile system, plus the sock specialty node
(Zhuji/Datang). It is the discovery floor for fabric buyers worldwide.

## What the city makes

Keqiao is the world's largest textile distribution center — ~1/4 of global fabrics traded here, sold to
192–206 countries — with 2025 market turnover RMB 441.39 billion (+10.07%) [cls-keqiao-textile]. It
holds ~40% of China's dyeing-and-printing capacity.

Datang's sock industry produces 25 billion+ pairs a year, RMB 70 billion+ output, ~70% of national and
1/3 of global [cls-zhuji-socks], with 194 digital workshops and 20,000+ smart sock machines.

## Data deep-dive

- **2024 modern-textile cluster output: RMB 259.89 billion (+9.5%)** — plus a fluorine fine-chemical
  cluster, both national-level (ranked #2 in Zhejiang by cluster count) [1].
- **1,929 above-scale textile firms; 17 with RMB 1B+ output; 219,000 workers** [1].
- **Big-textile (textile + chemical-fiber + garment) = ~50% of Shaoxing manufacturing value-added and
  ~58% of its manufacturing employment** [2].

## Cross-cluster position

Shaoxing is the **fabric layer** of textiles — upstream of Nantong's home textiles (bedding) and Hangzhou
Bay's garments. The Hangzhou Bay cluster (RMB 1.0135 trillion) formalizes this four-city chain. Its
fluorine fine-chemical cluster is a separate, co-equal specialty (Shangyu).

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Shaoxing is a **market-plus-factory textile city** — Keqiao's market (RMB 441B turnover) is
the discovery layer, with 8,000+ mills and ~40% of national dyeing capacity behind it. Its dyeing
consolidation signals an environmental-driven industry rationalization. The "big-textile ≈ half the
city's manufacturing" figure makes it a genuine single-industry city — concentration risk as much as
strength.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Shaoxing is the correct anchor for **raw and printed fabric plus dyeing**, and
**socks** (Datang). It is the fabric discovery floor (like Lecong is for furniture). For bedding, go to
Nantong; for garments, Hangzhou Bay.

## Buyer notes

- Raw/printed fabric and dyeing → Keqiao (China Textile City).
- Socks → Datang (70% national).
- Bedding → [Nantong](/cities/nantong/); garments → [Hangzhou](/cities/hangzhou/).

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Shaoxing is a market-plus-factory textile city — Keqiao's market is the discovery layer, with 8,000-plus mills and roughly 40% of national dyeing capacity behind it.*

For raw and printed fabric plus dyeing, Shaoxing is the correct anchor, and Datang adds the sock monopoly (70% of China's socks). "Textile capital" here means the fabric layer, not garments or home textiles.
