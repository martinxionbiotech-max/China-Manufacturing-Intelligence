# Manufacturing in Weifang

Weifang anchors China's power-equipment (diesel engine and powertrain) industry, led by Weichai (潍柴).
It is a "manufacturing city" in the fullest sense — 37 of China's 41 industrial classes, with
above-scale industrial output and revenue both past RMB 1.2 trillion.

## City role

Weifang is the **powertrain anchor** — the engine layer that sits beneath agricultural machinery
(Luoyang's YTO) and heavy equipment. It is a "chain-master" (链长制) city.

## What the city makes

The 2021 Weifang power-equipment cluster output was RMB 413.5 billion — about one-third of national —
with Weichai leading and 8 national single-champion enterprises [cls-weifang-power-equipment]. The city's
2024 GDP was RMB 820.32 billion (+5.9%). In August 2024 the city's party secretary took the
power-equipment "chain-master" role, launching the 12-chain system.

## Data deep-dive

- **Above-scale industrial output and revenue: both > RMB 1.2 trillion** — manufacturing ≈ 1/10 of
  Shandong, 1% of China [1].
- **GDP: RMB 820.32 billion (2024), +5.9%** [2].
- **"9+3+N" system: 12 key chains**; the Weifang powertrain cluster ≈ 1/3 of national [1].
- **Wei-Lin-Ri smart-agri-machinery cluster: RMB 165 billion (2023), 1/4 of national agri-machinery**
  [1].
- **15 firms above RMB 10 billion; 27 national single champions** [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Weifang's engines are the **upstream powertrain** of Luoyang's tractors and the broader heavy-equipment
sector. It is the "engine chain" adjacent to — but distinct from — Luoyang's "tractor chain."

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Weifang is a **champion-anchored powertrain city** — Weichai plus 27 national single
champions, coordinated by a "chain-master" policy system. Its 37-of-41 industry-class breadth is rare for
a non-megacity, and the agri-machinery cluster (1/4 of national) shows the engine chain feeding a
downstream machine chain.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Weifang is the correct anchor for **diesel engines and powertrain systems**
(Weichai). If the need is complete tractors/machines, route to Luoyang (YTO); if engines/powertrain,
route here. The engine and machine chains are adjacent but separate — and Weifang's breadth means it is
also a credible source for the wider "9+3+N" chain set.

## Buyer notes

- Complete tractors/harvesters → [Luoyang](/cities/luoyang/) (YTO).
