# Manufacturing in Shantou

Shantou anchors China's toy industry through Chenghai (澄海) — a district holding a third of global
plastic-toy capacity. It is also a multi-track city: a "three-new, two-special, one-big" (三新两特一大)
cluster system targeting RMB 700 billion, spanning textiles, new materials, offshore wind, and a unique
international-submarine-cable position.

## City role

Shantou is the **toy capital**, with Chenghai as the manufacturing core. The district's specialization is
total: 1 in 3 residents works in toys.

## What the city makes

Chenghai has 50,000+ toy enterprises and 200,000+ toy workers, holding 33% of global plastic-toy
capacity and leading nationally in toy brands, IP licenses, and patents [cls-chenghai-toys]. It updates
300,000 SKUs a year — over 1,000 new products daily — shipping to ~140 countries.

## Data deep-dive

- **Industrial investment +19% (2024)** — the city's fastest manufacturing investment growth [1].
- **Textiles: Chaonan district RMB 32.89 billion output (2024)** [2].
- **New materials: 302 above-scale firms, RMB 48.83 billion output (2024)**, 17.4% of city industrial
  value [3].
- **Offshore wind equipment park: RMB 4.08 billion investment, RMB 14.2 billion annual output at full
  capacity** [3].
- **International bandwidth: 6 submarine cables, >50% of national export bandwidth** — the only city
  with both an international cable landing station and a regional communications gateway [3].

## Cross-cluster position

Shantou/Chenghai is the **plastic-toy anchor**; Quanzhou anchors sports products (footwear); Dongguan
and Yiwu are adjacent distribution and small-commodity nodes.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Chenghai is a **speed-and-IP cluster** — its edge is iteration velocity (1,000 new SKUs/day)
plus an IP-licensing moat, not just cheap labor. The city's submarine-cable position (50%+ national
bandwidth) is an underappreciated digital-infrastructure asset that supports cross-border e-commerce
and digital-toy IP distribution.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Shantou is the correct anchor for **plastic toys, especially licensed/IP toys**,
where its patent and IP-license leadership matters. The 300,000-SKU/year churn rewards fast-moving
buyers; long-lead, safety-certified (EN71/ASTM) toys should pair Chenghai's manufacturing with explicit
certification management. The offshore-wind and materials tracks are secondary sourcing options, not the
headline.

## Buyer notes

- Plastic toys, especially licensed/IP toys → Chenghai (33% global capacity).
- Manage EN71/ASTM/CPSC certification explicitly — speed comes with quality dispersion.
- Offshore-wind equipment (RMB 14.2B full-capacity park) → Shantou, as a secondary track.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Chenghai is a speed-and-IP cluster — its edge is iteration velocity (1,000 new SKUs a day) plus an IP-licensing moat, not just cheap labor.*

For plastic toys, especially licensed and IP toys, Shantou is the correct anchor, where its patent and IP-license leadership matters. The 300,000-SKU breadth is a discovery asset, not a commodity signal.
