# Manufacturing in Yancheng

Yancheng is Jiangsu's clean-energy manufacturing hub — the only city in this dataset hosting *both* a
solar-PV and an offshore-wind national cluster.

## City role

Yancheng is the **dual clean-energy anchor** — offshore wind (39% of national) and solar PV (Trina
Solar's base) in one coastal city.

## What the city makes

- **Wind**: Yancheng's offshore-wind installed capacity was 3.52 GW (2020) — 61% of Jiangsu, 39% of
  national, ~10% of global [cls-yancheng-wind-power]. Goldwind's Dafeng test farm runs 98 offshore
  turbines up to 13.6 MW class.
- **Solar**: 2024 new-energy invoiced sales RMB 138.38 billion, with 106 above-scale crystalline-silicon
  PV chain firms; the PV cluster was named a national advanced cluster [cls-yancheng-pv].

## Data deep-dive

- **2024 GDP RMB 777.92 billion (+5.5%); industrial economy crossed the RMB 1-trillion mark in 2023**
  [1].
- **"5+2" strategic-emerging industries + 23 key industrial chains** (new-energy, PV, wind, etc.) [2].
- **Grain output >14 billion jin for 10 straight years** — a coastal-manufacturing city that is also a
  major food producer [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Yancheng is a **resource-plus-manufacturing city** — its offshore-wind *resource* (39% of national
installed) is inseparable from its *manufacturing* (Goldwind's test farm, Trina's PV base). Few cities
couple the resource and the factory this tightly.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Yancheng's clean-energy pairing (wind + PV) is unusual — most cities anchor one energy
cluster. Its offshore-wind test-farm infrastructure (98 turbines, 13.6 MW class) provides real-world
validation that manufacturing-only clusters lack. The RMB 1-trillion industrial base signals Yancheng is
now a broad manufacturing city, not just a clean-energy niche.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Yancheng is the correct anchor for **offshore-wind turbines/systems** and
**PV modules** (Trina). The ~10% global wind figure is 2020-vintage (likely understated); treat it as a
floor. The dual-cluster structure also lets a buyer source wind + solar from one geography.
