# Manufacturing in Shenzhen

Shenzhen is China's electronics capital — RMB 2.48 trillion in output, ~1/6 of the national total — and
the hardware anchor of the Shenzhen-Guangzhou medical-device cluster.

## City role

Shenzhen is the **component-and-design layer** of the Pearl River Delta, sitting upstream of Dongguan's
terminal assembly. It also anchors medical-device hardware via Mindray.

## What the city makes

- **Electronics**: 4,100+ above-scale electronics manufacturers, 5 firms >RMB 100B; ~1 in 7 smartphones
  and ~1/2 of global communication base stations are "made in Shenzhen"; Huaqiangbei is the world's
  largest electronics trading hub [cls-shenzhen-electronics]. 2024 total trade RMB 4.5 trillion — 32
  consecutive years #1 among mainland cities.
- **Medical devices**: Mindray's monitors #1 for 15 years, plus the ~20 km² International Medical Device
  City [cls-sg-medical-devices].

## Data deep-dive

- **2024 above-scale industrial output: RMB 5.4 trillion; industrial value-added +9.7% — national
  "double #1" for 3 straight years** [1].
- **Strategic-emerging industries >40% of GDP; 5 national advanced-manufacturing clusters** [1].
- **2024 robot output: 133,900 industrial + 7.1129 million service robots**; a "robot valley" of 100+
  chain firms (UBTech, Dobot) [2].
- **1,800+ medical-device firms + dozens of top research institutions** across the full R&D→clinical→
  incubation→industrial chain [2].

## Cross-cluster position

Shenzhen-Dongguan are complementary layers (components vs. assembly). Shenzhen-Guangzhou are the
device-hardware plus clinical-ecosystem pair. Shenzhen is also the hardware-integration node for
Hefei's voice AI and Shenyang's robots.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Shenzhen is a **market-grown, component-dense cluster** — the polar opposite of the
policy-scaled clusters (Chengdu-Chongqing) and the single-champion clusters (Ningde). Its RMB 5.4
trillion output, 40%-plus strategic-emerging share, and Huaqiangbei trading hub make it the ecosystem's
discovery-and-integration layer, and its robot output (133,900 industrial units) makes it China's de
facto robot-volume pole, not just an electronics city.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Shenzhen is the correct anchor for **components, PCBs, design, and device
hardware** — and the first stop for any electronics sourcing journey. For finished terminal assembly, go
to Dongguan; for cost-optimized inland assembly, Chengdu-Chongqing.

## Buyer notes

- PCBs, components, design, distribution → Shenzhen (Huaqiangbei).
- Device hardware (monitors, ultrasound, IVD) → Shenzhen (Mindray).
- Terminal assembly → [Dongguan](/cities/dongguan/).
