# Manufacturing in Chongqing

Chongqing is the western twin of China's inland electronics cluster, co-anchoring the Chengdu-Chongqing
electronics zone with [Chengdu](/cities/chengdu/). It is also the country's #2 auto producer and the
world's #1 laptop maker.

## City role

Chongqing is the **inland terminal-and-assembly half** of the twin-city electronics cluster. Where
Chengdu leads the zone's RMB 1.3 trillion electronics scale, Chongqing carries the laptop/terminal
assembly and the twin-city zone's logistics spine (a Yangtze + rail hub).

## What the city makes

The Chengdu-Chongqing zone co-builds four "trillion-yuan" (万亿级) clusters, with electronics output and
auto production together above 1/10 of national [cls-cy-electronics]. The zone's 2024 industrial added
value reached RMB 2.42 trillion (+6.3%).

## Data deep-dive

- **Autos: 2.54 million units (2024), #2 nationally; NEVs 953,000 (+90.5%)** [1].
- **Laptops: 66.82 million units (2024) — #1 globally**; IC output +110%, LCD panels +22.1% [1].
- **Power semiconductors: national top-3 capacity** [1].
- **Seres (赛力斯): >420,000 NEVs sold (+182.2%)**, becoming the city's 3rd RMB 100-billion industrial
  firm [2].
- **Software revenue RMB 349.5 billion (+10.9%)** [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Chongqing is the **cost-and-logistics play** in electronics — a Yangtze port plus the China-Europe rail
(Yuxin'ou) outbound corridor, which gives inland manufacturing an export route that bypasses coastal
ports. This is structurally different from Shenzhen/Dongguan's sea-port model.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* The Chengdu-Chongqing cluster is a **policy-scaled twin-city cluster** — its "trillion-yuan"
scale comes from combining two megacities under one economic-zone policy, not from a single organic
market density. Chongqing's rail export corridor and its #2-auto / #1-laptop duality are the structural
differentiators: it is a *terminal-and-powertrain* city, not a component city.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Chongqing is the right anchor for **inland, cost-optimized electronics assembly
with a rail export route to Europe/Central Asia**, plus **NEV powertrain** (Seres tier). It is not the
component-density play — that is Shenzhen. Treat it as the inland tier of the electronics-and-auto
sourcing ladder.

## Buyer notes

- Inland electronics assembly with rail export (Europe/Central Asia) → Chongqing.
- NEVs / powertrain (Seres, #2 national autos) → Chongqing.
- Twin-city scale (electronics + auto) → coordinate with [Chengdu](/cities/chengdu/).
- Deepest components → [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/).
