# Manufacturing in Jinhua

Jinhua anchors two of China's most distinctive manufacturing-and-market clusters: Yongkang hardware and
Yiwu small commodities. It is also a 50-year-old power-tool hub — China's first angle grinder was made
here in 1974.

## City role

Jinhua is a **market-plus-factory prefecture** — Yongkang (hardware manufacturing) and Yiwu (the world's
largest small-commodities market) sit under one prefecture, a "make it in Yongkang, sell it in Yiwu"
pairing.

## What the city makes

Yongkang: 7 of every 10 Chinese doors, 5 of every 10 global power tools, 6 of every 10 thermos cups;
RMB 50B+ output, 90% of Yongkang's industrial output [cls-yongkang-hardware].

Yiwu: the world's largest small-commodities wholesale market, 7.5万+ booths, 2024 transaction value RMB
279.8 billion (+20.02%), and the Yixin'ou China-Europe Railway Express (6,700+ trains over 10 years,
reaching 50+ countries) [cls-yiwu-small-commodities].

## Data deep-dive

- **Power tools: 448 above-scale firms, RMB 47.76 billion output (2023, +5.9%)** — 200+ whole-machine
  products, 2,000+ parts, 70%+ local parts rate [1].
- **Ten key industrial chains with 62 "chain-master" firms** (NEVs, smart PV, robotics, etc.) [2].
- **Above-scale industrial value RMB 509.27 billion**, with core-optoelectronics and NEV parts = 40.8%
  [3].

## Cross-cluster position

Yongkang and Yiwu are **complements**: Yongkang makes (hardware, tools, doors), Yiwu sells (210万+
product types to the world). The Yiwu "market procurement" (1039) trade mode and the Yixin'ou rail link
give inland Zhejiang a direct export corridor to Europe and Central Asia.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Jinhua is a **market-platform + manufacturing cluster pair** — Yiwu is the discovery and
distribution layer (the "world supermarket"), Yongkang the hardware manufacturing layer. Its power-tool
industry's 50-year, 70%-local-parts depth is the industrial backbone behind the market surface. This is
the market-plus-factory model (like Foshan/Lecong) at prefecture scale.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Jinhua is best used as a **combined sourcing surface**: discover categories and
order small-lot mixed containers in Yiwu, then trace hardware/power-tool categories back to Yongkang
makers. The Yixin'ou rail is a genuine inland-export advantage for Europe/Central Asia buyers.

## Buyer notes

- Mixed-category small-commodity sourcing → Yiwu (world's largest market).
- Power tools, doors, hardware → Yongkang.
- Use the Yixin'ou rail for Europe/Central Asia exports.
