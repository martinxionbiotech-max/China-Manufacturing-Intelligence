# Manufacturing in Qingdao

Qingdao is a two-cluster port city: smart home appliances (Haier, Hisense) and the total-assembly core
of the Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai shipbuilding cluster.

## City role

Qingdao is the **brand-scale appliance city** and the **assembly anchor** of the three-city shipbuilding
cluster. It holds 4 of Shandong's 6 national advanced-manufacturing clusters.

## What the city makes

- **Appliances**: Haier became Qingdao's first RMB 400B-revenue company in 2024; Haier and Hisense placed
  #2 and #4 among home-appliance firms on China's Top-500 list [cls-qingdao-appliances].
- **Shipbuilding**: the cluster entered the national list in December 2024, with Beihai Shipbuilding
  (北海造船) as the total-assembly anchor; ~140 above-scale firms and 40%+ local supporting rate
  [cls-qingdao-shipbuilding].

## Data deep-dive

- **4 of Shandong's 6 national advanced-manufacturing clusters are in Qingdao** [1].
- **Ship & marine-equipment cluster: 200+ above-scale total-assembly/parts firms (Beihai, CNOOC Qingdao,
  CIMC Raffles) + 600+ upstream/downstream enterprises** across R&D, materials, building, parts, service
  [1].
- **Smart-appliance cluster target: RMB 300 billion output by 2025** [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Qingdao's appliance cluster is the **brand-scale pole** of home appliances (vs. Shunde's manufacturing
depth and Cixi's niche density). Its shipbuilding cluster divides roles with Yantai (marine engineering)
and Weihai (co-building).

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Qingdao is a **dual-anchor city** — brand appliances (Haier) and heavy marine assembly
(Beihai) — two very different industrial logics in one port. The shipbuilding cluster's three-city
division (assembly/marine-engineering/co-building) is a role-split, not a competition. The "4 of 6
national clusters" concentration makes Qingdao Shandong's de facto advanced-manufacturing capital.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Qingdao is the correct anchor for **brand/OEM-scale white goods** (Haier, Hisense)
and **total assembly / large vessels** (Beihai). For marine-engineering platforms, route to Yantai; for
co-building fabrication, Weihai.

## Buyer notes

- Brand/OEM-scale white goods → Qingdao (Haier, Hisense).
- Total assembly / large vessels → Qingdao (Beihai).
- Marine engineering → [Yantai](/cities/yantai/); co-building → [Weihai](/cities/weihai/).

## The Author's Take

For brand and OEM-scale white goods (Haier, Hisense) and total assembly of large vessels (Beihai), Qingdao is the anchor. For marine-engineering platforms, route to Yantai; for co-building fabrication, to Weihai.
