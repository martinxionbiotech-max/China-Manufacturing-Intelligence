# Manufacturing in Jiaxing

Jiaxing is a co-member of the Hangzhou Bay textile cluster — the RMB 1.0135-trillion modern
textile-and-garment zone that is China's largest. It is also Zhejiang's #3 industrial-output city and a
chemical-new-materials base.

## City role

Jiaxing is the **northern member** of the four-city Hangzhou Bay textile cluster (Shaoxing, Hangzhou,
Ningbo, Jiaxing). It is a supporting manufacturing node, not the fabric or sock core.

## What the city makes

The Hangzhou Bay cluster's 2023 output was RMB 1.0135 trillion — nearly 1/5 of national, #1 in China —
and entered the national advanced-manufacturing list at end-2024 [cls-hangzhou-bay-textile]. Jiaxing
contributes garment and textile manufacturing to this shared cluster.

## Data deep-dive

- **Above-scale industrial output value: RMB 1.51 trillion (2024), #3 in Zhejiang** [1].
- **Industrial value-added RMB 338.8 billion (+6.5%)**; strategic-emerging industries = 44.4% of
  above-scale value, #2 in the province [1].
- **Chemical new-materials park: RMB 56.54B (2020) → RMB 87B (2024), +13.5%/yr** — 21 Fortune-500 or
  industry leaders (Shell, BASF) resident [2].
- **Hydrogen: 42 firms, full-chain layout** [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Jiaxing's role is **cluster membership, not cluster leadership** — the fabric core is
[Shaoxing](/cities/shaoxing/) (Keqiao), the sock core is [Zhuji/Datang](/cities/shaoxing/), and Hangzhou
adds brand/garment work. Jiaxing supplies manufacturing capacity inside the shared designation, while its
chemical-new-materials park is a separate, co-equal pillar.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Jiaxing illustrates how the Hangzhou Bay cluster is a **four-city shared designation** —
the "national cluster" boundary is larger than any single city's role. Its real industrial weight is
dual: textile co-membership plus a chemical-materials park (RMB 87B) that is one of the densest in
eastern China.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Jiaxing is best used as **one node in a Hangzhou Bay sourcing strategy** — source
fabric in Shaoxing, brand/garment in Hangzhou, and use Jiaxing/Ningbo for additional garment/textile
manufacturing capacity. For chemical new-materials, it is a credible anchor (Shell/BASF co-location).
Don't expect Jiaxing to be the fabric discovery floor.

## Buyer notes

- Garment/textile manufacturing capacity → Jiaxing (as part of Hangzhou Bay).
- Chemical new-materials → Jiaxing park (Shell/BASF tier).
- Raw fabric/dyeing → [Shaoxing](/cities/shaoxing/) (Keqiao).
- Treat the four cities as one sourcing geography.
