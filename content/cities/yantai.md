# Manufacturing in Yantai

Yantai anchors marine engineering in the Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai shipbuilding cluster, home to CIMC
Raffles (中集来福士). It is also a top-tier industrial city in its own right — above-scale industrial
output crossed RMB 1 trillion, spanning 37 of China's 41 industrial classes.

## City role

Yantai is the **marine-engineering leg** of the three-city shipbuilding cluster. Qingdao carries total
assembly (Beihai), Yantai carries offshore platforms and marine engineering (CIMC Raffles), Weihai
carries co-building.

## What the city makes

The shipbuilding cluster entered the national advanced-manufacturing list in December 2024, jointly built
by the three cities, with ~140 above-scale assembly/supporting firms, 40%+ local supporting rate for main
ship types, and 600+ upstream/downstream enterprises [cls-qingdao-shipbuilding]. CIMC Raffles is the
marine-engineering anchor.

## Data deep-dive

- **Above-scale industrial output value: RMB 1.110 trillion (2024)**, +42.7% vs 2020 [1].
- **6 thousand-yuan-level (RMB 100B) clusters; 13 firms above RMB 10 billion** [1].
- **Wanhua Chemical (万华): ~RMB 200 billion annual output** — the petrochemical anchor [2].
- **Petrochemical & new-materials chain: 248 → 328 above-scale firms (2021→2024), output RMB 95.3B →
  185.4B (+19.1%/yr)** [2].

## Cross-cluster position

Yantai's role is **role-division within the cluster** — it owns the offshore/marine-engineering layer,
distinct from Qingdao's assembly and Weihai's fabrication. A buyer sourcing offshore platforms routes
here; its petrochemical chain (Wanhua) is a separate, co-equal industrial pillar.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Yantai is a **dual-anchor city** — marine engineering (CIMC Raffles) plus petrochemicals
(Wanhua, RMB 200B). The RMB 1.11-trillion industrial base means marine engineering sits atop a deep,
diversified industrial stack rather than a single-purpose port town. The petrochemical chain's 19.1%/yr
growth is the city's real volume driver.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Yantai is the correct anchor for **offshore and marine-engineering platforms**
(CIMC Raffles), with Wanhua's petrochemical chain as a major secondary source. Total assembly routes to
Qingdao; supporting fabrication to Weihai. Verify component sourcing — the 40% local supporting rate is
solid but not Zhuzhou-level (80%+).
