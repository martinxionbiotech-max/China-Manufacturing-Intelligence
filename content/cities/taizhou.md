# Manufacturing in Taizhou

Taizhou is a two-cluster industrial city: East-Zhejiang machine tools (the "industrial mother machines")
and the pump-and-valve cluster (Wenling pumps, Yuhuan valves).

## City role

Taizhou anchors China's **mother-machine base** (with Ningbo) and the **pump-valve category-density**
core. It is the upstream enabler of much of the Delta's manufacturing.

## What the city makes

- **Machine tools**: East Zhejiang entered the national list in 2024; Taizhou has ~2,000 machine-tool
  firms with RMB 70B+ above-scale revenue; the broader cluster did ~RMB 280 billion (1/5 of national)
  [cls-ezd-machine-tools].
- **Pumps**: Wenling's small water pumps are 70%+ of national, with 2022 cluster output RMB 65B+
  (16.3% CAGR); Yuhuan's precision valves are a provincial "rising-star" cluster [cls-taizhou-pumps].

## Data deep-dive

- **2,000+ machine-tool equipment firms** (Heidman, Eastern CNC, Beiping); **economical CNC machine
  tools = 1/3 of national output** [1].
- **299 "hidden-champion" products with #1 domestic/global share; 55 listed firms** [2].
- **Emerging new-energy track:** Qingtao solid-state lithium-battery PACK plant (Huangyan) and a broader
  wind/PV/nuclear-storage play [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Taizhou's machine tools are the **upstream enabler** of every other cluster — they make the machines that
make everything else. Its pumps/valves pair with Wenzhou's low-voltage electrical (Chint) as the
power-and-fluids layer.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Taizhou is a **category-density city** — its edge is breadth (machine tools + pumps +
valves + motors) in one geography, not a single champion. The "299 hidden champions" figure is the
clearest signal: Taizhou wins through many small #1 niches, not one anchor. The machine-tool cluster's
institutional anchor (a provincial innovation center) signals a high-end-CNC upgrade path.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Taizhou is the correct anchor for **mid-to-high-end CNC machine tools** and
**pumps/valves** (Wenling for commodity pumps, Yuhuan for precision valves). Its mother-machine role
makes it a leverage point — sourcing production lines here can be coordinated with downstream
category machinery (e.g., Foshan's ceramic presses).

## Buyer notes

- CNC and general machine tools → Taizhou (Wenling).
- Commodity water pumps → Wenling; precision valves → Yuhuan.
- Coordinate machine tools + category machinery for integrated capex.

## The Author's Take
