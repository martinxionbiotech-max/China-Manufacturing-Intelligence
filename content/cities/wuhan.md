# Manufacturing in Wuhan

Wuhan anchors China's optoelectronics industry through the Optics Valley (光谷) — the Donghu High-tech
Zone that pulled China's first optical fiber in 1976.

## City role

Wuhan is the **fiber-and-optics capital** — home to the world's largest optical-fiber/cable,
optoelectronic-device, optical-communication R&D, and laser-equipment bases.

## What the city makes

Optics Valley spans 518 km² with ~2 million residents (average age ~31) and is one of China's 10
"world-class high-tech parks" [cls-wuhan-optics-valley]. It is one of China's three densest intelligence
zones: 42 universities, 56 research institutes, 8,000+ PhDs, 40,000+ master's-and-above talent. Its
foreign trade is one-third of Hubei and 60% of Wuhan.

## Data deep-dive

- **2024 optoelectronics industry revenue +10%+; 16,000+ optoelectronic firms** across the full
  "光芯屏端网" chain (fiber, chips, displays, terminals, networks) [1].
- **Anchors: China Information & Communication Tech (中信科), HG Tech (华工科技), YOFC (长飞光纤), Hisilicon
  (海思), Accelink (光迅科技)** [1].
- **S&T matrix: 1 national lab + 8 large scientific facilities + 10 Hubei labs + 163 national platforms
  + 525 new-type R&D institutes** [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Wuhan is the fiber/optics anchor; [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/) is the complementary display-and-
electronics integration node. Q1 2026 Hubei industrial profit grew 87.5% YoY (#1 nationally), with
computer/communications/electronics contributing more than half.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Optics Valley is an **institution-anchored cluster** — its engine is the 1976 fiber pull
plus a 42-university talent base and the 1+8+10+163+525 S&T matrix, not cheap labor or ports. That makes
it the intellectual capital of China's fiber-optic industry, structurally distinct from labor-density
clusters.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Wuhan is the correct anchor for **fiber, optical components, and laser
equipment**. Pair Wuhan (fiber/optics) with Shenzhen (display integration) for optoelectronic-system
sourcing, and leverage the 42-university talent base for R&D-heavy projects.
