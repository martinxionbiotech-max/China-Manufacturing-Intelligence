# Manufacturing in Wuxi

Wuxi anchors China's only national Internet-of-Things (IoT) cluster, plus the northern edge of the
Suzhou-Wuxi-Nantong home-textile belt. At RMB 1.63 trillion GDP, it is a "465" modern-cluster city where
materials, equipment, and IoT stack on top of a deep industrial base.

## City role

Wuxi is the **IoT anchor** of China — the country's only national IoT cluster — and a home-textile-belt
member.

## What the city makes

Wuxi's IoT cluster entered the first batch of national advanced-manufacturing clusters in 2021, the only
IoT one nationally. Its 585 above-scale IoT firms posted RMB 318.4 billion revenue, covering
information-sensing, transmission, computing-storage, and application-processing [cls-wuxi-iot]. Its
"one-sense, two-networks" (smart sensors, vehicle-networking, industrial-internet) signature is
distinct.

## Data deep-dive

- **GDP: RMB 1.63 trillion (2024)** [1].
- **7 clusters above RMB 200 billion revenue; new-materials and high-end equipment above RMB 300
  billion** under the "465" system [1].
- **463 national "little-giant" specialized SMEs** — 2nd in Jiangsu, 8th nationally [2].
- **New national clusters (2024):** the Yangtze Delta (incl. Jiangxi) large-aircraft cluster and the
  Sunan special-steel-materials cluster [1].

## Cross-cluster position

On textiles, Wuxi is the **northern edge** of the SWT home-textile belt (core: [Nantong](/cities/nantong/)).
Its IoT cluster is a technology-layer complement to Shenzhen's electronics (sensors + networking), and
its special-steel and large-aircraft clusters are the *materials* layer beneath Suzhou's equipment.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Wuxi is a **technology-cluster + belt-member city** — a knowledge-economy IoT anchor
coexisting with a traditional textile-belt membership, mirroring Hangzhou's dual economy. Its IoT edge is
the sensor-and-network layer of China's industrial internet; its steel/aerospace clusters are the
upstream materials layer.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Wuxi is the correct anchor for **IoT sensors, networking, and industrial-internet
solutions**, and increasingly for **special-steel and aerospace materials**. For home textiles, it is a
belt member, not the core — route bedding to Nantong.

## Buyer notes

- IoT sensors, networking, industrial-internet → Wuxi (only national IoT cluster).
- Special steel / aerospace materials → Wuxi (national cluster).
- Bedding/home textiles → [Nantong](/cities/nantong/) (SWT belt core).
