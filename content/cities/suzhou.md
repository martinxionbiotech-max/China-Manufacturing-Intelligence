# Manufacturing in Suzhou

Suzhou is a three-cluster city: biopharma (Suzhou Industrial Park), nanotechnology (a global top-5
cluster), and the northern edge of the SWT textile belt.

## City role

Suzhou is the **scale-up (CDMO) engine** of China's biopharma and a rare global nanotech cluster — plus a
textile-belt member.

## What the city makes

- **Biopharma**: Suzhou Industrial Park hosts 2,000+ biomed/health firms with RMB 165.5 billion output,
  #2 national comprehensive competitiveness [cls-suzhou-biomed].
- **Nanotech**: 2022 cluster scale RMB 146 billion (nano-materials above-scale output RMB 82.2 billion),
  one of the world's five major nanotech zones; 1,128 nano firms, 28 CAS/CAE academicians
  [cls-suzhou-nano].

## Data deep-dive

- **2024 above-scale industrial output: RMB 4.7 trillion (a new level); value-added +9.2%** [1].
- **Suzhou Industrial Park GDP: RMB 400.24 billion (2024)** [2].
- **"1030" system: 10 key clusters + 30 key chains**; targeting a 4th trillion-yuan cluster (new energy
  → RMB 1 trillion by 2026), after electronics, high-end equipment, and advanced materials [1][3].

## Cross-cluster position

Suzhou complements [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/)'s Zhangjiang in biopharma — Shanghai discovers and
licenses out; Suzhou develops and scales up (CDMO). On textiles, Suzhou is the northern edge of the SWT
home-textile belt (core: [Nantong](/cities/nantong/)).

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Suzhou is a **scale-and-materials city** — it monetizes others' discoveries (biopharma
CDMO) and builds advanced materials (nano) at industrial scale. Its position between Shanghai
(innovation) and the Delta's manufacturing mass is its structural advantage. The RMB 4.7-trillion
industrial base (with 3–4 trillion-yuan clusters) makes it China's strongest *non-provincial-capital*
manufacturing city.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Suzhou is the correct anchor for **biopharma CDMO/scale-up** and **advanced
nano-materials**. It is not the discovery/licensing node (Shanghai) or the home-textile core (Nantong).
