# Manufacturing in Baotou

Baotou anchors the world's light-rare-earth supply — Bayan Obo (白云鄂博) is the planet's largest
rare-earth mine, feeding the global NdFeB-magnet chain. Its rare-earth output crossed RMB 100 billion in
2024.

## City role

Baotou is the **light-rare-earth feedstock capital**. Its element portfolio (neodymium, praseodymium)
powers the permanent-magnet industry, while [Ganzhou](/cities/ganzhou/) supplies the complementary heavy
elements (dysprosium, terbium).

## What the city makes

Baotou's 2024 rare-earth output was projected past RMB 100 billion, anchored by **Northern Rare Earth
(北方稀土)** — the world's largest producer — which has built the world's largest rare-earth raw-material
and functional-material base on the Bayan Obo deposit [cls-baotou-rare-earth].

## Data deep-dive

- **2024 rare-earth industry output broke the RMB 100 billion mark** [1].
- **8 of China's top-10 magnet firms have set up in Baotou**; 10 of the national magnet top-15 are
  resident — plus a permanent-magnet motor industrial park [1][2].
- **100+ rare-earth enterprises are clustered in the Baotou Rare-Earth High-Tech Zone** [1].
- The city is building **"the country's largest rare-earth new-materials base"** [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Baotou's ore feeds Ningbo's [magnetic-materials cluster](/cities/ningbo/), which converts it into
finished NdFeB magnets — the classic "upstream ore → downstream magnet" split. The two cities are a
single supply chain, not competitors. Baotou's own magnet-firm in-migration (8 of top-10) shows it is
also pulling the downstream layer northward.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Baotou owns the **ore-and-oxide layer**; it is the price-setting node for light rare earth
globally. The "8-of-top-10 magnet firms now resident" shift is significant — Baotou is no longer a pure
ore exporter but is capturing a magnet layer, tightening the ore-to-magnet chain in one geography.
Ganzhou owns the heavy-element layer; Ningbo still holds the finished-magnet crown.

*Opinion:* For buyers, the practical question is *which layer* you buy. Ore and oxides → Baotou; finished
magnets → Ningbo (or Baotou's growing magnet base). Rare earth is export-controlled in both cities —
licensing and end-use verification are the binding constraints, not price alone.

## Buyer notes

- Light rare-earth ore and oxides (Nd, Pr) → Baotou.
- Heavy rare earth (Dy, Tb) → [Ganzhou](/cities/ganzhou/).
- Finished NdFeB magnets → [Ningbo](/cities/ningbo/) (and Baotou's rising magnet tier).
- Export controls apply; verify licensing and end-use up front.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Baotou owns the ore-and-oxide layer of the rare-earth chain — it is the price-setting node for light rare earth globally, not a finished-magnet hub.*

The practical question is which layer you buy: ore and oxides route to Baotou, finished magnets to Ningbo (or Baotou's growing magnet base). Rare earth is a quota-governed market, so layer and licensing matter more than price.
