# Manufacturing in Zhongshan

Zhongshan anchors Guzhen — a sub-50 km² town holding roughly half the world's lighting market, the
densest single-category concentration in this knowledge base.

## City role

Zhongshan is the **lighting category monopoly** — Guzhen is the volume-and-market core of global
lighting, with Shenzhen and Xiamen as component/design and export complements.

## What the city makes

Guzhen holds ~70% of China's domestic lighting market and ~50% of the global market, exporting to 130–
180 countries, with 30,000+ lighting business entities and 200,000+ workers [cls-guzhen-lighting]. The
cluster radiates over 3 cities and 11 towns with RMB 100B+ annual output. The Guzhen Lighting Fair (since
1999) is Asia-Pacific's largest lighting exhibition.

## Data deep-dive

- **2024 GDP RMB 414.32 billion (+3.7%)** — manufacturing value-added = 45% of GDP, a Pearl River Delta
  traditional-manufacturing exemplar [1].
- **"4+6" new "Ten Fleets" cluster system** (incl. the Torch High-Tech Zone) [1].
- **2024 Shenzhen-Zhongshan Link (深中通道) opened**, cutting Shenzhen↔Zhongshan travel time sharply [2].
- **2024 Hong Kong trade: RMB 15.56 billion (5.48% of total)** [3].

## Cross-cluster position

Guzhen is the manufacturing anchor; [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/) supplies LED components/design, Xiamen
export fixtures. The Guzhen Lighting Fair is the industry's discovery-and-trading floor. The new
Shenzhen-Zhongshan Link physically tightens the components↔assembly link.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Guzhen is the **purest category-monopoly cluster** in this dataset — a sub-50 km² town
owning ~half a global market through extreme spatial density (30,000+ entities). Its mechanism is
unmatched price efficiency plus ruthless commodity competition. The Shenzhen-Zhongshan Link is
structural: it fuses Guzhen's commodity manufacturing with Shenzhen's component/design layer, upgrading
a category monopoly toward a value chain.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Zhongshan/Guzhen is the correct floor for **commodity LED fixtures**, but the
"~50% global" figure is an industry estimate, not a government census — treat it as magnitude. Quality
varies sharply across the 30,000-entity tail; pair Guzhen's manufacturing with Shenzhen's
component/design layer when specs matter.

## Buyer notes

- Commodity LED fixtures → Guzhen (30,000+ entity floor).
- Treat "~50% global" as magnitude, not audit.
- Pair with [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/) for design/components.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Guzhen is the purest category-monopoly cluster in this dataset — a sub-50 km² town owning roughly half a global market through extreme spatial density.*

For commodity LED fixtures, Zhongshan and Guzhen are the correct floor. But the "roughly 50% global" figure is an industry estimate, not a government census, and the chip/driver layer is a separate, upstream oligopoly.
