# Manufacturing in Nantong

Nantong anchors China's home-textile industry — the core of the Suzhou-Wuxi-Nantong (SWT) textile belt,
built around the Dieshiqiao (叠石桥) international home-textile market. It is also a ship-and-marine
heavyweight — ~1/10 of China's shipbuilding and 1/4 of its marine engineering.

## City role

Nantong is the **home-textile core** of the SWT belt. Suzhou and Wuxi are the northern edges; Nantong is
where the bedding and fabric mass actually concentrates.

## What the city makes

Nantong's home-textile industry passed RMB 240 billion in 2024, one of the city's six RMB-100B clusters
[cls-swt-textile]. It runs on two markets — Haimen's Dieshiqiao and Tongzhou's Zhihao — with Dieshiqiao
international home-textile city exceeding RMB 150 billion in 2024 transaction value.

## Data deep-dive

- **Six key clusters: RMB 1.2 trillion output (2024), >80% of above-scale industry** [1].
- **Shipbuilding & marine engineering: > RMB 200 billion; ~1/10 of national ships, 1/4 of national
  marine engineering** — delivered 40+ "world-first / national-first" vessel types [2].
- **Above-scale industrial firms: 5,262 → 7,465** (five-year span) [1].
- **15 firms above RMB 10 billion; Zhongtian Technology (中天科技) crossed RMB 100 billion** [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Nantong is the **home-textile layer** of China's textile system, distinct from [Shaoxing](/cities/shaoxing/)
(fabric/dyeing) and [Hangzhou Bay](/cities/hangzhou/) (garments). It is the bedding-and-fabric anchor;
its shipbuilding is a separate, co-equal pillar shared with the Shandong ship cluster's orbit.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Nantong is a **market-plus-factory home-textile cluster** — the Dieshiqiao market is the
discovery layer, thousands of bedding makers the factory layer behind it. The "live-stream + industry
belt" model is shifting its supply chain toward "small-lot, quick-response" (小单快反). Its
shipbuilding/marine-engineering (1/4 of national) is a second industrial identity many buyers miss.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Nantong is the correct anchor for **bedding and home textiles** (Dieshiqiao), with
shipbuilding/marine engineering as a major secondary track. The RMB 240B figure is city-level media
reporting, not consolidated cluster output — treat it as magnitude. For raw fabric and dyeing, go to
Shaoxing; for garments, Hangzhou Bay.

## Buyer notes

- Bedding and home textiles → Nantong (Dieshiqiao, RMB 150B market).
- Shipbuilding / marine engineering → Nantong (1/4 of national).
- Raw fabric/dyeing → [Shaoxing](/cities/shaoxing/).
- Garments → [Hangzhou Bay](/cities/hangzhou/).
