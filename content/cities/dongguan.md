# Manufacturing in Dongguan

Dongguan is China's smartphone-assembly powerhouse — 1 in 5 phones worldwide — and the furniture half of
the Foshan-Dongguan "pan-home" cluster. It is a "world factory" that keeps redefining itself upward.

## City role

Dongguan is the **terminal-assembly layer** of the Pearl River Delta electronics corridor, sitting one
step downstream of Shenzhen's component-and-design layer.

## What the city makes

Dongguan's 2024 smartphone shipments were ~24% of global total — roughly 1 in 5 phones worldwide — with 1
in 8 global phones from Chang'an Town alone [cls-dongguan-smart-terminal]. Huawei/OPPO/vivo's combined
output grew 13.3% in 2023, reversing three years of decline. The city has ~220,000 industrial
enterprises, ~14,000 above designated scale. Chang'an Town's 2025 GDP hit RMB 110 billion.

## Data deep-dive

- **GDP: RMB 1.228 trillion (2024), +4.6%**; secondary industry +6.6% [1].
- **Global share: ~1/5 smartphones, 1/5 sweaters, 1/4 animation derivatives, ~85% of China's art toys**
  [2].
- **Electrical machinery & equipment manufacturing > RMB 500 billion; new materials > RMB 100 billion**
  [2].
- **300+ national "little-giant" SMEs** — most among prefecture-level cities in the province [2].
- **Cross-border e-commerce export growth: ~25%/yr** [2].

## Cross-cluster position

Dongguan pairs with [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/) as complementary layers: Shenzhen = components/PCB/
design + Huaqiangbei distribution; Dongguan = scale terminal assembly. On the furniture side, Dongguan
co-anchors the Foshan-Dongguan pan-home cluster (RMB 1.5 trillion, 14 categories) with
[Foshan](/cities/foshan/), hosting the "world-class furniture cluster" events.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Dongguan is a **dual-industry city** — electronics (smartphones) and furniture (pan-home) —
a rare combination that reflects the Pearl River Delta's "one city, many supply chains" pattern. Its
electronics role is scale assembly; its furniture role is market-and-factory co-location. The
cross-border e-commerce 25%/yr growth is a distribution-speed moat, not a manufacturing one.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Dongguan is the correct anchor for **finished smartphone/terminal assembly**
(Huawei/OPPO/vivo tier). For components, go upstream to Shenzhen. For furniture, pair Dongguan's
world-class-furniture events with Foshan's Lecong showrooms. The 1/5-global-sweater figure is a reminder
that Dongguan's breadth extends far beyond electronics.

## Buyer notes

- Finished smartphones/terminals → Dongguan (Huawei/OPPO/vivo).
- Components/PCB/design → [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/).
- Furniture (pan-home) → Dongguan + [Foshan](/cities/foshan/) jointly.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Dongguan is a dual-industry city — smartphones and furniture — a rare combination that reflects the Pearl River Delta's "one city, one chain" pattern.*

For finished smartphone and terminal assembly (the Huawei, OPPO, vivo tier), Dongguan is the anchor. For components, go upstream to Shenzhen; for furniture, it is the Foshan-Dongguan pair. Each half of the city routes differently.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
