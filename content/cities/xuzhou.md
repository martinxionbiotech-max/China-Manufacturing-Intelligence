# Manufacturing in Xuzhou

Xuzhou anchors China's single-champion construction-machinery pole — XCMG (徐工), the integrated group
behind the country's #1 excavator output. It is also a case study in how a legacy coal-and-steel
industrial city re-pivoted its entire economy around advanced manufacturing.

## City role

Xuzhou is the **single-champion pole** of China's construction-machinery industry, complementing
[Changsha](/cities/changsha/)'s multi-brand pole. As a former heavy-industrial city, it rebuilt its base
around an explicitly named "343" innovation-cluster system — three advantage clusters, four emerging,
three future — with construction machinery as its "No. 1 industry."

## What the city makes

XCMG posted RMB 91.66 billion 2024 revenue (overseas RMB 41.69 billion, +12%) [cls-xuzhou-construction-
machinery]. XCMG leads in excavators (RMB 40B, #1), cranes (RMB 18B, #1), and pile machinery (RMB 9B,
#1). Beyond the champion, Xuzhou runs a **"1+100+1000" enterprise matrix** — one RMB 100-billion
company, 100 firms above RMB 100 million, and 1,000+ SMEs — organized around XCMG's R&D system [1].

## Data deep-dive

- **GDP: RMB 900 billion+ (2024)**, and RMB 729.81 billion in the first three quarters of 2025
  (+6.0%, tied with Huai'an for Jiangsu's fastest) [3].
- **"343" innovation clusters: RMB 780 billion (2024)** — construction machinery is the largest [1].
- **Construction-machinery cluster: RMB 200 billion+ annual output, >20% national market share** [2].
- **Enterprise matrix: 1 × RMB 100B + 100 × RMB 100M + 1,000+ SMEs** [1].
- 2024 added one national manufacturing "single-champion" (XCMG new-energy loaders) and two national
  "little-giant" specialists, lifting the city's cumulative count to 12 [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Xuzhou and [Changsha](/cities/changsha/) are the industry's two-horse race: Xuzhou = integration and
full lineage (one group), Changsha = competition and variety (five firms, 70% of national variety).

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Xuzhou is a **champion-led city** — one vertically-integrated group (XCMG) carrying a full
machine lineage (excavator → crane → pile). The "1+100+1000" matrix is the structural signature: a
single anchor firm pulls a deep supplier ecosystem, the classic central-SOE-plus-cluster pattern.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Xuzhou is the correct anchor for **single-account scale and full-lineage
consistency** (XCMG). For multi-vendor negotiation leverage and niche specs, route to Changsha. The
choice is procurement-strategy-driven, not quality-driven — both are export-grade. The GDP trajectory
(700B in 2019 → 900B in 2024 → pacing toward RMB 1 trillion) confirms the re-pivot worked, but also
flags a concentration risk: Xuzhou's manufacturing story is unusually dependent on one champion.

## Buyer notes

- Single-vendor scale, full machine lineage → Xuzhou (XCMG).
- Multi-vendor leverage, niche specs → [Changsha](/cities/changsha/).
- Concentration risk: one champion carries the cluster — qualify the supplier ecosystem, not just XCMG.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Xuzhou is a champion-led city — one vertically-integrated group (XCMG) carrying a full machine lineage, from excavator to crane to pile.*

For single-account scale and full-lineage consistency (XCMG), Xuzhou is the correct anchor. For multi-vendor negotiation leverage and niche specs, route to Changsha instead — the two are different strategies.
