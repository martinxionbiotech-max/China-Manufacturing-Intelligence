# Manufacturing in Ningde

Ningde anchors the densest battery concentration on earth — CATL (宁德时代), the world's #1 battery
maker, in a city of 330 GWh of lithium capacity.

## City role

Ningde is the **global power-battery capital**, and Fujian's *only* national advanced-manufacturing
cluster. It is the shared upstream of both China's NEV poles (Changchun's FAW and Shanghai's Tesla).

## What the city makes

CATL held 39.2% global power-battery share in 2025 (#1 for 9 straight years) and 30.4% of global
energy-storage shipments (#1 for 5 years) [cls-ningde-power-battery]. CATL's 2025 revenue was RMB 423.7
billion (+17%), net profit RMB 72.2 billion (+42%), with 772 GWh global capacity.

## Data deep-dive

- **Lithium-battery new energy: Ningde's first RMB 200B+ cluster (2022); stainless-steel new materials
  its second (2023)** [1].
- **CATL >130 GWh energy-storage shipments (2025, #1); Hithium (海辰储能) rose to global #2** [1].
- **First entry into the global top-100 innovation clusters (2025)** [1].
- World-first outputs: sodium-ion batteries passing the new national standard, 9 MWh mega energy-storage
  systems, condensed-matter batteries, and "Shenxing" super-fast-charging cells [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Ningde sits at the **top of China's NEV supply chain** — its cells power FAW (Changchun), Tesla
(Shanghai), and the whole EV industry. The Fuding CATL base (120 GWh planned, >RMB 100B output) is CATL's
largest single project globally.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Ningde is a **champion-anchored ecosystem** — CATL plus 90+ upstream/downstream firms in
one city. Its 39.2% global share is a genuine concentration risk *for buyers*: the industry is far less
diversified than multi-brand electronics or construction machinery. The Hithium #2 rise is a nascent
second pole inside the same city.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Ningde is unavoidable for high-performance cells, but the 39% concentration argues
for **dual-sourcing** — anchor volume with CATL-tier cells while qualifying a second source (e.g.,
FAW-Fudi in Changchun) for resilience. Lead times and allocation matter here more than in any other
cluster.

## Buyer notes

- High-performance EV and storage cells → Ningde (CATL, 39.2% global).
- Dual-source against concentration risk; FAW-Fudi (Changchun) is one adjacent cell source.
- Plan capacity/lead time early — CATL scale means allocation, not just price.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Ningde is a champion-anchored ecosystem — CATL plus 90-plus upstream and downstream firms in one city — and its dominant global share is a genuine concentration risk for buyers.*

Ningde is unavoidable for high-performance cells, but the concentration argues for dual-sourcing: anchor volume with CATL-tier cells while qualifying a credible second source. Do not put the whole supply chain in one city.
