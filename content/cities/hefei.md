# Manufacturing in Hefei

Hefei anchors China's intelligent-voice industry — "China Speech Valley" (中国声谷), the densest
voice-and-language AI cluster in the country. It is also the "Hefei model" city: four pillars spanning
display, NEVs, IC, and advanced PV.

## City role

Hefei is the **voice-AI manufacturing anchor**, built around iFlytek (科大讯飞), with Beijing and
Shenzhen as the research and hardware-integration complements.

## What the city makes

Hefei's intelligent-voice cluster entered the national advanced-manufacturing list in H1 2025, gathering
iFlytek, Geovis (中科星图), and Amazfit (华米科技) [cls-hefei-intelligent-voice]. "China Speech Valley"
reached RMB 65 billion output and 430 resident firms by 2018. The "iFlytek Town" AI campus opened in
September 2024 and has drawn 1,000+ innovation enterprises.

## Data deep-dive

- **Four pillars:** new display, NEVs, IC, and advanced PV + new-energy storage [1].
- **New display output: RMB 123.535 billion (2024), +21.5%** — BOE (global display leader) anchors 200+
  upstream/downstream firms [2].
- **NEVs: >1.35 million units (2024)** — BYD, NIO, Volkswagen-Anhui, 65% local parts rate [1].
- **Advanced PV + storage: RMB 150 billion; PV inverter shipments #1 globally** [3].

## Cross-cluster position

Hefei's voice cluster is an **algorithm-plus-institution cluster** — built on USTC research and a national
champion, not cheap labor or ports. Its natural complement is [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/) for
edge-AI hardware integration.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Hefei's voice industry is structurally "sticky" — anchored by a national champion and a
research university, not by labor-cost arbitrage. But that also concentrates risk in iFlytek's
trajectory. The 1,000-firm iFlytek Town signals an ecosystem widening beyond the single champion, while
the four-pillar display/NEV/IC/PV base shows the "Hefei model" of government-anchored cluster building.

*Opinion:* For buyers, the split is **software/algorithm licensing** (Hefei, iFlytek) versus **hardware
integration** (Shenzhen). "AI" is too broad a sourcing category — voice, edge AI, and smart-terminal AI
are separate chains anchored in different cities.

## Buyer notes

- Voice/LLM software, voice modules → Hefei (iFlytek ecosystem).
- Edge-AI and smart-terminal hardware integration → [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/).
- Watch the iFlytek Town ecosystem widening as a second-tier supplier signal.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Hefei's voice industry is structurally sticky — anchored by a national champion and a research university, not by labor-cost arbitrage — but that also concentrates the category.*

For software and algorithm licensing (iFlytek), Hefei is the anchor; for hardware integration, Shenzhen. "AI" is too broad a sourcing category — split the software from the hardware and the routing becomes obvious.
