# Manufacturing in Weihai

Weihai co-builds the Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai shipbuilding and marine-engineering cluster — the third city
in Shandong's three-city national cluster. It is also quietly a printer-and-carbon-fiber base: one of
the few Chinese cities manufacturing whole printers at global scale.

## City role

Weihai is the **co-builder node** of the three-city shipbuilding cluster. Qingdao carries total assembly
(Beihai Shipbuilding), Yantai carries marine engineering (CIMC Raffles), and Weihai carries supporting
fabrication and co-building.

## What the city makes

The shipbuilding cluster entered the national advanced-manufacturing list in December 2024, jointly built
by Qingdao, Yantai, and Weihai, with ~140 above-scale assembly/supporting firms and 40%+ local
supporting rate for main ship types [cls-qingdao-shipbuilding]. Weihai's broader industrial base: 1,402
above-scale firms across 35 industry classes, with 2024 above-scale industrial revenue of RMB 282.14
billion [1]. Six firms exceed RMB 10 billion — Weigao (medical), Goertek (acoustics), Dison, Chengshan,
Triangle, and Hengbang [1].

## Data deep-dive

- **Above-scale industrial revenue: RMB 282.14 billion (2024), +3.1%** [1].
- **Ship/rail/aerospace equipment manufacturing value-added: +57%** (2024) — the fastest industry [2].
- **Printers: 17 million laser printers/year**, with 94%+ parts localized — targeting "1 of every 3
  printers globally" by 2025 [3].
- **Carbon fiber** — a national SME-characteristic cluster in Huancui district [3].

## Cross-cluster position

Weihai's role is **role-division, not leadership** — it is the supporting fabrication leg of a three-city
cluster. A buyer sourcing supporting fabrication or co-building routes here; total assembly routes to
Qingdao, marine engineering to Yantai.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Weihai illustrates the **role-split cluster** — the national designation covers three cities
with distinct functions. But its +57% ship-equipment growth reveals a second identity: a *supporting-
industrial city* that grows fastest in the upstream layers (fabrication, parts, printers) rather than
headline assembly.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Weihai is best used as the **supporting-fabrication node** in a coordinated
Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai sourcing strategy, plus a **printer-and-carbon-fiber** source outside shipbuilding.
Do not expect it to be the total-assembly or marine-engineering anchor — those are Qingdao and Yantai.

## Buyer notes

- Supporting fabrication and co-building → Weihai.
- Laser printers (94% localized parts) and carbon fiber → Weihai.
- Total assembly / large vessels → [Qingdao](/cities/qingdao/).
- Marine-engineering platforms → [Yantai](/cities/yantai/).

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Weihai illustrates the role-split cluster — the national designation covers three cities with distinct functions, and Weihai is the supporting-fabrication node.*

So it is best used as the fabrication node in a coordinated Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai sourcing strategy, plus a printer-and-carbon-fiber secondary track. Do not treat it as a standalone shipbuilding anchor.
