# Manufacturing in Shenyang

Shenyang is the birthplace of Chinese robotics — the CAS institute that built China's first industrial
robot, and SIASUN (新松), the national robot industrialization base.

## City role

Shenyang is the **technology-origin pole** of Chinese robotics. Shanghai and Shenzhen are the
integration-and-application nodes; Shenyang is where the technology was born.

## What the city makes

The CAS Shenyang Institute of Automation has ~50 years of robotics research and produced China's first
industrial-robot prototype [cls-shenyang-robotics]. SIASUN, founded 2000, entered the MIIT *Industrial
Robot Industry Standard Conditions (2024 edition)* list and is pursuing "robot + AI" fusion (embodied
intelligence).

## Data deep-dive

- **The robotics cluster is led by 7 academicians and gathers 40+ national experts** from the CAS
  Shenyang Institute of Automation, Institute of Metal Research, and China Machinery Institute [1].
- **Liaoning added 3 national clusters in Dec 2024 — Shenyang aviation, Shenyang-Dalian machine tools,
  Dalian-Panjin green petrochemical — raising the province to 4** [1].
- **Northeast Food Sci-Tech Park: RMB 10.5 billion investment, 3.24 km²** (Sujiatun) — a food-industry
  diversification track [2].

## Cross-cluster position

Shenyang anchors **robot arms**; Shanghai/Shenzhen anchor **system integration and deployment**. The
"robot + AI" embodied-intelligence pivot is diverging these into separate supply chains. Its
Shenyang-Dalian machine-tool cluster adds a mother-machine layer alongside Taizhou.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Robotics is a **technology-origin cluster**, not a volume cluster — Shenyang's strength is
the ~50-year CAS lineage and SIASUN's industrial base, not enterprise density. The volume and deployment
actually concentrate in the Delta (Shenzhen produced 133,900 industrial robots in 2024). The 7-academician
leadership marks it as a research-anchored cluster, like Hefei's voice AI.

*Opinion:* For buyers, the split is **robot arms (Shenyang/SIASUN)** versus **system integration
(Shanghai/Shenzhen)**. Evaluate whether a supplier is a traditional-arm maker or an AI-integration
player — they are diverging chains. Shenyang anchors the former; Shenzhen owns volume and deployment.

## Buyer notes

- Industrial robot arms / national-standard tech → Shenyang (SIASUN).
- System integration and AI deployment → [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/) / [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/).
- Evaluate "robot + AI" capability separately from classic arm hardware.
