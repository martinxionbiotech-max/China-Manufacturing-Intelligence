# Manufacturing in Foshan

Foshan is China's densest "home" manufacturing city — ceramics, furniture, and home appliances — three
separate world-class clusters inside one prefecture. Above-scale industrial output crossed RMB 3
trillion, making it China's #4 industrial city.

## City role

Foshan is a **multi-cluster home-goods capital**: building ceramics, furniture, and home appliances, all
under the Foshan-Dongguan "pan-home" umbrella (RMB 1.5 trillion, 14 categories).

## What the city makes

- **Ceramics**: RMB 101.97 billion 2023 output, 745M m² of tiles, ~80% of China's ceramic presses
  (Lital, Keda), and the 2020-registered "Foshan Ceramics" collective trademark [cls-foshan-ceramics].
- **Furniture**: Shunde's 30,000+ furniture firms and the 2M m² Lecong Furniture City
  [cls-foshan-furniture].
- **Appliances**: Shunde produces ~15% of China's appliances, passed RMB 400 billion output, anchored by
  Midea (Beijiao HQ, WEF Lighthouse Factory) and Galanz [cls-shunde-home-appliances].

## Data deep-dive

- **Above-scale industrial output value: > RMB 3 trillion (2023)** — Guangdong's #3 economy, China's #4
  industrial city [1].
- **"2+2+4" cluster system** — two strategic pillars, two strategic-emerging, four characteristic [1].
- **Exports to Hong Kong: RMB 27.94 billion (2024)**, 7.21% of the city total [2].

## Cross-cluster position

Foshan's three clusters are unified by the **"pan-home" concept** — ceramics, furniture, and appliances
are complementary categories a home buyer sources together. The Foshan-Dongguan pan-home cluster
formalizes this cross-category bundling.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Foshan is a **category-agglomeration city** — its edge is that a single buyer can source
ceramics + furniture + appliances in one geography. The "pan-home" framing is a deliberate
cross-cluster strategy to bundle 14 categories into one procurement surface, and the RMB 3-trillion
industrial base means the bundling rests on genuine depth in each category.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Foshan is the strongest single-city floor for **home-goods multi-category
sourcing**. Its weakness is that two of its three clusters (ceramics, furniture) are consolidating or
value-shifting — ceramics from volume to machinery, furniture still fragmented. Appliances (Midea/Galanz)
are the most industrialized of the three, and the ones to anchor on.

## Buyer notes

- Ceramics (branded tile + production machinery) → Foshan.
- Furniture (walk Lecong showrooms) → Foshan.
- Kitchen appliances → Shunde (Midea, Galanz).
- Bundle all three via the pan-home framing for one-geography sourcing.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Foshan is a category-agglomeration city — its edge is that a single buyer can source ceramics, furniture, and appliances in one geography, the "pan-home" frame.*

That makes it the strongest single-city floor for home-goods multi-category sourcing. Its weakness is that two of its three clusters (ceramics, furniture) are mature and consolidating, so anchor on branded suppliers.
