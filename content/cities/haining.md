# Manufacturing in Haining

Haining anchors China's leather-and-fur fashion industry — the "China Leather Capital," built around the
listed Haining China Leather City. It is also a fast-rising photovoltaics-and-semiconductor county under
Jiaxing.

## City role

Haining is a **market-plus-fashion cluster** — the Leather City trading platform is the organizing asset,
with thousands of small makers behind it. Structurally it mirrors Foshan's furniture (Lecong) model.

## What the city makes

Haining "leveraged one piece of leather into a RMB-100B industry cluster" [cls-haining-leather]. Haining
China Leather City took ~50 quality enterprises to the 2024 CHIC spring show with a 3,200 m²
autumn-winter display, and posted RMB 237.7 million in 2024 revenue. Its core business is market
development and operation (leasing, shops) — confirming the platform is the value anchor.

## Data deep-dive

- **Above-scale industrial value-added: RMB 51.5 billion (2024), +8.7%** [1].
- **"142" cluster system:** 1 thousand-yuan fashion cluster + 4 RMB 50B clusters (pan-semiconductor,
  high-end equipment, PV new-energy, new-materials) + 2 RMB 10B clusters [2].
- **PV: Jinko and Chint as dual anchors**, a "PV + storage" model in Zhejiang's "415X" core zone [1].
- **Pan-semiconductor: > RMB 25 billion output (2023)** — a national semiconductor-equipment materials
  base [1].

## Cross-cluster position

Haining is the leather/fur fashion node; it has no direct competitor city in this dataset (leather is a
single-city industry). Its nearest structural analogue is [Foshan's furniture](/cities/foshan/), which
also runs on a "trading floor + maker tiers" model. Its PV/pan-semiconductor tracks connect it to the
wider Jiaxing "135N" system.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Haining's leather industry is a **seasonal fashion supply chain** — its strength is finished
autumn-winter garments (coats, fur), where it holds "dominant" influence, not commodity hides. The
"142" cluster system shows a deliberate pivot: leather (the heritage pillar) is now flanked by PV and
pan-semiconductor, diversifying a one-industry county.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Haining is the correct anchor for **finished, fashion-tier leather/fur garments**,
sourced in season (aligned to the CHIC spring / autumn-winter cycle). Commodity leather hides and mass
garment blanks are not its strength — match the fashion tier, not the commodity tier. The PV/
pan-semiconductor tracks are secondary options, not the headline.
