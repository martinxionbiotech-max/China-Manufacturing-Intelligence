# Manufacturing in Chengdu

Chengdu anchors western China's electronics industry — the inland counterweight to the Pearl River
Delta's Shenzhen–Dongguan corridor. It became a RMB 1-trillion electronics chain in 2020 and RMB 1.3
trillion by 2023.

## City role

Chengdu is the **inland ICT anchor**, jointly building the Chengdu-Chongqing electronics cluster with
[Chongqing](/cities/chongqing/) — one of four "trillion-yuan" (万亿级) clusters the two cities co-build.

## What the city makes

Chengdu's electronics industry reached RMB 1.3 trillion in 2023, with RMB 792.6 billion in the first
eight months of 2024 (+5.6%) [cls-cy-electronics]. The Chengdu-Chongqing economic zone's electronics
output and auto production together account for more than 1/10 of the national total.

## Data deep-dive

- **Crossed RMB 1 trillion in 2020, then RMB 1.3 trillion in 2023** — a ~20-year "slow-burn" build that
  stands out among leading electronics agglomerations [1].
- The chain runs on **display (BOE), chips, and terminal assembly** as the inland ICT spine, with a
  robotics cluster (78 chain firms incl. national/provincial specialists) emerging in the Chenghua
  Economic Zone [2].

## Cross-cluster position

Chengdu-Chongqing is the **cost-optimized inland alternative** to Shenzhen-Dongguan. It does not compete
on component density (Shenzhen) or terminal assembly (Dongguan) — it competes on inland labor/logistics
cost and the "twin-city" scale of two megacities combined.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* The Chengdu-Chongqing cluster is a **policy-and-geography cluster** — its "trillion-yuan"
scale is partly a product of the twin-city economic-zone policy, not just organic market density. That
distinguishes it from Shenzhen's market-grown electronics. Chengdu's ~20-year climb (not a sudden spike)
suggests a genuinely deepened chain rather than a policy-only headline.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Chengdu-Chongqing is the right anchor for **cost-sensitive, inland-scale
electronics assembly and ICT manufacturing**. It is not the place for the deepest component ecosystem —
that remains Shenzhen. Treat the two geographies as complementary tiers, not substitutes.

## Buyer notes

- Cost-optimized inland electronics assembly → Chengdu-Chongqing.
- Deepest component/distribution ecosystem → [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/).
- Terminal-assembly scale → [Dongguan](/cities/dongguan/).

## The Author's Take

*In my view, the Chengdu-Chongqing cluster is a policy-and-geography cluster — its "trillion-yuan" scale is partly a product of the twin-city economic-zone policy, not just organic industrial depth.*

For buyers, it is the right anchor for cost-sensitive, inland-scale electronics assembly and ICT manufacturing. It is not the place for design, component, or cutting-edge work — that stays in Shenzhen and the Deltas.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
