# Manufacturing in Ningbo

Ningbo is a four-cluster manufacturing city: magnetic materials, Cixi small appliances, green
petrochemicals, and a seat in the Hangzhou Bay textile belt. It is also China's first "city of 100
single-champions."

## City role

Ningbo is Zhejiang's **most diversified industrial city** in this dataset — four distinct clusters,
spanning advanced materials, appliances, petrochemicals, and textiles.

## What the city makes

- **Magnetic materials**: ~22% of China's rare-earth magnet firms, 40%+ of national output — a
  "something-from-nothing" cluster built with no local rare-earth ore [cls-ningbo-magnetic-materials].
  Ningbo Yunsheng posted RMB 5.464 billion 2025 revenue (+8.39%), selling 14,197 tonnes of NdFeB.
- **Cixi small appliances**: RMB 108.38 billion 2025 core-cluster revenue; Yueda ships 20,000 air fryers
  daily [cls-cixi-small-appliances].
- **Green petrochemicals**: Zhenhai Refining's "world-class" base, targeting RMB 400 billion by 2025
  [cls-ningbo-green-petrochemical].

## Data deep-dive

- **National single-champion firms: 104 (added 21 in 2024) — China's first "hundred-champion city"
  (百冠之城)** [1].
- **NEV output > 42% of Zhejiang's total**; above-scale AI manufacturing value-added +15.5% (2024) [2].
- **"361" industrial system:** 3 world-class clusters + 6 high-energy clusters + future tracks [3].

## Cross-cluster position

Ningbo's magnetic-materials cluster is the **downstream of Baotou/Ganzhou rare earth** — it converts
upstream ore into finished NdFeB magnets. Its petrochemical cluster is the materials backbone for the
broader Delta economy.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Ningbo's defining trait is **vertical complementarity** — it takes others' raw materials
(rare earth from Baotou/Ganzhou) and adds the high-value magnet layer. The "104 single-champions" figure
is the clearest signal of *breadth* — Ningbo's real moat is not one champion but a hundred specialized
niche leaders. Its "no ore, still #1" magnetic story is the clearest "industrial base beats resource
endowment" case in this dataset.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Ningbo is the correct anchor for **finished NdFeB magnets** (downstream of ore)
and **niche small appliances** (Cixi). It is not the ore source — that is Baotou/Ganzhou. Route by
layer: ore upstream, magnet downstream.

## Buyer notes

- Finished NdFeB magnets → Ningbo (Yunsheng, 40%+ national).
- Niche small appliances (air fryers, personal care) → Cixi.
- Ore/oxide → [Baotou](/cities/baotou/) / [Ganzhou](/cities/ganzhou/).
