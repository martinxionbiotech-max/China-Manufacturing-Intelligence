# Where Are Ships Built in China? The Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai Cluster

China's shipbuilding includes the Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai marine-engineering cluster — Beihai
Shipbuilding, CIMC Raffles, 140+ firms, 40%+ local supply.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8901 (cruise ships, excursion boats, ferries, cargo ships, barges).
- **Anchor cluster:** Qingdao shipbuilding (`cls-qingdao-shipbuilding`).
- **Model:** three-city role-split (assembly / marine-engineering / fabrication).

## The cluster, in data

The Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai cluster holds 140+ firms and 40%+ local supply — Beihai Shipbuilding (total
assembly), CIMC Raffles (marine engineering), and Weihai co-building fabrication [cls-qingdao-
shipbuilding].

## Market data

**2023 above-scale shipbuilding revenue: RMB 623.7 billion (+20%); profit RMB 25.9 billion (+131.7%)**
[1]. 2024 H1 revenue was RMB 327.35 billion (+23.8%) with profit +137.3% — a high-boom cycle [1]. 2024
completion was ~45 million DWT, with new orders ~50 million DWT [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Shipbuilding is a **cyclical, capital-intensive champion industry** — the profit-growth
outpacing revenue is the signature of a supply-constrained upcycle where yards hold pricing power. The
three-city division (assembly/marine-engineering/fabrication) is a role-split, not competition.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **total assembly/large vessels → [Qingdao](/cities/qingdao/) (Beihai)**;
**marine-engineering platforms → [Yantai](/cities/yantai/) (CIMC Raffles)**; **co-building → [Weihai](/cities/weihai/)**.
In a tight upcycle, secure yard slots and green-ship specs early — capacity, not price, is the binding
constraint.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 8901** — verify vessel type (cargo vs. ferry vs. cruise) subheading.
- Assembly → [Qingdao](/cities/qingdao/); marine engineering → [Yantai](/cities/yantai/); co-building → [Weihai](/cities/weihai/).
- **Caveat:** RMB 623.7B is above-scale revenue; the upcycle favors early slot-locking.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, shipbuilding is a cyclical, capital-intensive champion industry, and profit growth outpacing revenue is the signature of a supply-constrained upcycle — which affects both price and lead time.*

Route by role: total assembly and large vessels to Qingdao, marine-engineering platforms to Yantai, co-building fabrication to Weihai. Treat the three as one role-split cluster, not three ports.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
