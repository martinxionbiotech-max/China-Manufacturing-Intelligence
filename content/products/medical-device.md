# Where Are Medical Devices Made in China? Shenzhen and Suzhou

China's medical-device manufacturing anchors on Shenzhen (Mindray, patient monitors #1 for 15 years)
with Suzhou as a second hub.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 9018 (instruments/appliances used in medical/surgical/veterinary sciences).
- **Anchor clusters:** Shenzhen-Guangzhou medical devices (`cls-sg-medical-devices`), Suzhou biopharma
  (`cls-suzhou-biomed`).
- **Model:** hardware anchor (Shenzhen) + device-adjacent biologics (Suzhou).

## The cluster, in data

Mindray's patient-monitoring line is China's #1 for 15 consecutive years; the ~20 km² Shenzhen Medical
Device City is the physical signal of high-end device priority [cls-sg-medical-devices]. Suzhou
complements with device-adjacent biologics and consumables scale-up.

## Market data

**2023 medical-device industry revenue: RMB 1.31 trillion — #2 globally, 10% CAGR (2014–2023)** [1]. By
H1 2024, 1.42 million Class-II/III device operating enterprises existed nationally, Shanghai alone
holding 350,000 [1].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Medical devices sit at the **hardware-software intersection** — combining electronics
(Shenzhen's strength), precision manufacturing, and clinical validation. That is why Shenzhen, not a
pure-pharma city, is the device anchor. The 1.42-million-enterprise base shows a vast but still mostly
distribution-heavy structure.

*Opinion:* For buyers, the practical split mirrors biopharma: **hardware devices** (monitors, ultrasound,
IVD instruments) → Shenzhen; **device-adjacent consumables/biologics scale-up** → Suzhou. Regulatory
load (NMPA + CE/FDA) is the real cost driver — factor certification lead time into sourcing, not just
unit price.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, medical devices sit at the hardware-software intersection, which is why the sourcing split mirrors biopharma — hardware in one place, scale-up in another.*

Hardware devices (monitors, ultrasound, IVD instruments) go to Shenzhen; device-adjacent consumables and biologics scale-up go to Suzhou. Recognize which half of the device you are actually buying.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
