# Where Are Solar Panels Made in China? The Yancheng-Changzhou PV Belt

China's PV module manufacturing spans Yancheng, Changzhou, Wuxi, and Hefei — anchored by Trina Solar's
Yancheng base.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8541 (diodes, transistors, photovoltaic cells/panels).
- **Anchor cluster:** Yancheng PV (`cls-yancheng-pv`).
- **Model:** full-chain, cost-leadership belt.

## The cluster, in data

Yancheng anchors the PV manufacturing base (Trina Solar, 106 above-scale chain firms) with Changzhou,
Wuxi, and Hefei completing the Jiangsu-Anhui PV belt [cls-yancheng-pv].

## Market data

**2024 new PV installations: 277.6 GW (+28.3%); cumulative 885.7 GW** — #1 globally for 12 straight
years [1]. The optimistic 2030 projection is 1,000+ GW of new global PV installations [1]. China owns
the full polysilicon-wafer-cell-module chain.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Solar panels are a **full-chain, cost-leadership industry** — China owns the entire value
chain and sets global module pricing. The 12-year #1 installation streak reflects a policy-supported
domestic market that also subsidizes global cost leadership. The key risk is grid absorption and price
over-competition, not technology.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **PV modules → Yancheng (Trina Solar)** and the Jiangsu-Anhui belt. The
cost leadership means module pricing is globally competitive, but expect volatility — lock contracts and
dual-source across the belt. Pair PV with Yancheng's offshore-wind cluster for integrated clean-energy
capex.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 8541** — PV cells/panels classify with semiconductors; verify subheading.
- PV modules → [Yancheng](/cities/yancheng/) (Trina Solar); dual-source across the belt.
- **Caveat:** 277.6 GW is new installations, not manufacturing output.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, solar panels are a full-chain, cost-leadership industry — China owns the entire value chain and sets global module pricing, which is both the opportunity and the risk.*

For PV modules, Yancheng (Trina Solar) and the Jiangsu-Anhui belt are the anchors. The cost leadership means globally competitive pricing, but expect volatility — you are buying into a price-setting, not price-taking, node.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
