# Where Is Agricultural Machinery Made in China? Luoyang and YTO

Luoyang (洛阳), Henan, anchors China's agricultural-machinery manufacturing — YTO Group (中国一拖),
300+ linked firms, RMB 60 billion output, and Henan's first national advanced-manufacturing cluster.

## Product at a glance

- **HS codes:** 8432 (soil prep / cultivation machinery), 8433 (harvesting/threshing machinery).
- **Anchor cluster:** Luoyang agricultural machinery cluster (`cls-luoyang-agri-machinery`).
- **Model:** single-champion anchor (YTO) + one national cluster.

## The cluster, in data

The Luoyang cluster entered the national list in 2024 — Henan's "zero-to-one" breakthrough — led by
YTO with 300+ linked firms and RMB 60 billion output [cls-luoyang-agri-machinery]. YTO's large-wheel
tractor line and the 8568 multi-function combine harvester were developed by the National Agricultural
Machinery Equipment Innovation Center.

## Market data

China's agricultural-machinery market reached **RMB 610 billion (2024)**, while above-scale enterprise
revenue was an estimated **RMB 195.8 billion** [1]. **YTO (一拖股份) led the sector with RMB 10.96
billion in 2024 agricultural-machinery revenue** [2]. The industry is consolidating after a 2023 dip
(above-scale revenue RMB 188.32 billion, -7.6%) [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Agricultural machinery is a **single-champion industry** — one anchor (YTO), one city
(Luoyang), one national cluster. That concentration produces a deep, stable supply chain but weaker
competitive pricing pressure than multi-brand sectors. The RMB 610B market-vs-RMB 195.8B above-scale
gap reflects a large but fragmented downstream service/trade layer.

*Opinion:* For buyers, split **complete machines → Luoyang (YTO)** versus **engines/powertrain →
[Weifang](/cities/weifang/) (Weichai)**. For tractors and combines, YTO is the standards-backed choice;
for powertrain components, route to the engine chain.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 8432/8433** — separate cultivation from harvesting machinery.
- Complete machines → [Luoyang](/cities/luoyang/); engines → [Weifang](/cities/weifang/).
- **Caveat:** RMB 610B is a broad market estimate; above-scale revenue is the narrower, auditable figure.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, agricultural machinery is a single-champion industry — one anchor, one city, one cluster — so the real sourcing decision is which machine you need, not which region to shop.*

For complete tractors and combines, YTO in Luoyang is the anchor; for engines and powertrain, Weichai in Weifang is a separate lane. That concentration gives you a deep, stable supply chain, but it also means less competitive tension than in a multi-champion category.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
