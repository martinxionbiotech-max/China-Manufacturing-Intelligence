# Where Are Wind Turbines Made in China? Yancheng's Offshore Wind Base

Yancheng anchors China's offshore wind — 41.5% of Jiangsu's wind capacity and ~10% of global offshore
wind, with Goldwind's 800MW Dafeng base.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8502 (electric generating sets).
- **Anchor cluster:** Yancheng wind power (`cls-yancheng-wind-power`).
- **Model:** manufacturing + test-farm base.

## The cluster, in data

Yancheng holds 41.5% of Jiangsu's wind capacity and ~10% of global offshore wind, with Goldwind's 800MW
Dafeng base and a test farm of 98 turbines (13.6 MW class) [cls-yancheng-wind-power].

## Market data

**2024 cumulative grid-connected wind capacity: 521 GW (+18%); new installations 79.82 GW (~70% of
global)** [1]. 2024 wind generation was 991.6 TWh (+16%) at 95.9% utilization [1]. Offshore wind reached
41 GW cumulative, with 18–20 MW turbines installed and 26 MW prototypes built [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Wind turbines are a **resource-plus-manufacturing, scale-leaping industry** — China's ~70%
of global new installations makes it the price-setting node, and the 18–26 MW turbine leap signals a
technology cycle. Yancheng's test-farm infrastructure provides real-world validation that pure-
manufacturing clusters lack.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **offshore-wind turbines/systems → Yancheng (Goldwind)** and pair PV + wind
from one geography for integrated clean-energy capex. The ~10%-global figure is 2020-vintage (now
understated) — treat it as a floor, and factor the large-turbine shift into project economics.
