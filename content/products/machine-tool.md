# Where Are Machine Tools Made in China? The East-Zhejiang Cluster

China's machine-tool manufacturing anchors in East Zhejiang (Taizhou, Ningbo) — nearly 2,000 firms, RMB
280B output, ~1/5 of national.

## Product at a glance

- **HS codes:** 8458 (lathes), 8459 (milling/drilling/boring machines).
- **Anchor cluster:** East Zhejiang machine tools (`cls-ezd-machine-tools`).
- **Model:** self-grown, CNC-upgrading cluster.

## The cluster, in data

East Zhejiang (Taizhou + Ningbo) holds nearly 2,000 firms and RMB 280 billion output (~1/5 of national),
built from a private, self-grown base that is now upgrading toward CNC and precision tooling
[cls-ezd-machine-tools].

## Market data

China's **2023 CNC-machine-tool market was ~RMB 409 billion**, with the metal-cutting segment the
largest [1]. The structural story is the **CNC-ization rate** — the share of CNC (vs. conventional)
machine tools — which still lags advanced economies and defines the upgrade premium.

China is the **world's largest machine-tool exporter** — 2024 exports hit **US$21.7 billion (+4%)** and the CNC-ization rate climbed to **51%** [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Machine tools is a **self-grown, upgrade-driven cluster** — East Zhejiang's private base
provides volume and price, while the national story is the CNC-ization climb. That split (commodity
volume vs. CNC precision) is the key buyer distinction.

*Opinion:* For buyers, split **commodity/conventional machine tools → East Zhejiang** (price edge) from
**high-end CNC → the broader national chain (Shenyang, Dalian, Chongqing)**. Verify the CNC-ization rate
and precision tier — it is the real differentiator, not the base price.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 8458/8459** — lathes vs. milling; verify exact machine type.
- Commodity → [Taizhou](/cities/taizhou/) / [Ningbo](/cities/ningbo/); high-end CNC → national chain.
- **Caveat:** RMB 280B is East-Zhejiang cluster output; national total is larger.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, East Zhejiang is a self-grown, upgrade-driven cluster, so the buying decision is about the specific tolerance you need, not the region's average quality.*

It provides volume and price on commodity and conventional machine tools, while the national story is the CNC-ization climb. For commodity tools, East Zhejiang wins on price; for high-end CNC, you specify and verify per supplier.
