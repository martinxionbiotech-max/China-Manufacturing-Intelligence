# Where Is Furniture Made in China? Foshan's Lecong and Longjiang

Foshan's Shunde district hosts 30,000+ furniture firms and the 2-million-m² Lecong Furniture City —
China's furniture sourcing heartland.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 9403 (other furniture and parts).
- **Anchor cluster:** Foshan furniture cluster (`cls-foshan-furniture`).
- **Model:** market-plus-factory (Lecong showroom + 30,000+ makers).

## The cluster, in data

Foshan's Shunde district hosts 30,000+ furniture firms and the 2M m² Lecong Furniture City, the world's
largest furniture-market cluster [cls-foshan-furniture]. The Foshan-Dongguan pan-home cluster (RMB 1.5
trillion, 14 categories) formalizes the cross-category bundling.

## Market data

**2024 above-scale furniture revenue was RMB 677.15 billion (+0.4%) across 7,459 firms**; furniture and
parts exports were **US$67.88 billion (+5.8%)** [1]. China is the world's largest furniture producer
(~40% of global output) [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Furniture is a **market-plus-factory cluster** — Lecong's 2M m² showroom floor is the
discovery layer, with 30,000+ makers behind it. The flat domestic market (+0.4%) against faster exports
(+5.8%) signals a sector leaning on overseas demand and brand consolidation.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Foshan is the correct anchor for **multi-category furniture sourcing** (walk
Lecong). Expect commodity margins to stay thin; anchor on branded home-furnishing or custom/ODM rather
than commodity volume. Pair Foshan with Dongguan's world-class furniture events.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 9403** — "other furniture" is the broad catch-all; verify seat vs. storage subheadings.
- Multi-category → [Foshan](/cities/foshan/) (Lecong, Shunde); pan-home → pair with [Dongguan](/cities/dongguan/).
- **Caveat:** RMB 677.15B is above-scale revenue; total market is larger.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Foshan is the right anchor for multi-category furniture sourcing because of one asset — Lecong's showroom floor, where you can physically compare thousands of suppliers in one place.*

But the flat domestic market means commodity margins stay thin. The play is to walk Lecong for discovery, then anchor on branded home-furnishing suppliers rather than the anonymous factory long tail.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
