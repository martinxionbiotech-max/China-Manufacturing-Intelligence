# Where Is AI Voice Technology Made in China? Hefei's iFlytek Cluster

Hefei (合肥), Anhui, anchors China's intelligent-voice industry — iFlytek (科大讯飞), China Voice
Valley, and a national advanced manufacturing cluster.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8517 (telephone sets / apparatus for transmission/reception of voice).
- **Anchor cluster:** Hefei intelligent voice cluster (`cls-hefei-intelligent-voice`).
- **Model:** single-champion (iFlytek) + national voice-industry designation.

## The cluster, in data

Hefei entered the national advanced-manufacturing list in 2021 (China's first voice-AI cluster), with
iFlytek as anchor and a 1,000+ firm ecosystem in China Voice Valley [cls-hefei-intelligent-voice].
iFlytek's H1-2024 revenue was RMB 9.325 billion (+18.91%).

## Market data

China's **core AI-industry scale reached ~RMB 700 billion (2024)**, heading toward RMB 800 billion by
2025 [1]. Hefei is a core node of the "national AI innovation pilot zone" and the voice/AI layer of the
larger AI industry.

The dedicated intelligent-voice market was **RMB 34.1 billion (2022)**, forecast to reach **RMB 46.9 billion by 2024** [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Intelligent voice is a **champion-anchored, policy-designated industry** — one firm
(iFlytek) plus a national cluster designation concentrated the entire voice-AI chain in a single city.
That produces deep R&D depth but a thinner competitive field than consumer-electronics clusters.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Hefei (iFlytek) is the correct anchor for **speech recognition, voice synthesis,
and voice-AI platform licensing**, but note the concentrated structure — for hardware *devices*
(speakers, terminals) the ecosystem is broader. Treat Hefei as the software/IP layer, not the hardware
volume layer.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 8517** — voice apparatus sits in a broad telecom-code; verify subheading.
- Voice-AI software/IP → [Hefei](/cities/hefei/) (iFlytek); hardware devices → broader ecosystem.
- **Caveat:** RMB 700B is the core-AI-industry figure, not voice-AI alone.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, intelligent voice is a champion-anchored, policy-designated industry — one firm plus one national cluster concentrated the entire chain in Hefei.*

That concentration is great for speech recognition, synthesis, and platform licensing (iFlytek), but it also means less competitive tension. For voice-AI you anchor in Hefei; for the hardware that runs it, Shenzhen is the integration node.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
