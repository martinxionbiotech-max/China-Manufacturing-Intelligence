# Where Are EVs Made in China? Shanghai (Tesla) and Changchun (FAW)

China's EV manufacturing anchors on Tesla's Shanghai Gigafactory (RMB 230B output) and Changchun's
FAW-Red Flag pivot — the world's largest NEV producer.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8703 (motor cars and other motor vehicles for transporting persons).
- **Anchor clusters:** Shanghai NEV (`cls-shanghai-nev`), Changchun auto (`cls-changchun-auto`).
- **Model:** EV-first (Shanghai/Tesla) + legacy-OEM pivot (Changchun/FAW).

## The cluster, in data

Tesla's Shanghai Gigafactory produces ~950,000 vehicles/year at RMB 230 billion output, with ~95%
localization and the 2026 Megapack energy-storage factory [cls-shanghai-nev]. FAW's Hongqi (Red Flag)
is its NEV pivot line, alongside FAW's joint-venture brands in Changchun.

## Market data

**2024 China NEV production and sales hit 12.886 million and 12.87 million (+34.4%/35.5%), with
penetration past 40%** [1][2]. China leads the world in NEV output and battery deployment, with BYD
(Shenzhen), Tesla (Shanghai), and FAW (Changchun) as the three production poles.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* EVs are a **three-pole, penetration-driven industry** — the story is 40%+ penetration, not
just volume growth. The poles diverge structurally: BYD (Shenzhen) is vertically integrated, Tesla
(Shanghai) is a high-localization foreign anchor, and FAW (Changchun) is a legacy-OEM pivot.

*Opinion:* For buyers, split **NEV platforms and vertical integration → [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/)
(BYD)**; **foreign-brand EV and energy-storage → [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/) (Tesla)**; **legacy-OEM
NEV/ICE transition → [Changchun](/cities/changchun/) (FAW)**. Match the supply chain to the platform.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 8703** — passenger vehicles; NEV vs. ICE share the same code.
- BYD → [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/); Tesla/energy storage → [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/); FAW → [Changchun](/cities/changchun/).
- **Caveat:** 40% penetration is the NEV share of total passenger-vehicle sales.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, EVs are a three-pole, penetration-driven industry, and the poles diverge structurally, not just geographically.*

BYD in Shenzhen is vertically integrated, Shanghai is the foreign-brand and energy-storage pole, and Changchun is the near-site FAW ecosystem. Route by what you actually need — platform, export brand, or domestic cost structure.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
