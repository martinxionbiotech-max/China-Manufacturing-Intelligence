# Where Are Smartphones Made in China? Dongguan and Shenzhen

One in five smartphones worldwide ships from Dongguan, China — home to OPPO, vivo, and Huawei Terminal —
with Shenzhen contributing one in seven.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8517 (telephone sets incl. smartphones).
- **Anchor clusters:** Dongguan smart terminal (`cls-dongguan-smart-terminal`), Shenzhen electronics
  (`cls-shenzhen-electronics`).
- **Model:** brand-anchored final assembly + components.

## The cluster, in data

Dongguan ships ~1 in 5 smartphones globally (OPPO, vivo, Huawei Terminal); Shenzhen adds ~1 in 7, and
Zhengzhou (Foxconn) completes the iPhone assembly pole [cls-dongguan-smart-terminal].

## Market data

China's **2024 mobile-phone output was ~1.67 billion units** [1]. The smartphone supply chain is a
three-pole assembly system — Dongguan (Android brands), Shenzhen (design/components), Zhengzhou (iPhone)
— over a Pearl River Delta components base.

Jan–Oct 2024 output was **1.339 billion phones** (990 million smartphones), with **1.089 billion exported — 81.3% of output** [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Smartphones are a **brand-anchored assembly industry** — the "1 in 5 / 1 in 7" figures are
assembly-share metrics, distinct from the components/design layer in Shenzhen and the materials layer in
the Delta. The three-pole structure (Dongguan/Shenzhen/Zhengzhou) is the manufacturing backbone.

*Opinion:* For buyers, split **final assembly/ODM → Dongguan** (Android) and Zhengzhou (iPhone) from
**components/design → Shenzhen**. Match the tier: brand-owned lines have different MOQs and quality bars
than ODM contract lines.

- **Caveat:** 1.67B units is national mobile-phone output, not smartphones alone.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, smartphones are a brand-anchored assembly industry, and the "1 in 5 / 1 in 7" figures are assembly-share metrics — distinct from the components-and-design layer in Shenzhen.*

For final assembly and ODM, Dongguan (Android) and Zhengzhou (iPhone) are the anchors. For components and design, go upstream to Shenzhen. Do not conflate assembly share with component capability.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
