# Where Are Hardware and Tools Made in China? Yongkang

Yongkang (永康), Zhejiang, makes 7 of every 10 Chinese doors, half the world's power tools, and 6 of
10 thermos cups — anchored by China's largest hardware market.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8205 (hand tools, not elsewhere specified).
- **Anchor cluster:** Yongkang hardware cluster (`cls-yongkang-hardware`).
- **Model:** category monopoly + one 50-year power-tool chain.

## The cluster, in data

Yongkang: 7 of every 10 Chinese doors, 5 of every 10 global power tools, 6 of every 10 thermos cups;
RMB 50B+ output, 90% of Yongkang's industrial output [cls-yongkang-hardware]. Its power-tool chain is
50 years old (China's first angle grinder, 1974) with 70%+ local parts rate.

## Market data

China's **2024 hardware-tools market was RMB 103.96 billion** [1]. Yongkang's power-tool chain spans
448 above-scale firms and RMB 47.76 billion output (2023, +5.9%) [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Yongkang is a **category monopoly, not a brand monopoly** — it dominates hardware through
enterprise density and a 50-year, 70%-local-parts power-tool chain, not a single champion. This keeps
pricing competitive but quality variable across the tail.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **power tools and doors → Yongkang** (per-factory safety certs required),
and **kitchen cutlery → [Yangjiang](/cities/yangjiang/)** (standards-led). Match the specialist to the
category rather than seeking "the" dominant brand.

That means power tools and doors route to Yongkang, but with per-factory safety certification as a hard gate, and kitchen cutlery routes to Yangjiang, where standards leadership matters more.
