# Where Are Biopharmaceuticals Made in China? Shanghai and Suzhou

China's biopharma innovation anchors on Shanghai Zhangjiang (1 in 5 approved new drugs) and Suzhou
Industrial Park (2,000+ firms) — the discovery-and-scale-up pair.

## Product at a glance

- **HS codes:** 3002 (blood/immune/biological products), 3004 (medicaments, dosed).
- **Anchor clusters:** Zhangjiang biopharma (`cls-zhangjiang-biomed`), Suzhou biopharma
  (`cls-suzhou-biomed`).
- **Model:** discovery (Shanghai) + scale-up/CDMO (Suzhou).

## The cluster, in data

Zhangjiang holds 20+ Class-1 new drugs (1/5 national) and 47% of 2024 national license-out
[cls-zhangjiang-biomed]. Suzhou Industrial Park hosts 2,000+ biomed/health firms with RMB 165.5 billion
output, #2 national competitiveness [cls-suzhou-biomed].

## Market data

China's biopharma market reached **RMB 1.9548 trillion (2023)**, with the biological-drug segment
estimated past RMB 700 billion in 2024 [1][2]. **Hengrui (恒瑞医药) posted RMB 25.01 billion in 2024
pharmaceutical-manufacturing revenue** [2]. The segment mix is shifting: chemical drugs ~47%, biologics
~28%, TCM ~25% [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Biopharma is a **discovery-and-scale-up pair** — Shanghai discovers and licenses out; Suzhou
develops and manufactures (CDMO). The chemical-drug 47% share shows China's biopharma is still largely a
*generic-plus-innovation* market, not yet pure innovation.

*Opinion:* For buyers, split **licensing/innovation → Shanghai Zhangjiang** and **CDMO/biologics
scale-up → Suzhou**. Regulatory load (NMPA + CE/FDA) is the real cost driver — factor certification
lead time, not just unit price.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 3002/3004** — biologics vs. dosed medicaments sit in adjacent codes; verify.
- Discovery/licensing → [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/); scale-up → [Suzhou](/cities/suzhou/).
- **Caveat:** RMB 1.9548T is the broad biopharma-industry figure; the narrower biologics segment is ~RMB 700B.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, biopharma is a discovery-and-scale-up pair, and you should route by that split rather than treating Shanghai and Suzhou as competitors.*

Shanghai discovers and licenses out; Suzhou develops and manufactures at CDMO scale. But the real cost driver is regulatory load — NMPA plus CE/FDA — which you should price in before anything else.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
