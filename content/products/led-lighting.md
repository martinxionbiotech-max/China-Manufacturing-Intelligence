# Where Is LED Lighting Made in China? Guzhen, One Town, Half the World

Guzhen (古镇), a sub-50 km² town in Zhongshan, holds ~70% of China's domestic lighting market and ~50%
of the global market — the single densest LED lighting cluster on earth.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 9405 (lamps and lighting fittings).
- **Anchor cluster:** Guzhen lighting cluster (`cls-guzhen-lighting`).
- **Model:** extreme-density category monopoly.

## The cluster, in data

Guzhen packs ~70% of China's domestic lighting and ~50% of the global market into a sub-50 km² town —
the densest LED cluster on earth [cls-guzhen-lighting]. It anchors the full chain: chips, packaging,
drivers, fixtures, and finishes.

## Market data

China's **2024 LED lighting market was ~RMB 716.9 billion (+5.3%)**, with Guzhen/Zhongshan the
manufacturing core [1]. The upstream LED chip segment is a separate oligopoly (San'an, HC SemiTek), while
Guzhen owns the fixture-and-assembly layer.

2024 lighting-product exports totaled **US$56.3 billion (-3.3%)**, with LED lighting at **US$42.3 billion — 75% of exports** [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* LED lighting is a **density-driven category monopoly** — Guzhen's value is spatial density
(complete chain in one town), which produces unmatched speed and price on fixtures. The chip layer is a
separate, more concentrated supply chain (San'an), so "Guzhen" ≠ the whole lighting value chain.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Guzhen is the correct anchor for **fixtures and assembly** (price/speed edge), but
the chip and driver layer is a distinct oligopoly — source upstream separately if quality-tier matters.
For export fixtures, pair Guzhen's assembly with Shenzhen's component depth.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Guzhen's LED lighting is a density-driven category monopoly — its edge is spatial density that produces unmatched speed and price on fixtures.*

But the chip and driver layer is a distinct oligopoly, and that is where the technology actually lives. Anchor fixtures and assembly in Guzhen, and source the chip/driver upstream, separately.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
