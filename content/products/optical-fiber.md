# Where Is Optical Fiber Made in China? Wuhan's Optics Valley

Wuhan's Optics Valley (武汉光谷) makes the world's largest volume of optical fiber and cable — China's
first fiber was drawn here in 1976.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 9001 (optical fibers and bundles; cables of optical fibers).
- **Anchor cluster:** Wuhan Optics Valley (`cls-wuhan-optics-valley`).
- **Model:** full-chain optoelectronics cluster.

## The cluster, in data

Wuhan Optics Valley is China's optoelectronics cradle — home to the "first fiber" (1976), the world's
largest fiber-and-cable volume, and a full chain from preforms through fiber to cable
[cls-wuhan-optics-valley]. It anchors the optoelectronics super-sector alongside Shenzhen's components.

## Market data

China's **2024 optical-communication market was ~RMB 215.4 billion (+5.5%)**, driven by 5G, data-center,
and FTTH buildout [1]. Wuhan Optics Valley holds the preform-fiber-cable core; Shenzhen anchors the
optical components/modules layer.

National optical-cable output reached **269 million fiber-km (2024, down 18.2%)** as demand softened after the 5G buildout peak [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Optical fiber is a **full-chain, single-anchor industry** — Wuhan owns the preform-fiber-
cable vertical (the high-barrier core), while Shenzhen owns components and modules (the fast-moving
layer). The 1976 first-fiber lineage gave Wuhan a first-mover preform advantage that persists today.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **fiber preform/cable → Wuhan Optics Valley** and **optical components/
modules → [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/)**. Match the layer: the preform is the high-barrier, few-supplier
core; modules are the competitive, many-supplier layer.
