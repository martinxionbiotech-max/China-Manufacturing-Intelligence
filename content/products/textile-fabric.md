# Where Are Textile Fabrics Made in China? Keqiao and Hangzhou Bay

Keqiao (柯桥, Shaoxing) trades ~1/4 of the world's fabrics; Hangzhou Bay's textile cluster holds ~1/5 of
China's output.

## Product at a glance

- **HS codes:** 5208 (woven cotton fabrics), 5407 (woven synthetic-filament fabrics).
- **Anchor clusters:** Keqiao textile (`cls-keqiao-textile`), Hangzhou Bay textile
  (`cls-hangzhou-bay-textile`).
- **Model:** fabric-trading + garment-layer pair.

## The cluster, in data

Keqiao trades ~1/4 of the world's fabrics and ~40% of national dyeing (China Textile City)
[cls-keqiao-textile]. Hangzhou Bay holds ~1/5 of China's textile output [cls-hangzhou-bay-textile].
Together they anchor the fabric-and-garment layer of the 229-cluster textile system.

## Market data

2024 above-scale textile revenue +4% and profit +7.5%; capacity utilization at 78.5% (textiles) and
85. 4% (chemical fiber), both above the 75% national average [1]. The 229 clusters hold 302,000+ firms
and RMB 4.18 trillion output [1].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Textile fabrics are a **trading-plus-manufacturing layer** — Keqiao's ~1/4-of-world fabric
*volume* is a trading-and-dyeing metric (China Textile City), distinct from Hangzhou Bay's
*manufacturing* share. The high chemical-fiber utilization (85.4%) signals a feedstock-intensive,
healthy upstream.

*Opinion:* For buyers, split **raw/printed fabric and dyeing → Keqiao (Shaoxing)** from **garment-layer
production → Hangzhou Bay**. For finished home textiles (bedding), route to Nantong; for socks, Zhuji —
the layer determines the anchor.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 5208/5407** — cotton vs. synthetic-filament weaves; verify subheading.
- Raw/printed fabric and dyeing → [Shaoxing/Keqiao](/cities/shaoxing/); garments → [Hangzhou](/cities/hangzhou/) Bay.
- **Caveat:** ~1/4 of world fabrics is a trade-volume estimate, not manufacturing share.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, textile fabrics are a trading-plus-manufacturing layer, and you route by which layer of the textile stack you actually need.*

Raw and printed fabric plus dyeing route to Keqiao (Shaoxing); garment-layer production routes to Hangzhou Bay; finished home textiles route to Nantong. "Textile" is four different sourcing decisions wearing one name.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
