# Where Are Sneakers Made in China? Jinjiang, the Shoe Capital

Jinjiang (晋江), Fujian, packs 3,000+ shoe firms into Chendai town, producing 1 billion+ sneakers a
year — home to Anta, Xtep, and 361°.

## Product at a glance

- **HS codes:** 6402 (other footwear, rubber/plastic soles), 6403 (leather-upper footwear).
- **Anchor cluster:** Jinjiang footwear cluster (`cls-jinjiang-footwear`).
- **Model:** extreme density (3,000 firms) + brand tier (Anta/Xtep/361°).

## The cluster, in data

Chendai town hosts 3,000+ shoe firms producing 1 billion+ sports shoes a year [cls-jinjiang-footwear].
Quanzhou's 2024 textile-and-footwear output exceeded RMB 700 billion, with sports shoes at 40% of
national and 20% of global [cls-quanzhou-sports].

## Market data

China's **2023 sports-shoe-and-apparel market was RMB 492.6 billion**, with ~780 million pairs of
sports shoes sold [1]. The OEM layer is consolidating into professional groups — **Huali (华利) and
Shenzhou (申洲国际)** are the scaled contract-manufacturing leaders [1].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Footwear is a **density-plus-brand cluster** — Chendai's extreme spatial density provides
the commodity-OEM floor, while Anta/Xtep pull the value layer toward ODM and global-standard setting.
The Huali/Shenzhou rise signals the OEM middle is professionalizing.

*Opinion:* For buyers, match the supplier tier deliberately: **commodity-volume sneakers → Chendai's
3,000-firm base**; **branded/ODM → Anta/Xtep tier**; **scaled contract manufacturing → Huali/Shenzhou**.
The three tiers have different minimums, lead times, and quality bars.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 6402/6403** — rubber/plastic vs. leather uppers; verify.
- Commodity → Chendai; branded/ODM → Anta/Xtep; scaled OEM → Huali/Shenzhou.
- **Caveat:** 1B+ pairs and 40% national share are industry estimates.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, footwear is a density-plus-brand cluster, and the mistake is treating Chendai's commodity floor and the Anta/Xtep brand tier as one market.*

They are different supplier tiers with different pricing, quality, and lead-time profiles. Match deliberately: commodity volume to Chendai's 3,000-firm base, branded or ODM work to the Anta/Xtep tier.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
