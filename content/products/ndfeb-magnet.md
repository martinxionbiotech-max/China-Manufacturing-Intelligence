# Where Are NdFeB Magnets Made in China? Ningbo Fabrication, Baotou Raw Material

China's NdFeB permanent-magnet industry splits: Ningbo fabricates (40%+ of national output), Baotou
supplies the world's largest rare-earth mine.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8505 (electromagnets; permanent magnets and articles).
- **Anchor clusters:** Ningbo magnetic materials (`cls-ningbo-magnetic-materials`), Baotou rare earth
  (`cls-baotou-rare-earth`).
- **Model:** split value chain (raw → fabrication).

## The cluster, in data

Ningbo fabricates 40%+ of national NdFeB output (90%+ of national share in high-end rare-earth
permanent-magnet materials) [cls-ningbo-magnetic-materials]. Baotou's Bayan Obo supplies the light-rare-
earth feedstock (Nd, Pr); Ganzhou supplies the heavy rare earth (Dy, Tb) for high-temperature grades.

## Market data

China produced **270,000 tonnes of rare earth (2024) — ~70% of global** — with the 2024 *Rare Earth
Management Regulation* imposing export controls on 7 categories of medium/heavy rare earth [1]. This
directly shapes NdFeB supply: light-element feedstock (Baotou) is abundant, heavy-element feedstock
(Ganzhou) is export-controlled.

2024 rare-earth permanent-magnet exports were **58,100 tonnes** and NdFeB output reached **246,600 tonnes** (market RMB 72.1 billion) [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* NdFeB magnets are a **three-node value chain** — raw light rare earth (Baotou), raw heavy
rare earth (Ganzhou), finished magnets (Ningbo). The export controls on heavy rare earth (Dy/Tb) make
high-temperature magnet supply the strategic choke point, not the abundant light-element grades.

*Opinion:* For buyers, match the magnet grade to the node: **standard NdFeB → Baotou/Ningbo chain**;
**high-temperature (Dy/Tb) magnets → Ganzhou-heavy rare earth + Ningbo fabrication**. Verify
heavy-rare-earth content and export licensing for high-grade magnets.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 8505** — permanent magnets vs. electromagnets; verify subheading.
- Standard NdFeB → [Baotou](/cities/baotou/)/[Ningbo](/cities/ningbo/); high-temperature → [Ganzhou](/cities/ganzhou/) + [Ningbo](/cities/ningbo/).
- **Caveat:** 40%+ Ningbo share is an industry estimate; heavy-rare-earth export controls apply.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, NdFeB magnets are a three-node value chain, and matching the magnet grade to the node is the whole game.*

Raw light rare earth is Baotou, raw heavy rare earth is Ganzhou, finished magnets are Ningbo. For standard NdFeB, the Baotou-to-Ningbo chain works; for high-temperature grades, you depend on Ganzhou's dysprosium and terbium.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
