# Where Are Socks Made in China? Zhuji's Datang Town

One Zhejiang town — Datang, Zhuji (诸暨·大唐) — makes 25 billion pairs of socks a year, 70% of China's
and a third of the world's, with 194 digital workshops.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 6115 (pantyhose, tights, socks, hosiery).
- **Anchor cluster:** Zhuji socks (`cls-zhuji-socks`).
- **Model:** single-town category monopoly + digital-workshop upgrade.

## The cluster, in data

Datang's sock cluster makes 25 billion pairs/year (70% of China, ~1/3 of the world) with 194 digital
workshops [cls-zhuji-socks]. It is the extreme example of single-town category concentration.

## Market data

China's textiles-and-garments sector runs through 229 clusters (302,000+ firms, RMB 4.18 trillion),
with hosiery a specialized niche [1]. Zhuji's Datang owns the sock layer inside the larger textile
system — density unmatched at any scale.

The sector's output value topped **RMB 70 billion (2024)**, exported to 121 countries and regions [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Socks is a **single-town category monopoly** — Datang's 25-billion-pair scale (a third of
the world) is density-driven, with the 194 digital workshops signaling an automation upgrade, not a
commodity floor. It is the extreme end of China's town-anchored manufacturing.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Datang is the only meaningful anchor for **volume hosiery** — the density produces
unmatched price/speed. For *branded/design* socks, the value layer sits above the commodity base; pair
Datang's production with a separate design/brand partner.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Datang is a single-town category monopoly — for volume hosiery there is effectively no alternative anchor of equivalent scale, and the density produces unmatched price and speed.*

The 194 digital workshops signal it is automating to defend share, not resting on it. But for branded and design socks, the value layer sits upstream; Datang is the commodity floor, not the brand layer.
