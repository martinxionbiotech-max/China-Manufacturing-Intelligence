# Where Are Industrial Robots Made in China? Shenyang and SIASUN

Shenyang (沈阳), Liaoning, is China's robotics cradle — SIASUN (新松), born from the CAS institute that
built the country's first industrial robot.

## Product at a glance

- **HS codes:** 8428 (lifting/handling machinery), 8479 (machines with individual functions).
- **Anchor cluster:** Shenyang robotics cluster (`cls-shenyang-robotics`).
- **Model:** technology-origin anchor + national deployment.

## The cluster, in data

SIASUN pioneered China's first industrial robot in 1982 (with CAS Shenyang); it later absorbed 7
academician-led research teams. The base also hosts the National Robot Innovation Center and the Robot
Industry Institute [cls-shenyang-robotics].

## Market data

**2024 industrial-robot sales: 302,000 units — #1 globally for 12 straight years**; China's robot
patent applications = 2/3 of the global total [1]. The 2024 market was ~RMB 58 billion, with Shenzhen
alone producing 133,900 industrial + 7.11 million service robots [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Industrial robots is a **technology-origin + volume-distributed industry** — Shenyang owns
the standards and lineage (SIASUN), while Shenzhen owns volume and price (133,900 units). The four
sub-arenas (industrial/service/public/specialty) are diverging supply chains, not one market.

*Opinion:* For buyers, split **robot arms → Shenyang (SIASUN)** from **system integration/AI deployment
→ [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/) / [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/)**. Evaluate "robot + AI" (embodied
intelligence) separately from classic arm hardware — they are diverging chains.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 8428/8479** — industrial robots classify across multiple codes; verify exact function.
- Robot arms → [Shenyang](/cities/shenyang/) (SIASUN); integration → [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/) / [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/).
- **Caveat:** 302K-unit figure is annual sales; installed base is larger.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, industrial robots are a technology-origin plus volume-distributed industry, so you must separate the arm from the deployment.*

Shenyang (SIASUN) owns the standards and lineage for robot arms; Shenzhen and Shanghai own volume, integration, and AI deployment. Evaluate whether a supplier is a traditional-arm maker or an embodied-intelligence integrator — they are different bets.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
