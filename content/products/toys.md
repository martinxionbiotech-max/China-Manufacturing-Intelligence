# Where Are Toys Made in China? Chenghai, a Third of the World's Plastic Toys

Shantou's Chenghai district holds 33% of global plastic-toy capacity — 50,000+ firms and #1 in toy
brands, IP licenses, and patents.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 9503 (toys, incl. dolls, wheeled toys, puzzles).
- **Anchor cluster:** Chenghai toys (`cls-chenghai-toys`).
- **Model:** commodity volume + IP upgrade.

## The cluster, in data

Chenghai holds 33% of global plastic-toy capacity, 50,000+ firms, and #1 in toy brands, IP licenses,
and patents [cls-chenghai-toys]. Dongguan adds ~85% of China's art toys; Quanzhou the sports-products
layer.

## Market data

**2024 trend-and-collectible toys retail: RMB 55.83 billion** — the sector's new growth engine [1].
Global AI-toy market forecast >US$35 billion by 2030 [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Toys is a **volume-to-IP shift industry** — Chenghai's 33%-of-global commodity plastic-toy
base is being layered with Dongguan's art-toy (~85% national) and a rising AI-toy segment. Value is
migrating from molded plastic to IP, collectibility, and embedded intelligence.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **commodity plastic toys → Chenghai**; **art/trend toys → [Dongguan](/cities/dongguan/)**;
**sports products → [Quanzhou](/cities/quanzhou/)**. The IP-and-AI segments carry the margin — source the
IP/AI layer, not just the commodity mold.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, toys are a volume-to-IP shift industry, and the two ends of the market are different cities.*

Commodity plastic toys route to Chenghai (a third of the world), while art and trend toys route to Dongguan, with a rising AI-toy layer on top. Match the product tier — commodity, IP, or AI — to the city.
