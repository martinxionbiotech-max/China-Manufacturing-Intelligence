# Where Is Low-Voltage Electrical Equipment Made in China? Yueqing

Yueqing (乐清), Wenzhou, is "中国电器之都" — China's electrical-apparatus capital — home to the
country's largest self-grown low-voltage electrical cluster.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8536 (electrical apparatus for switching/protecting circuits, ≤1,000V).
- **Anchor cluster:** Wenzhou electrical cluster (`cls-wenzhou-electrical`).
- **Model:** self-grown (草根) cluster + one champion (Chint).

## The cluster, in data

Yueqing's cluster entered MIIT's national advanced-manufacturing list in **2022** (one of 20, with
Zhejiang holding 5 seats) [cls-wenzhou-electrical]. It covers **200+ series, 6,000+ categories,
25,000+ models**, holding **65%+ national market share**. Chint Group (正泰) was founded in 1984 by Nan
Cunhui (南存辉) and reached **US$22.1 billion** revenue in 2024.

## Market data

China's low-voltage electrical market was an estimated **RMB 84.7 billion (2023)** [1]. The high-voltage
transmission layer (Baoding, Weifang) and the low-voltage distribution layer (Yueqing) are distinct
sub-chains — Yueqing owns the low-voltage/≤1,000V segment.

The market is forecast to **exceed RMB 85.4 billion (2024)**, with connectors the top export category at 44.9% of export value [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Yueqing is a **self-grown cluster with a champion tier** — it emerged from township
enterprises and traders (not a state anchor), which explains both its extreme category breadth
(25,000+ models) and its "one champion + long tail" structure. The 65% share is held *collectively*
across thousands of firms, not by Chint alone.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Yueqing is the correct anchor for **low-voltage breakers, contactors, relays,
and switchgear**, complementing Baoding's high-voltage transmission pole. But certification tiers vary
sharply across the long tail: Chint-tier suppliers carry full international certs; the tail does not.
Verify per-model CCC/CE/UL, and treat "65% share" as an industry estimate.
