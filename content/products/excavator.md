# Where Are Excavators Made in China? Changsha vs. Xuzhou

China's excavator manufacturing splits between Changsha (Sany, Zoomlion — 5 global top-50 firms) and
Xuzhou (XCMG) — the two poles of the world's largest construction-machinery base.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8429 (self-propelled earthmoving/road machinery).
- **Anchor clusters:** Changsha (`cls-changsha-construction-machinery`) and Xuzhou
  (`cls-xuzhou-construction-machinery`).
- **Model:** multi-brand competition (Changsha) vs. single-champion lineage (Xuzhou).

## The cluster, in data

Changsha holds five global top-50 firms (Sany, Zoomlion, Sunward, CRCHI, Sinoboom) and 70% of national
product variety [cls-changsha-construction-machinery]. Xuzhou is XCMG's home — XCMG's excavators are
#6 globally and #2 domestically, with loaders #1 domestically and #1 globally in new-energy loaders
[1].

## Market data

XCMG leads 16 machine categories domestically (truck cranes, rollers) and holds global #1 in cranes,
mobile cranes, and horizontal directional drills [1]. The industry is cyclical: national revenue fell to
RMB 797.7 billion (2022, -12%) before recovering, targeting a RMB 1-trillion market [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Excavators sit inside a **dual-pole oligopoly** — Changsha's five-firm competitive
structure (variety) versus Xuzhou's single-champion lineage (integration). XCMG's #6-global excavator
ranking shows a scale-and-breadth strategy, while Changsha's 70%-variety share shows a competitive-
ecosystem strategy.

*Opinion:* For buyers, split **multi-vendor leverage and niche specs → Changsha** from **single-account
scale and full lineage → Xuzhou**. The equipment-renewal policy is a demand tailwind — expect
competitive excavator and crane pricing given the multi-brand structure.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, excavators sit inside a dual-pole oligopoly, and the choice between Changsha and Xuzhou is variety versus integration — two different buying strategies, not two interchangeable regions.*

Go to Changsha for multi-vendor negotiation leverage and niche specs; go to Xuzhou for single-account scale and a full machine lineage. Match the strategy to the purchase, not the other way around.
