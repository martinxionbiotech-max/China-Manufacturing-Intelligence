# Where Is Home Textile Made in China? Nantong

Nantong (南通), Jiangsu, anchors China's home-textile industry — bedding and linens — through the
Suzhou-Wuxi-Nantong (SWT) textile cluster.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 6302 (bed linen, table linen, toilet linen, kitchen linen).
- **Anchor cluster:** SWT textile cluster (`cls-swt-textile`).
- **Model:** market-anchored (two giant trading towns) + fast small-order response.

## The cluster, in data

Nantong's home-textile industry passed **RMB 240 billion** in 2024, with high-end textiles one of
Nantong's six RMB-100-billion clusters [cls-swt-textile]. It is anchored on two markets — the Haimen
Diegeng Bridge market (叠石桥) and the Tongzhou Zhihao market. The **Diegeng Bridge International
Home-Textile City broke RMB 150 billion** in 2024 turnover.

## Market data

China's textiles-and-garments sector runs through **229 clusters across 27 provinces, holding 302,000+
firms and RMB 4.18 trillion output** [1]. 2024 above-scale textile revenue grew +4% and profit +7.5%,
with capacity utilization at 78.5% (textiles) and 85.4% (chemical fiber) — both above the 75% national
average [1].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Nantong home textiles are **market-anchored, not factory-anchored** — the two trading towns
are the cluster's gravitational center, with thousands of factories orbiting them. That market-first
structure produces a distinctive capability: **small-order, fast-response** bedding, ideal for
e-commerce and rapid SKU rotation.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Nantong is the correct anchor for **bedding and linens**, especially where the
need is small batches and fast iteration (the Diegeng Bridge RMB 150B turnover is the liquidity signal).
For commodity *fabric* rather than finished bedding, route to Keqiao instead. The RMB 240B figure is
cluster-wide (includes fabric upstream), not finished-goods-only.
