# Where Is Power Transmission Equipment Made in China? Baoding

Baoding (保定), Hebei, anchors North China's power-equipment manufacturing — 6,300+ firms, RMB 166
billion output, and a wind-solar-hydrogen-storage-transmission chain.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8504 (electrical transformers, static converters, inductors).
- **Anchor clusters:** Baoding power equipment (`cls-baoding-power-equipment`), Weifang power equipment
  (`cls-weifang-power-equipment`).
- **Model:** state-anchored transmission-equipment pole.

## The cluster, in data

Baoding entered the national advanced-manufacturing list in 2023 (Hebei's first), with 6,300+ firms and
RMB 166 billion output [cls-baoding-power-equipment]. The chain spans wind, solar, hydrogen, storage,
and transmission — a full "new-power-system" stack. Baoding Tianwei Baobian (保变电气) is a flagship.

## Market data

Baobian Electric posted **RMB 3.173 billion in 2024 revenue (+8.03%)** as a flagship transformer maker
[1]. The ultra-high-voltage (UHV) transmission layer is the strategic core, complementing the
low-voltage distribution layer (Yueqing).

China is now the **world's largest transformer producer**, with 2025 transformer exports reaching **RMB 64.6 billion (+36%)** [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Power equipment is a **state-anchored, transmission-layer industry** — Baoding owns the
high-voltage/UHV transformer-and-transmission stack (a policy-priority, few-supplier layer), distinct
from Yueqing's low-voltage distribution. The "new-power-system" framing (wind/solar/hydrogen/storage)
makes it the grid-transition supply node.

*Opinion:* For buyers, split **high-voltage/UHV transmission equipment → Baoding (Baobian)** from
**low-voltage distribution → [Yueqing](/cities/jinhua/)**. The transmission layer has fewer suppliers and
higher barriers — plan lead times and certification accordingly.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 8504** — transformers vs. converters; verify subheading and voltage class.
- HV/UHV transmission → [Baoding](/cities/baoding/); low-voltage → [Yueqing](/cities/jinhua/).
- **Caveat:** RMB 166B is Baoding cluster-wide; RMB 3.173B is Baobian single-firm revenue.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, power transmission equipment is a state-anchored, transmission-layer industry, and "power equipment" is too broad a label to source against.*

Baoding owns the high-voltage/UHV transformer-and-transmission stack, a policy-priority, few-supplier segment. For high-voltage and UHV, Baoding is the anchor; low-voltage is a different city, and generation is a third.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
