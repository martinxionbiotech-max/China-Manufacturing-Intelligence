# Where Is Leather Made in China? Haining

Haining (海宁), Zhejiang, anchors China's leather-and-fur industry — the Haining Leather City and a
high-end autumn-winter fashion supply chain.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 4203 (articles of apparel/clothing accessories of leather).
- **Anchor cluster:** Haining leather cluster (`cls-haining-leather`).
- **Model:** trade-city anchor + fashion-design upgrade.

## The cluster, in data

Haining's leather industry was built around the Haining Leather City trade hub; it now covers garment
leather, sofa leather, and automotive leather. The city holds Zhejiang's leather-and-fur manufacturing
base designation [cls-haining-leather].

## Market data

China's **2024 above-scale leather, fur, and feather-products revenue was RMB 853.32 billion (+3.7%)**,
with apparel and shoe upper leather the largest segments [1]. The leather industry is mature and
consolidating, with design/ODM the rising value layer.

2024 export delivery value reached **RMB 197.51 billion (-0.5%)**, with China the world's largest leather-goods producer and exporter [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Haining leather is **trade-city-anchored, fashion-upgrading** — the market (Leather City)
was the original gravitational center; the cluster is now climbing from commodity leather toward
design-led autumn-winter fashion. This mirrors the broader leather sector's shift from tanning volume to
branded apparel.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Haining is the correct anchor for **leather garments and high-end autumn-winter
fashion**, but match the tier: the market floor is commodity; the design/ODM tier carries the margin.
For sofa/automotive leather, confirm the specialized sub-cluster separately.

## Buyer notes

- **HS 4203** — leather apparel vs. other leather articles; verify subheading.
- Leather garments/fashion → [Haining](/cities/haining/); sofa/automotive → verify separately.
- **Caveat:** RMB 853.32B is the national leather-and-fur revenue, not Haining alone.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Haining leather is a trade-city-anchored, fashion-upgrading chain, and the tier you buy from matters more than the city itself.*

The market floor is commodity; the design-and-ODM tier is the value layer. Haining is the right anchor for finished leather garments and autumn-winter fashion, but commodity hides and mass garment blanks are not its strength.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
