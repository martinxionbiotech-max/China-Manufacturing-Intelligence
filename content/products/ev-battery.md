# Where Are EV Batteries Made in China? Ningde and CATL

CATL's Ningde base anchors China's EV-battery manufacturing — 39.2% global power-battery share and the
densest battery concentration on earth.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8507 (electric accumulators, incl. lithium-ion).
- **Anchor cluster:** Ningde power battery cluster (`cls-ningde-power-battery`).
- **Model:** champion-anchored ecosystem (CATL + 90+ firms).

## The cluster, in data

CATL held 39.2% global power-battery share in 2025 (#1 for 9 straight years) and 30.4% of global
energy-storage shipments (#1 for 5 years) [cls-ningde-power-battery]. Its 2025 revenue was RMB 423.7
billion (+17%), with 772 GWh global capacity.

## Market data

**2024 China installations hit 548.4 GWh (+41.5%), 61.3% of global**; cumulative output was 1,096.8 GWh
(+41.0%) [1][2]. The market is a steep oligopoly: **CATL 46.4% + BYD 25.1% = 71.5%**, top-10 at 97.1%
[2]. LFP dominates, with CATL/BYD each shipping 100+ GWh of LFP in 2024.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* EV batteries is the **most concentrated industry in this dataset** — effectively two volume
suppliers (CATL, BYD). This is genuine concentration risk for buyers. The 61.3% global share makes China
the price-setting node for world battery supply.

*Opinion:* For buyers, CATL is unavoidable for high-performance cells, but the concentration argues for
**dual-sourcing** — anchor volume with CATL, qualify BYD or a second source (e.g., FAW-Fudi in
[Changchun](/cities/changchun/)). Lead times and allocation matter more here than anywhere else.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, EV batteries are the most concentrated industry in this dataset, and that concentration is a real risk you should hedge, not accept.*

CATL is effectively unavoidable for high-performance cells, but with two firms holding the bulk of supply, the prudent move is dual-sourcing — anchor volume with CATL and qualify BYD or a credible second source in parallel.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
