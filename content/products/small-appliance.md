# Where Are Small Appliances Made in China? Cixi

Cixi (慈溪), a top-6 county in Zhejiang, anchors China's small-appliance manufacturing — air fryers,
kettles, and the "Cixi smart appliances" consumer brand cluster.

## Product at a glance

- **HS code:** 8516 (electric water heaters, space heaters, hair dryers, cooking appliances).
- **Anchor cluster:** Cixi small appliances (`cls-cixi-small-appliances`).
- **Model:** niche-density county cluster.

## The cluster, in data

Cixi holds RMB 108.38 billion core revenue (2023); Yueda (悦达) alone produces 20,000 air fryers/day
[cls-cixi-small-appliances]. It anchors the "Cixi smart appliances" consumer-brand cluster, the niche
layer beneath Shunde (kitchen) and Qingdao (white goods).

## Market data

China's **2024 small-appliance market was ~RMB 110 billion, with kitchen small appliances at 56%** [1].
Cixi is the density-and-iteration node; Shunde and Qingdao own the larger white-goods categories.

Exports are the growth engine: **2024 export volume rose 18.3% and value 11.4%**, with vacuum cleaners the top category at US$6.53 billion [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Small appliances is a **niche-density layer** — Cixi's 20,000-air-fryers/day rhythm and RMB
108. 38B core revenue show a cluster optimized for fast SKU iteration and niche volume, beneath the
brand-scale (Qingdao) and manufacturing-depth (Shunde) tiers.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Cixi is the correct anchor for **niche kitchen/personal-care small appliances**
(air fryers, kettles, hair dryers) where fast iteration and price matter. For full-line white goods,
route to Qingdao/Shunde instead.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Cixi is the niche-density layer of small appliances — a cluster optimized for fast SKU iteration and price, not for brand equity.*

For air fryers, kettles, and hair dryers where speed and price matter, Cixi is the right anchor. For branded white goods, you are in the wrong city — that is Qingdao's or Shunde's layer.
