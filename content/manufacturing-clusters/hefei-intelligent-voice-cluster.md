# Hefei Intelligent Voice: iFLYTEK and the China Speech Valley

Hefei's intelligent-voice cluster is the twentieth formation mechanism in this project: a single
research source (the University of Science and Technology of China, USTC) coupled to a single industrial
anchor (iFLYTEK), building outward from speech technology into a full-stack AI hub.

## What the cluster is

Hefei's intelligent-voice cluster was named a **national advanced manufacturing cluster** in the first
half of 2025, gathering **iFlytek (科大讯飞)**, Zhongke Xingtai, and Huami (华米) [1]. The "China Voice
Valley" (中国声谷) posted **RMB 65 billion** output in 2018 with 430 resident firms; iFlytek's AI
headquarters campus — "iFlytek Town" (讯飞小镇) — opened in September 2024 and has drawn **1,000+
innovative firms** [1].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "single research source + single champion + park." Hefei couples USTC (the
research seed) to iFlytek (the industrial anchor), building outward from speech technology into a
full-stack AI hub. The "1,000-firm campus" is a *talent-and-ecosystem* signal, not a
manufacturing-volume signal: voice AI is software-heavy, so "made in Hefei" means *developed in Hefei*
more than *assembled in Hefei*.

## Data deep-dive

- **1,000+ firms** — iFlytek Town campus (opened Sept 2024) [1].

## Original analysis

*Opinion:* Hefei is the **Chinese-language voice-AI anchor** — its edge is Mandarin performance and a
ready ecosystem, not commodity hardware cost. For buyers, Hefei is the correct anchor for
**Chinese-language AI voice engines, speech recognition/synthesis, and voice-hardware modules**
(iFlytek's stack). The RMB 65B is 2018 vintage — treat as a floor, not current (the cluster only gained
national designation in 2025). The structural caveat is asymmetry: Western buyers seeking multilingual
or English-first voice AI should benchmark iFlytek's non-Chinese performance carefully — the cluster's
strength skews Mandarin.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Hefei is the Chinese-language voice-AI anchor, and its edge is Mandarin performance plus ecosystem, not commodity hardware cost.*

USTC seeded the research and iFlytek industrialized it, which is why Hefei wins on Chinese-language capability rather than on being the cheapest place to build a speaker. For Chinese voice and speech-AI sourcing, it is the right anchor; for low-cost commodity hardware, it is not the differentiator.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
