# Qingdao Appliances: Haier and Hisense's High-End Manufacturing Base

Qingdao is the thirty-second formation mechanism in this project — a dual-champion appliance cluster
anchored by Haier and Hisense on a high-end manufacturing path.

## What the cluster is

Qingdao's smart-appliance cluster is a **national advanced manufacturing cluster** anchored by **Haier
(海尔)** and **Hisense (海信)** [1]. In the September 2024 China Top-500 list, of the 8 listed appliance
firms, Qingdao held 2 — Haier at #74 and Hisense at #135, ranking #2 and #4 among appliance firms
respectively [2]. **Haier became Qingdao's first RMB 400-billion-revenue firm in 2024** [2][3].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "dual champion + high-end manufacturing." Qingdao is China's *branded
smart-appliance* pole — distinct from Shunde's *volume + component* pole and Cixi's *export small-
appliance* pole. Its champions (Haier, Hisense) compete on brand, smart-home integration, and high-end
manufacturing, not commodity volume.

## Data deep-dive

- **Haier #74 / Hisense #135** on the 2024 China Top-500 (appliance #2/#4) [2].
- **Haier: RMB 400 billion revenue (2024)** — Qingdao's first [2][3].
- **National advanced manufacturing cluster** designation [1].

## Original analysis

*Opinion:* Qingdao is the **brand-and-smart pole** of China's appliance trio — Shunde owns volume,
Cixi owns export speed, Qingdao owns branded smart appliances. For buyers, Qingdao is the correct
anchor for **branded/smart appliances** (refrigerators, TVs, smart-home systems), with Haier's RMB 400B
revenue as the scale signal. The cluster's differentiator is *brand equity + smart-home integration*,
not commodity cost — so it is the wrong anchor for white-label or ultra-low-cost goods. The high-end
manufacturing path is the forward signal: Qingdao is betting on premium, connected appliances.

## What it makes

## The honest caveats

## What this means for buyers

- **Branded/smart appliances** with high-end manufacturing.
- **Complement to Shunde (volume) and Cixi (export speed).**
- **Caveats:** brand concentration; wrong anchor for white-label goods.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Qingdao is the brand-and-smart pole of China's appliance triangle, and that is what you are paying for here.*

Shunde owns volume, Cixi owns export speed, and Qingdao owns branded smart appliances (Haier, Hisense). For a buyer who wants a branded, smart, high-end appliance line, Qingdao is the anchor; for pure ODM volume or fastest export turnaround, the other two cities fit better.
