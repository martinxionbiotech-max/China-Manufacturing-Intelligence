# Nantong Home Textiles: The Die-shi-qiao E-commerce Cluster

The Suzhou-Wuxi-Nantong textile belt is the twenty-ninth formation mechanism in this project — a
home-textile specialty cluster anchored by the Die-shi-qiao global hub and reshaped by live-stream
e-commerce.

## What the cluster is

Nantong's home-textile industry passed **RMB 240 billion** in 2024, with high-end textiles one of
Nantong's six RMB-100-billion clusters [2]. It is anchored on two markets — the **Haimen Diegeng Bridge
market (叠石桥)** and the Tongzhou Zhihao market — with the **Diegeng Bridge International Home-Textile
City breaking RMB 150 billion** in 2024 turnover [3]. The cluster's 2024 target was RMB 220 billion in
above-scale textile output under a "three-major, three-support" pattern [2]. Live-stream e-commerce is
reshaping the supply chain [3].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "market-anchored + e-commerce reshaped." Nantong home textiles are
*market-first, factory-second* — the two trading towns are the gravitational center, with thousands of
factories orbiting them. That structure produces a distinctive capability: **small-order, fast-response**
bedding, ideal for e-commerce and rapid SKU rotation — now amplified by live-stream selling.

## Data deep-dive

- **RMB 240 billion (2024)** — home-textile industry scale [2].
- **RMB 150 billion (2024)** — Diegeng Bridge market turnover [3].
- **Six RMB-100B clusters** — high-end textiles is one [2].

## Original analysis

*Opinion:* Nantong is the **bedding-and-linens anchor**, distinct from Keqiao's commodity *fabric*
anchor — the two serve different buyers. The RMB 150B Diegeng Bridge turnover is the *liquidity* signal:
it means small orders clear fast, the defining edge for e-commerce-driven buyers. The RMB 240B figure is
cluster-wide (includes fabric upstream), not finished-goods-only — discount when sizing the finished-
bedding opportunity. The live-stream e-commerce layer is the structural new driver, shifting the
cluster's value from wholesale floors toward direct-to-consumer speed.

## What it makes

- **Home textiles** — bedding, linens.
- **Yarn and fabric** — the upstream layer.

## The honest caveats

- **Cluster-wide figure.** RMB 240B includes upstream fabric, not finished goods alone.
- **Market-anchored.** Value sits in the trading towns; factory depth varies.

## What this means for buyers

- **Bedding and linens** with small-order, fast-response capability.
- **Diegeng Bridge (RMB 150B)** = the liquidity/discovery floor.
- **Caveats:** cluster-wide scale; e-commerce-driven supply chains favor speed over depth.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Nantong is the bedding-and-linens anchor, distinct from Keqiao's commodity-fabric anchor — and the two serve different buyers.*

The RMB 150B Die-shi-qiao turnover is the liquidity signal, and live-stream e-commerce has reshaped the cluster's speed. For home textiles and bedding, Nantong is the anchor; for raw commodity fabric, Keqiao and Hangzhou Bay remain the floor.
