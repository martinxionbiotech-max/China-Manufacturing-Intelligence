# Cixi Small Appliances: Ningbo's ODM Home-Appliance Base

Cixi — a county-level city under Ningbo, Zhejiang, ranked sixth among China's top-100 counties — is the
seventeenth formation mechanism in this project [1]. It belongs to the same small-appliance family as
Shunde, but with a different center of gravity: where Shunde built brand chain leaders (Midea, Galanz),
Cixi built ODM/OEM export capacity across the full small-appliance catalog.

## What the cluster is

Cixi's modern-furniture-and-smart-appliance cluster core posted **RMB 108.38 billion** revenue in 2025,
with "Cixi smart appliances" named to the national consumer-brand list [1]. Its 2026 two-month appliance
exports hit **RMB 5.02 billion (+6.6%)** [1]. Cixi ranks **#6 among China's top-100 counties**, with
small appliances a core pillar; Yueda Electronics (悦达电子) ships **20,000 air fryers a day** with
industry-leading patent holdings [1].

## The velocity engine: Yueda as the model

Yueda Electronics is the cluster's demonstrative firm rather than a one-off. Beyond the 20,000-unit
air-fryer daily throughput, its product structure is widening toward **beauty devices and cooling
appliances**, with **200+ product projects running concurrently in 2026** [1]. This is the Cixi
pattern in miniature: an ODM engine that converts category trends (air fryers yesterday, beauty
devices today) into exportable SKUs within a season.

## Market context: a 500-billion small-appliance base in a price-for-volume phase

The national context explains Cixi's positioning. Industry reports put China's small-appliance
market above **RMB 500 billion in 2024** — a global production-and-export base [3]. The wider home-
appliance market was **RMB 881.1 billion in 2024 (+5.7%)**, with online channels contributing 52.9%
of retail [4]. On the export side, small appliances showed a clear **"price-for-volume" (以价换量)
pattern in 2025** — volumes up, unit values down across most categories (vacuum cleaners led at
US$7.1 billion, +8.8%) [5]. For an ODM-export cluster like Cixi, that price-for-volume phase is the
operating environment: throughput and cost control are the survival skills, which is precisely what
the county's factory base is built for.

## Ningbo context: the hundred-champion city

Cixi sits inside Ningbo's "361" industrial system — three world-class clusters, six high-energy
clusters, plus future tracks — and Ningbo is China's first city with **100+ national
single-champion firms** (104, adding 21 in 2024) [6]. Cixi's small-appliance base is one of the
county-level pillars inside that system; the city's above-scale AI manufacturing value-added grew
15.5% in 2024 [6]. The practical meaning: Cixi's ODM suppliers plug into a city-level ecosystem of
champion firms and digital-upgrade programs, not an isolated county industry.

*Inference:* the mechanism is "county-level ODM/OEM export velocity." Cixi's signature is *throughput*
(20,000 fryers/day from one firm; RMB 5B in two months), not champion brands or deep vertical
integration. Its "top-6 county" status is the structural reminder that Zhejiang's manufacturing
concentration often lives at the *county* level, not the city level.

- **Air fryers, kettles, kitchen and beauty appliances.**
- **ODM/OEM export capacity** across the small-appliance catalog.

## The export tide: small appliances are the volume engine

Cixi's export velocity rides a national wave. China's small-appliance exports grew **18.3% in
volume and 11.4% in value in 2024**, continuing a volume-plus-value uptrend that started in
August 2023 [7]. The full home-appliance category shipped **448.11 million units (+20.8%) worth
RMB 712.2 billion (+15.4%)** in 2024, with the electrical-machinery chamber forecasting total
appliance exports above US$120 billion for the year [8][9].

The structural point for Cixi: small appliances are the category's volume engine, and the
county's ODM base is positioned exactly on that engine. But the same data shows the
price-for-volume trade-off — value growth (11.4%) lagging volume growth (18.3%) in 2024 —
which is the margin pressure every Cixi ODM is managing by rotating into higher-value
categories (beauty, cooling) [1][7].

## The appliance trio compared: Cixi's seat at the table

Cixi's position is clearest when set against the two brand poles of China's appliance industry.
Qingdao is the corporate-champion pole: in the 2024 China Top-500 list, of the eight listed
appliance firms, Qingdao held two — Haier (#74) and Hisense (#135) — and Haier became the
city's first company to pass **RMB 400 billion** in annual revenue [10][11]. Shunde is the
volume-plus-brand pole (Midea, Galanz). Against those, Cixi's RMB 108.4 billion cluster core
and county-level ODM base are a different species entirely — no Fortune-500 anchor, but
export throughput measured in millions of units per month [1][10].

The trio comparison is the sourcing decision in one paragraph: **Qingdao for brand equity and
smart-appliance R&D, Shunde for brand-plus-volume white goods, Cixi for ODM/OEM export speed**
[10]. The three are complements, not substitutes — a buyer assembling an appliance program
often touches two of the three.

## The ODM relationship model: how Cixi procurement actually works

Cixi's county structure shapes the transaction form. Buyers rarely purchase branded goods
here; they commission ODM/OEM runs against a factory's existing platform [1]. Yueda's 200+
concurrent product projects are the model: a buyer arrives with a spec (or borrows one from
the factory's pipeline), the factory maps it onto its air-fryer/kettle/beauty-device
platform, and the run ships in weeks [1]. The trade-off is control: the speed comes from
reusing the platform, which caps true customization. For a buyer the rule is simple —
platform-fit products get Cixi's full speed advantage; bespoke products do not. This is
why Cixi procurement runs through long-term ODM relationships rather than spot brand
purchases.

## The honest caveats

- **County-level structure.** Manufacturing concentration lives at the county, not city level.
- **Firm-specific claims.** Patent holdings are firm-specific, not cluster-wide.
- **Price-for-volume exports.** 2025 export mix shows volumes up and unit values down — margin
  pressure is structural, not cyclical.
- **Report calibers.** National market-size figures are industry-research reports, not official
  statistics.

## What this means for buyers

- **Export-oriented small appliances** with fast turnaround.
- **ODM/OEM relationships** are the procurement norm.
- **Category-trend responsiveness** — beauty and cooling categories are being absorbed into the
  existing air-fryer/kettle base.
- **National export tailwind** — 2024 small-appliance exports +18.3% volume/+11.4% value [7].
- **Trio logic** — pair Cixi with Qingdao (brand/R&D) or Shunde (brand+volume) when the
  program needs more than throughput.
- **Platform-fit test** — products that fit an existing ODM platform get the full speed
  advantage; bespoke designs get only part of it [1].
- **Caveats:** treat patent claims as firm-specific; note county-level structure.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Cixi is the export-velocity pole of China's appliance triangle — it is not where you go for brand, it is where you go for speed.*

One firm pushing 20,000 fryers a day and RMB 5B in two months is a throughput signal, not a quality signal. Shunde owns volume-plus-brand, Qingdao owns branded smart appliances, and Cixi owns ODM/OEM export speed — so match your need to the right city.
