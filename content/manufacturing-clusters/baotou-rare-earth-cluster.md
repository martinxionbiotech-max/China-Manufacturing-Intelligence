# Baotou Rare Earth: The World's Largest Light Rare Earth Base

Baotou is the twenty-seventh formation mechanism in this project — a resource-endowment cluster built on
the world's largest light rare earth mine, anchored by a single global champion.

## What the cluster is

Baotou's **Bayan Obo (白云鄂博) mine is the world's largest rare-earth reserve**; 2024 rare-earth output
projected above **RMB 100 billion** [1][3]. **Northern Rare Earth (北方稀土)** is the global-scale
leader in raw material and functional materials, built on Bayan Obo's ore [2]. Baotou's 2024 five
strategic-emerging-industry (incl. rare earth) output plan targeted **RMB 110 billion+** [2].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "resource endowment + single champion." Baotou owns the *light* rare
earth fraction (cerium, lanthanum — the volume ore), the complement to Ganzhou's *heavy* rare earth
(ionic clay). The single-champion structure (Northern Rare Earth) is a central-SOE consolidation,
mirroring Ganzhou's China Rare Earth Group.

## Data deep-dive

- **Bayan Obo — world's largest rare-earth mine** [1].
- **RMB 100 billion+ (2024)** — projected rare-earth output [1][3].
- **Northern Rare Earth** — global-scale raw-material and functional-materials leader [2].

## Original analysis

*Opinion:* Baotou is the **light-rare-earth (volume) anchor**, complementary to Ganzhou's heavy-rare-
earth anchor — the two ore types, two cities structure. For buyers, Baotou is the **raw-material/oxide
node** (NdFeB magnet buyers ultimately depend on Baotou's light ore for bulk), while Ganzhou supplies
the heavy-fraction performance additives. This is a **quota-regulated market** — price and availability
track policy as much as geology. The RMB 100B+ figure is projected (2024), not audited — treat as
directional. The structural insight is that Baotou controls the *volume* of China's rare earth, the
strategic leverage that underpins the entire downstream magnet chain.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Baotou is the light-rare-earth volume anchor, and treating it as interchangeable with Ganzhou is the single most common sourcing mistake in rare earths.*

Baotou owns the cerium-lanthanum (volume ore) fraction from Bayan Obo, while Ganzhou owns the rarer heavy fraction. For raw light-rare-earth material and its downstream uses, Baotou is the anchor; for heavy rare earths and finished magnets, you look elsewhere.
