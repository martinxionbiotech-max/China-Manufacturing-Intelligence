# Zhuji Datang Socks: A Third of the World's Socks From One Town

Zhuji is the fifty-first formation mechanism in this project — a town-level cluster that produces a
third of the world's socks with a digitized manufacturing base.

## What the cluster is

Datang (大唐), a town in Zhuji, Zhejiang, produces **25 billion+ pairs of socks a year** — **~70% of
China's and one-third of global** production — at **RMB 70 billion+** output scale [1][2]. It runs **194
digital sock workshops and 20,000+ smart sock machines**, making it the world's largest digitally-
enabled sock production base [2]. The 18th China Datang International Sock Expo was held in September
2024, with Zhuji positioned as the world's largest sock cluster [3].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "category monopoly + digital modernization." Datang already held the
volume (70% of China), and the 194 digital workshops show it is *automating to defend* that share
against rising labor costs — the mature-stage move of a commodity cluster: lock in the category with
capital intensity, not just cheap labor.

## Data deep-dive

- **25 billion+ pairs/year** — ~70% of China, ~1/3 of global [1][2].
- **RMB 70 billion+** — output scale [1].
- **194 digital workshops / 20,000+ smart machines** — the automation moat [2].
