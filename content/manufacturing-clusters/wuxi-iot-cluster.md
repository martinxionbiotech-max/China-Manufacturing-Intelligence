# Wuxi IoT: China's Only National IoT Manufacturing Cluster

Wuxi's Internet of Things cluster is the twenty-second formation mechanism in this project — a
state-led cluster that is China's sole national advanced manufacturing cluster for IoT, and one that
holds standard-setting authority over the industry it anchors.

## What the cluster is

Wuxi's IoT cluster was named to the first batch of national advanced manufacturing clusters in 2021,
and remains the **only IoT national advanced manufacturing cluster in China** [1]. As of last year, its
**585 above-designated-size IoT enterprises recorded RMB 318.4 billion** in operating revenue, forming a
complete chain covering information sensing, transmission/networking, computing/storage, and application
processing [1]. Its focus has converged on a distinctive **"one sensing, two networks"** profile — smart
sensors, the connected-vehicle network (V2X), and the industrial internet [1]. Beyond production, Wuxi
holds standard-setting influence: CAS academician Yin Hao chairs both the MIIT IoT standardization
committee and the national IoT cluster's expert advisory committee [1].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "national strategy + full chain + standard-setting authority." Wuxi is not
only an industrial base but a source of standards — it occupies the upper layer of the IoT value chain,
distinct from clusters that compete on manufacturing cost alone.

## Data deep-dive
