# Suzhou Nano: A Full-Chain Nanotechnology Cluster

Suzhou's nanotechnology cluster is the twenty-first formation mechanism in this project — a "research
institute + national strategy + full industry chain" model that stands apart for its closed loop from raw
materials through equipment, process, and application.

## What the cluster is

Suzhou's nano cluster reached **RMB 146 billion** in 2022 — one of the **world's five major nano hubs** —
with nano-materials industrial output of RMB 82.2 billion [1]. It gathers **1,128 nano-related firms**
(570 core nano-materials firms, 116 above-scale industrial), with **28 CAS/foreign academicians and 83
national-talent-program experts** [1]. In 2022 the nano-materials industry was named a **MIIT national
advanced manufacturing cluster**, anchored by listed firms including Suda Weige, Nanda Optoelectronic,
Jingfang Semiconductor, and Jufeng New Materials [1].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "research institute + national strategy + full chain." Suzhou's nano
cluster stands apart for its *closed loop* — from raw materials through equipment, process, and
application — the full chain in one park. Its talent density (28 academicians, 83 national-talent
experts) is the knowledge signal; the "world's five major nano hubs" status is the global benchmark.

## Data deep-dive

- **1,128 firms** — 570 core, 116 above-scale [1].

- **MIIT national advanced cluster (2022)** [1].

## Original analysis

(raw-material-to-application), which few advanced-materials clusters achieve. For buyers, Suzhou is the
correct anchor for **nano-electronic materials, nano-bio materials, and micro-nano devices**, especially
where a complete-chain, R&D-dense supplier matters. The RMB 146B (2022) figure is now several years
old — treat as a baseline, not current. The talent density (28 academicians) signals deep R&D capability
but also a research-centric, not volume-centric, profile — size expectations accordingly.
