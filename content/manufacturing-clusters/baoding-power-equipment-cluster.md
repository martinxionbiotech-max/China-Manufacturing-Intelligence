# Baoding Power Equipment: North China's 'Electric Valley'

Baoding is the forty-seventh formation mechanism in this project — a "China Electric Valley" cluster
that is North China's largest power and new-energy equipment base.

## What the cluster is

Baoding's power-equipment cluster (core: Baoding High-tech Zone) gathers **6,300+ firms**, with **2024
cluster output of RMB 166 billion** — North China's largest power and new-energy high-end equipment
base [1]. Its spatial layout is "one core, six zones, three bases" along a
**"wind-solar-hydrogen-storage-transmission" (风光氢储输)** chain [2]. Target: **RMB 250 billion by
2025**, with 8 firms above RMB 10 billion revenue [3].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "high-voltage + new-energy chain." Baoding is China's *high-voltage
transmission* pole, complementing Wenzhou's low-voltage pole. The "风光氢储输" chain is the structural
story: it does not just make transformers; it integrates wind, solar, hydrogen, storage, and
transmission into one equipment ecosystem — positioning it for the energy-transition capex cycle, not
just grid maintenance.

## Data deep-dive

- **6,300+ firms / RMB 166 billion (2024)** — realized cluster output [1].
- **"One core, six zones, three bases"** — spatial architecture along the 风光氢储输 chain [2].
- **RMB 250 billion (2025 target)** — a forward-looking plan, with 8 firms >RMB 10B [3].

## Original analysis

*Opinion:* Baoding is a **high-voltage + new-energy chain cluster** — its differentiator is the
*wind-solar-hydrogen-storage-transmission* integration, which no single-category cluster matches. The
RMB 250B target vs. RMB 166B realized (2024) is a plan-vs-reality gap to discount. For buyers, Baoding
is the correct anchor for **high-voltage transformers and grid equipment**, while Weifang anchors
generation equipment — the two are complementary, not substitutes. The energy-transition capex cycle is
the demand driver; price against realized 2024 output, not the 2025 target.

## What it makes

- **Power transmission equipment** — high-voltage transformers, switchgear.
- **Wind-solar-hydrogen-storage equipment** — the integrated new-energy chain.

## The honest caveats

- **Target vs. realized.** RMB 250B (2025) is a target; RMB 166B (2024) is realized.
- **Government figures.** Output and firm counts come from government/media sources.

## What this means for buyers

- **High-voltage transformers and grid equipment** at North China's largest scale.
- **Integrated new-energy chain** (风光氢储输) is the differentiator.
- **Caveats:** discount the 2025 target; complement (not substitute) to Weifang generation equipment.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Baoding is the high-voltage + new-energy pole — and if you source a full electrical BOM you will almost certainly touch both Baoding and Wenzhou, because no single Chinese city owns the whole voltage spectrum.*

Its real differentiator is the wind-solar-hydrogen-storage-transmission chain: it is North China's largest power and new-energy equipment base, not just a transformer town. That makes it the right anchor for high-voltage transmission and grid-scale new-energy gear.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
