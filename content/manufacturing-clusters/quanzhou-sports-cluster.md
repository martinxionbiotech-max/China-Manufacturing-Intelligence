# Quanzhou Sports: The Brand Cluster Behind 20% of the World's Sneakers

Quanzhou is the thirty-first formation mechanism in this project — a private-champion brand cluster that
evolved from OEM contract work into global brands, now extending from footwear into the full
sports-product scene.

## What the cluster is

The Quanzhou modern sports-products cluster was named a **national advanced manufacturing cluster in
2024**, with leisure sports as its signature and textile-footwear as its core [1]. Quanzhou's
textile-footwear industry passed **RMB 700 billion** in 2024, with **athletic footwear at 40% of
China's and 20% of the world's output** [1]. In 2024, **ANTA (安踏) led the formation of a "sports-goods
industry innovation consortium"** uniting suppliers, universities, and R&D institutes to co-innovate and
help set global standards [1].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "private champion + brand cluster + OEM-to-brand ladder." Quanzhou evolved
from OEM contract work into global brands (ANTA, Xtep, 361°), then extended from footwear into the full
sports-product scene. The innovation-consortium move (ANTA-led) is the signal that the cluster is now
competing on *standards and innovation*, not just brand or volume.

## Data deep-dive

- **RMB 700 billion (2024)** — textile-footwear industry [1].
- **40% of China's / 20% of the world's athletic footwear** [1].
- **2024 national designation** — leisure sports signature [1].
- **ANTA-led innovation consortium** — suppliers + universities + R&D [1].

## Original analysis

*Opinion:* Quanzhou is the **brand-and-volume anchor** of China's sports products, distinct from
Jinjiang's pure manufacturing density (the two overlap — Jinjiang is a county within Quanzhou). For
buyers, Quanzhou is the correct anchor for **athletic footwear and sportswear at brand-and-OEM scale**.
The 20%-global footwear share is an output *volume* figure, not a revenue/value share — China's
dominance is volume-led, and value capture is more contested. The ANTA-led consortium is the forward
signal: the cluster is climbing from "made here" to "standards set here."

## What it makes

- **Athletic footwear** — 20% of global output.
- **Sportswear and sports equipment.**

## The honest caveats

- **Volume, not value.** 20% global is output share, not revenue share.
- **Overlap with Jinjiang.** Jinjiang is the manufacturing core within Quanzhou.

## What this means for buyers

- **Athletic footwear/sportswear** at brand-and-OEM scale.
- **Standards-and-innovation climb** (ANTA consortium) is the forward signal.
- **Caveats:** volume-led dominance; pair with Jinjiang for manufacturing depth.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Quanzhou is the brand-and-volume anchor of China's sports products, and its OEM-to-brand ladder is the mechanism that matters most.*

ANTA, Xtep, and 361° climbed from contract work to global brands, and Jinjiang (a county within Quanzhou) is the manufacturing density underneath. For brand-capable sportswear and footwear, Quanzhou is the anchor; note the overlap with Jinjiang rather than treating them as two markets.
