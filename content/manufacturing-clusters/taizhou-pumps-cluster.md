# Taizhou Pumps & Valves: A Category-Dense Pump Hub

Taizhou is the fiftieth formation mechanism in this project — a category-density cluster spanning
pumps, motors, and valves across its Wenling and Yuhuan cores.

## What the cluster is

Taizhou's pump-and-valve cluster splits across two county-level cores with distinct specialties:
**Wenling (温岭)** for pumps and motors, and **Yuhuan (玉环)** for precision valves.

Wenling's pump-and-motor industry is the city's single largest pillar. Its **small pumps hold 70%+
of national output**, with 2022 cluster output of **RMB 65 billion** (RMB 32.5 billion above-scale)
and a **16.3% average annual growth rate** over the prior three years — a growth signal, not a
mature-hold one [2]. Wenling hosts the China (Taizhou) Industrial Expo and Motor & Water Pump
Exhibition, the industry's regional trade floor. Yuhuan was named to Zhejiang's third-batch
provincial high-end-equipment "precision pump-valve rising-star" industry cluster [1], and a local
innovation center has strengthened engineer-team collaboration to meet Wenling's large pump-and-motor
demand [3].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "category density + pumps-and-motors + precision valves." Taizhou's
edge is density across two cores — Wenling for pumps and motors, Yuhuan for precision valves —
covering the full pump/motor/valve category range, with a precision-upgrade trajectory (e.g.,
lead-free angle valves). Unlike a single-champion cluster, no one firm dominates; the cluster's
strength is the breadth of the specialist tail.

## Data deep-dive

- **70%+ national small-pump share** — Wenling is the category floor for small pumps [2].
- **RMB 65 billion (2022)** — cluster output, with RMB 32.5 billion above-scale; a ~16.3% three-year
  CAGR shows share consolidation rather than stasis [2].
- **Two-core specialization** — Wenling (pumps/motors) vs. Yuhuan (precision valves) is a deliberate
  division of labor, not overlap.
