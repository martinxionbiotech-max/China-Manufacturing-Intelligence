# Chengdu-Chongqing Electronics: An Inland Trillion-Yuan Twin-City Cluster

Chengdu-Chongqing is the thirty-seventh formation mechanism in this project — a cross-provincial
twin-city cluster driven by national strategy, with electronics output above a tenth of China's total.

## What the cluster is

The Chengdu-Chongqing electronics cluster is a cross-provincial, national-strategy-driven twin-city
play. Chengdu's electronics industry reached **RMB 1.3 trillion** in 2023, with **RMB 792.6 billion in
the first 8 months of 2024 (+5.6%)** [1]. The two cities jointly build electronics among **four
trillion-yuan clusters**; together their auto output and electronics output value exceed **1/10 of
China's national total** [2]. In 2024 the Chengdu-Chongqing twin-city economic circle's industrial
added value was **RMB 2.42 trillion (+6.3%)** [2].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "national strategy + cross-provincial twin city." This is an
*inland-relocation* cluster — national policy pushed electronics capacity from coastal Guangdong/Jiangsu
into the Chengdu-Chongqing interior, creating a west-of-China electronics pole. It is the strategic
counterweight to the Pearl River Delta, not a market-emergent cluster.

## Data deep-dive

- **RMB 1.3 trillion (2023)** — Chengdu electronics scale [1].
- **RMB 792.6 billion (Jan–Aug 2024, +5.6%)** — growth signal [1].
- **>1/10 of national electronics output** — combined Chengdu-Chongqing share [2].
- **RMB 2.42 trillion (2024)** — twin-city industrial added value, +6.3% [2].

## Original analysis

*Opinion:* Chengdu-Chongqing is the **inland counterweight** to the coastal electronics clusters — its
significance is *strategic* (supply-chain diversification away from the coast) as much as commercial.
For buyers, it is the correct anchor for **inland electronics, ICs, and display panels** when
coastal-cluster concentration (Shenzhen/Dongguan) is a supply-chain risk to hedge. The ">1/10 national"
share is a meaningful but not dominant position — treat it as a *diversification* option, not a
replacement for the Pearl River Delta. The cross-provincial structure (Sichuan + Chongqing) adds
coordination complexity a buyer should not underestimate.

## What this means for buyers

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Chengdu-Chongqing is the inland counterweight to the coastal electronics clusters, and its significance is as much strategic as commercial.*

The trillion-yuan twin-city scale is real, but the strategic logic — supply-chain diversification away from the coast — is what a buyer should weigh. It is a serious second sourcing base for laptop and terminal assembly, not a replacement for Shenzhen's components and design density.
