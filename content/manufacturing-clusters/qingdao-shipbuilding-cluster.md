# Qingdao Shipbuilding: A Tri-City Marine Engineering Cluster

Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai is the forty-fourth formation mechanism in this project — a tri-city shipbuilding
and marine-engineering cluster with 40%+ local supply.

## What the cluster is

The Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai shipbuilding and marine-engineering cluster (青烟威) entered the national
advanced-manufacturing list in **December 2024**, jointly built by Qingdao, Yantai, and Weihai [1][2].
It gathers **~140 above-scale assembly and supporting firms** — including **Beihai Shipbuilding
(北海造船)** and **CIMC Raffles (中集来福士)** — with **600+ upstream/downstream enterprises** and a
**40%+ local supporting rate** for main ship types [2]. Shandong's national advanced clusters rose to
six, with Qingdao holding four [2].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "tri-city role split." The national designation covers three cities with
distinct functions — Qingdao (total assembly and large vessels), Yantai (marine-engineering platforms,
CIMC Raffles), Weihai (supporting fabrication) — reflecting the geographic spread of port-and-shipyard
infrastructure along Shandong's coast. The 40% local supporting rate is solid but below Zhuzhou's 80%+
rail cluster, indicating a less self-contained supply chain.

## Data deep-dive

- **~140 above-scale firms / 600+ upstream-downstream** [2].
- **40%+ local supporting rate** for main ship types [2].
- **December 2024** national designation; Shandong's 6 national clusters, Qingdao holding 4 [2].

## Original analysis

*Opinion:* This is a **role-split cluster, not a single-anchor one** — a buyer routes
**total-assembly/large-vessels to Qingdao**, **offshore/marine-engineering to Yantai**, and
**supporting fabrication to Weihai**. The 40% local rate means a meaningful share of components is
sourced outside the cluster — factor that into lead-time and logistics planning. The tri-city structure
is both a strength (full chain coverage) and a coordination cost (three jurisdictions). For buyers, the
practical move is a coordinated three-city sourcing strategy covering the full vessel chain.

## What it makes

- **Ships** and large vessels (Qingdao/Beihai).
- **Offshore and marine-engineering platforms** (Yantai/CIMC Raffles).
- **Supporting fabrication** (Weihai).

## The honest caveats

- **40% local supply** — below the most self-contained clusters (e.g. Zhuzhou 80%+).
- **Tri-city coordination** — three jurisdictions mean coordination overhead.

## What this means for buyers

- **Role-split sourcing:** Qingdao (assembly), Yantai (offshore), Weihai (fabrication).
- **40%+ local supply** — plan for out-of-cluster components.
- **Caveats:** tender-driven contracts; tri-city coordination overhead.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, the Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai cluster is a role-split cluster, not a single-anchor one — and a buyer should route by role, not by a single port name.*

Qingdao does total assembly and large vessels, Yantai concentrates on marine engineering, and Weihai is the co-building base. Treating them as interchangeable will send you to the wrong dock for the wrong vessel type.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
