# Yancheng Offshore Wind: China's Offshore Wind Power First City

Yancheng is the fifty-third formation mechanism in this project — China's "offshore new-energy first
city," anchored by Goldwind's assembly base and high-absorption grid integration.

## What the cluster is

Yancheng's new-energy installed capacity was **13.619 GW** by October 2023 — leading Jiangsu in total,
wind, and PV capacity (22%, 41.5%, 10.7% of Jiangsu respectively) [1]. Its offshore-wind installed
capacity (2020) was **3.52 GW — 61% of Jiangsu, 39% of national, ~10% of global** [1]. **Goldwind's
(金风科技) Dafeng test farm** runs 800 MW with **98 intelligent offshore turbines** up to the
**GWH252-13.6 MW** class [3].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "resource-plus-manufacturing." Yancheng's ~10% global offshore-wind share
is a *resource* figure (installed capacity) as much as a *manufacturing* one. The Goldwind Dafeng test
farm (98 turbines, 13.6 MW class) is the structural differentiator: a real-world validation ground that
manufacturing-only clusters lack — Yancheng is where offshore turbine technology is *proven*, not just
built.

## Data deep-dive

- **800 MW / 98 turbines / 13.6 MW class** — Goldwind Dafeng test farm [3].
