# Weifang Power Equipment: Weichai and the 'Chain-Length' Model

Weifang is the forty-sixth formation mechanism in this project — a power-equipment cluster anchored on
Weichai under Shandong's distinctive "chain-length" industrial governance model.

## What the cluster is

Weifang's power-equipment cluster is built around **Weichai (潍柴)** — China's leading diesel-engine
maker — and is governed by Shandong's **"chain-length" (链长) model**, where a senior official acts as
"chain leader" for a specific industry chain. In August 2024, Weifang's party secretary took the
chain-length role for the power-equipment chain, convening a dedicated meeting at Weichai Group — part
of a city-wide push that put all **12 industry chains** under chain-length governance [1].

The scale is city- and cluster-level: Weifang's **2024 GDP was RMB 820.32 billion (+5.9%)** [2]. Its
power-equipment cluster hit **RMB 413.5 billion in output in 2021 — roughly one-third of China's
national power-equipment total** — with **8 national single-champion firms**, Weichai among them [3].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "chain-length governance + single champion + national share." Weifang is
the power-generation pole of China's power-equipment industry, complementing Baoding's high-voltage
transmission pole. The chain-length model is the structural signature — state coordination organized
around named chain leaders rather than a free market.

## Data deep-dive

- **RMB 413.5 billion (2021)** — cluster output, ~1/3 of national power-equipment output [3].

- **RMB 820.32 billion (2024)** — Weifang GDP, +5.9% YoY [2].

## Original analysis

*Opinion:* Weifang is a **generation-equipment cluster, not a transmission cluster** — its Weichai
diesel engines serve trucks, ships, and generators, a different buyer universe from Baoding's
transformers and switchgear. The "1/3 national" figure (2021) is the scale signal, but it is
now-vintage; treat it as a magnitude marker, not a live number. The chain-length model is worth
understanding: it means procurement and policy access in Weifang runs through named chain leaders —
a governance reality foreign buyers rarely see but should price in.
