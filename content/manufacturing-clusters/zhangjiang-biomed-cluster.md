# Shanghai Zhangjiang: China's Innovation-Drug Export Epicenter

Zhangjiang is the forty-first formation mechanism in this project — a research-anchored biopharma
cluster that is China's epicenter for innovation-drug exports.

## What the cluster is

Zhangjiang (张江) has cumulatively approved **20+ Class-1 innovative drugs** — nearly **1/5 of the
national total** — plus 17 approved innovative medical devices (10% of national) and **800+ pipeline
candidates** [1]. Its licensing engine is the standout: Shanghai recorded **38 license-out deals in
2024 (35% of national)** worth **US$30.7 billion (47% of national)** — nearly half of all Chinese
biopharma out-licensing value [2]. Nineteen of the global top-20 pharma and medtech firms each have a
China HQ or R&D center in Shanghai [2].

*Inference:* the mechanism is "research anchor + licensing engine." Zhangjiang owns China's
*discovery-and-transaction* layer of biopharma — its US$30.7B license-out value is the liquidity signal
that Chinese innovation-drug assets are now globally tradeable, with Shanghai as the deal-flow gateway.

## Data deep-dive

- **20+ Class-1 new drugs (~1/5 national) / 800+ pipeline** [1].
- **38 license-out deals (35% national) / US$30.7B (47% national)** in 2024 [2].
- **19 of global top-20 pharma/medtech each in Shanghai** [2].

## Original analysis

*Opinion:* Zhangjiang is the **discovery-and-licensing pole**, complementary to Suzhou's
industrialization pole — a molecule is discovered in Zhangjiang, then manufactured in Suzhou. For
buyers/licensees, Zhangjiang is the **deal-flow gateway** (its 47% national license-out value is the
signal to watch). A Western buyer who ignores it misses the largest single out-licensing pool in China.
The "20+ drugs / ~1/5" is cumulative, not annual — do not read as yearly output. The structural insight
is that Zhangjiang's value has shifted from *making* drugs to *monetizing* them globally.

## What it makes

- **Innovative drugs** and medical devices.
- **Deal flow** — China's out-licensing epicenter.

## The honest caveats

- **Cumulative figures.** 20+ drugs and 800+ pipeline are cumulative, not annual.
- **Transaction layer.** The 47% national value is license-out value, not manufacturing output.

## What this means for buyers

- **Discovery and licensing** — China's deal-flow gateway.
- **Suzhou pairing** for downstream industrialization.
- **Caveats:** treat approval counts as cumulative; value sits in licensing, not volume.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, Zhangjiang is the discovery-and-licensing pole of biopharma, complementary to Suzhou's industrialization pole — a molecule is discovered in Zhangjiang and manufactured in Suzhou.*

Its licensing-out engine is the differentiated asset. For innovation-drug R&D, licensing, and early-stage biotech, Zhangjiang is the anchor; for scale manufacturing, follow the corridor to Suzhou.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
