# China Footwear Industry

China's footwear industry anchors on Jinjiang — Chendai town packs 3,000+ firms producing 1 billion+
sneakers a year, 40% of China's sports shoes.

## Industry at a glance

- **2023 sports-shoe-and-apparel market: RMB 492.6 billion** [1].
- **2023 sports-shoe sales: ~780 million pairs**; sports-shoe OEM market RMB 22.7 billion (2023) [2].

## National footprint

- [Jinjiang/Quanzhou](/cities/quanzhou/): the manufacturing core (Chendai's 3,000 firms, Anta/Xtep/361°).
- [Wenzhou](/cities/wenzhou/): leather-shoe manufacturing complement.

## Data deep-dive

The OEM layer is consolidating into professional groups — **Huali (华利) and Shenzhou (申洲国际)** are the
capital-market darlings of footwear/apparel contract manufacturing, reflecting a shift from fragmented
small makers to scaled ODM platforms [1]. The value chain is splitting: branded ODM (Anta tier) at the
top, commodity OEM (Chendai's 3,000-firm tail) at the base.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Footwear is a **density-plus-brand cluster** — Chendai's extreme spatial density (3,000
firms) provides the commodity-OEM floor, while the anchor brands (Anta, Xtep) pull the value layer up
toward ODM and global-standard setting. The Huali/Shenzhou rise signals the OEM middle is professionalizing.

*Opinion:* For buyers, match the supplier tier deliberately: **commodity-volume sneakers → Chendai's
3,000-firm base**; **branded/ODM → Anta/Xtep tier**; **scaled contract manufacturing → Huali/Shenzhou
group**. The three tiers have different minimums, lead times, and quality bars.

## Buyer notes

- Commodity-volume OEM → Chendai's 3,000-firm base.
- Branded/ODM → Anta, Xtep, 361° tier.
- Scaled contract manufacturing → Huali, Shenzhou.
- Match supplier tier to requirement (volume vs. brand vs. scaled OEM).

## The Author's Take

*In my view, footwear is a density-plus-brand cluster — Chendai's extreme spatial density provides the commodity-OEM floor, while the anchor brands pull the whole cluster up-market.*

So match the supplier tier deliberately: commodity-volume sneakers to Chendai's 3,000-firm base; branded and ODM work to the Anta and Xtep tier; scaled contract manufacturing elsewhere. Different tiers, different prices, different quality.
