# China Pumps & Valves Industry

China's pump-and-valve industry anchors on Taizhou (Wenling pumps, Yuhuan valves) and Wenzhou — the
category-density core of the "power-and-fluids" layer.

## Industry at a glance

- **2022 China pump market: ~RMB 210 billion, ~50% of the global market** [1].
- **Global pump market: US$67.1 billion (2023), forecast US$90.93 billion by 2030** (4.5% CAGR) [2].

## National footprint

- [Taizhou](/cities/taizhou/): Wenling small water pumps (70%+ of national), Yuhuan precision valves.
- [Wenzhou](/cities/wenzhou/): valve-and-pump complement.

## Data deep-dive

The pump market is **highly fragmented but consolidating** — the domestic leaders (Kaiquan RMB 4.85B,
Leo RMB 3.78B, Nanfang RMB 3.56B, Dongfang RMB 3.4B in 2024) are far below the global tier (Ebara),
signaling a consolidation runway [1][2]. The value is shifting toward intelligent, energy-efficient, and
modular pumps driven by municipal, energy, and industrial demand.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Pumps is a **fragmented, consolidating industry** — no single champion, just a long tail of
mid-size leaders, with ~50% of the global market by volume but a smaller share of value. Its Wenling
small-pump monopoly (70%+ of national) is a category-density play, not a brand play.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **commodity small water pumps → Wenling** and **precision valves →
Yuhuan**, with Wenzhou as the valve-and-pump complement. The fragmentation means per-factory quality
verification matters more than brand — and the consolidation runway means a tier of mid-size leaders is
emerging as reliable OEM partners.

## Buyer notes

- Commodity water pumps → [Wenling/Taizhou](/cities/taizhou/).
- Precision valves → Yuhuan.
- Verify per-factory quality (fragmented market); watch the consolidating leader tier.
