# China Power Battery Industry

China's power-battery industry anchors on Ningde — CATL holds 39.2% global share, the single densest
battery concentration on earth.

## Industry at a glance

- **2024 China installations: 548.4 GWh (+41.5%), 61.3% of global total** [1].
- **2024 cumulative battery output: 1,096.8 GWh (+41.0%)** [2].
- **Top-3 share 78.4%, top-5 86.1%, top-10 97.1%** — a steeply concentrated oligopoly [2].

## National footprint

- [Ningde](/cities/ningde/): CATL's home base (46.4% domestic share).
- [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/): BYD (25.1% domestic share).

## Data deep-dive

The concentration is extreme: **CATL 46.4% + BYD 25.1% = 71.5%** of H1-2024 installations, with the top-10
at 97.1% [2]. LFP (lithium iron phosphate) dominates the technology route, and CATL/BYD each shipped
100+ GWh of LFP alone in 2024 [1]. The market is a two-pole oligopoly with a long tail of sub-5% players.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Power batteries is the **most concentrated industry in this dataset** — a 71.5% two-firm
share with the top-10 at 97.1%. This is genuine concentration risk for buyers: unlike multi-brand
electronics or construction machinery, there are effectively two volume suppliers. The 61.3% global
share makes China the price-setting node for the world's battery supply.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Ningde/CATL is unavoidable for high-performance cells, but the concentration
argues for **dual-sourcing** — anchor volume with CATL, qualify BYD or a second source (e.g., FAW-Fudi in
Changchun) for resilience. Lead times and allocation matter more here than in any other industry.

## Buyer notes

- High-performance cells → [Ningde](/cities/ningde/) (CATL) / [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/) (BYD).
- Dual-source against concentration risk (FAW-Fudi is one adjacent cell source).
- Plan capacity/lead time early — allocation, not just price.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, power batteries is the most concentrated industry in this dataset — a dominant two-firm share with the top-10 near saturation — and that is genuine concentration risk for buyers.*

Ningde and CATL are unavoidable for high-performance cells, but the concentration argues for dual-sourcing: anchor volume with CATL, qualify BYD or a credible second source in parallel, and do not put your supply chain in one basket.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
