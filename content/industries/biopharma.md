# China Biopharmaceutical Industry

China's biopharma innovation anchors on Shanghai Zhangjiang (1 in 5 approved new drugs) and Suzhou
Industrial Park (2,000+ firms), the discovery-and-scale-up pair.

## Industry at a glance

- **2023 market size: RMB 1.9548 trillion**; 2024 biological-drug segment estimated past RMB 700 billion
  [1][2].
- **Hengrui (恒瑞医药) posted RMB 25.01 billion in 2024 pharmaceutical-manufacturing revenue** [2].

## National footprint

- [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/) Zhangjiang: discovery and licensing (20+ Class-1 new drugs, 47% of 2024
  national license-out).
- [Suzhou](/cities/suzhou/) Industrial Park: scale-up/CDMO (2,000+ firms).

## Data deep-dive

The segment mix is shifting: chemical drugs still dominate (~47% of market), but biologics (~28%) and
TCM (~25%) are rising year over year — reflecting the innovation-and-diversification trend [2].
Shanghai's Pudong new area alone reached RMB 410 billion (41.6% of the city's biopharma) with 140,000+
professionals and 20+ academicians [3].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Biopharma is a **discovery-and-scale-up pair** — Shanghai discovers and licenses out;
Suzhou develops and manufactures (CDMO). This mirrors the IC and device industries' hardware-ecosystem
splits. The chemical-drug 47% share shows China's biopharma is still largely a *generic-plus-innovation*
market, not yet a pure-innovation one.

*Opinion:* For buyers, split **licensing/innovation → Shanghai Zhangjiang** and **CDMO/biologics
scale-up → Suzhou**. Regulatory load (NMPA + CE/FDA) is the real cost driver — factor certification lead
time, not just unit price.

## Buyer notes

- Discovery/licensing → [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/) Zhangjiang.
- CDMO/biologics scale-up → [Suzhou](/cities/suzhou/).
- Factor NMPA/CE/FDA lead time into sourcing.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, biopharma is a discovery-and-scale-up pair — Shanghai discovers and licenses out; Suzhou develops and manufactures at CDMO scale — and it mirrors the IC and device industries' two-city logic.*

So split licensing and innovation (Shanghai Zhangjiang) from CDMO and biologics scale-up (Suzhou). The real cost driver is regulatory load — NMPA plus CE/FDA — and you should price that in before anything else.
