# China Building Materials & Ceramics Industry

China's building-ceramics industry anchors on Foshan — RMB 100 billion output, 30–40% of national tiles,
and ~80% of ceramic-production presses.

## Industry at a glance

- **2024 ceramic-product exports: 19.57 million tonnes (+2.8%)**; architectural-ceramic exports 13.76
  million tonnes (-1.4%) [1].
- **Architectural-ceramic above-scale firms: 1,026 (2022)**, a consolidating base [2].

## National footprint

- [Foshan](/cities/foshan/) anchors branded tile (RMB 101.97 billion 2023, 745M m²) plus the machinery
  layer (Lital, Keda ~80% of ceramic presses).

## Data deep-dive

The industry is in a **volume-to-value shift**: domestic tile volume is flat-to-declining while the
value layer (branded tile, production machinery) consolidates. Ceramic *equipment* is now the strategic
export — Foshan's press makers (Keda) are expanding into Africa, turning "tile city" into
"tile-machinery city."

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Building ceramics is a **maturing, consolidating cluster** — the 1,026-firm base (down from
peaks) and flat export volume signal an industry rationalizing around branded tile and equipment rather
than commodity volume. This mirrors the broader building-materials cycle (post-real-estate-boom).

*Opinion:* For buyers, the actionable split is **branded tile → Foshan** versus **production
machinery/turnkey lines → Foshan's Keda/Lital** (the higher-margin, Africa-exporting layer). For
commodity tile volume, expect thinning margins — anchor on branded or machinery instead.

## Buyer notes

- Branded ceramic tile → [Foshan](/cities/foshan/).
- Ceramic-production machinery / turnkey lines → Foshan (Keda, Lital).
- Expect volume-to-value shift: anchor on brand or machinery, not commodity tile.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, building ceramics is a maturing, consolidating cluster — a shrinking firm base and flat export volume signal an industry rationalizing around branded product.*

So the split is branded tile (Foshan) versus production machinery and turnkey lines (Foshan's Keda and Lital, the higher-margin, Africa-exporting layer). If you only want commodity tile, expect thin margins and consolidation risk.
