# China Home Appliance Industry

China's home-appliance industry spans Shunde (kitchen), Qingdao (brand scale), and Cixi (small
appliances) — the world's largest appliance producer and consumer.

## Industry at a glance

- **2024 home-appliance market: ~RMB 2,000 billion (five-year CAGR 5.43%)** [1].
- **Air conditioning = 23% and refrigerators = 16% of the market** — the two largest segments [1].
- **Small-appliance market: ~RMB 110 billion; kitchen small appliances = 56%** [2].

## National footprint

- [Shunde/Foshan](/cities/foshan/): kitchen-appliance manufacturing depth (~15% of national, Midea).
- [Qingdao](/cities/qingdao/): brand scale (Haier, Hisense).
- [Cixi](/cities/ningbo/): niche small appliances (air fryers, personal care).

## Data deep-dive

The segment structure is stable: the "big four" (AC, fridge, TV, washer) still dominate, with AC and
fridge alone at 39% of market [1]. Leading manufacturers — Midea, Haier, Gree, Hisense — anchor the
brand layer, while Cixi's niche small-appliance cluster (RMB 108B, Yueda's 20,000 air fryers/day) covers
the long tail [2][cls-cixi-small-appliances].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Home appliances is a **three-tier industry** — brand scale (Qingdao), manufacturing depth
(Shunde), and niche density (Cixi) — each city owning a different layer of the same value chain. The 5.43%
CAGR signals a mature, slow-growth market where the competition is brand-and-scale, not category
expansion.

*Opinion:* For buyers, route by tier: **brand/OEM-scale white goods → Qingdao (Haier, Hisense)**;
**kitchen appliances → Shunde (Midea)**; **niche small appliances → Cixi**. The slow growth means
pricing is competitive — leverage the three-city structure for multi-tier sourcing.

## Buyer notes

- Brand/OEM white goods → [Qingdao](/cities/qingdao/) (Haier, Hisense).
- Kitchen appliances → [Shunde/Foshan](/cities/foshan/) (Midea).
- Niche small appliances → [Cixi](/cities/ningbo/).
- Mature market = competitive pricing; use multi-tier sourcing.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, home appliances is a three-tier industry — brand scale, manufacturing depth, and niche density each live in a different city, so routing all appliance needs to one place is the classic error.*

Brand and OEM-scale white goods go to Qingdao (Haier, Hisense); manufacturing depth to Shunde; niche density and fast SKU iteration to Cixi. Pick the tier, then the city.
