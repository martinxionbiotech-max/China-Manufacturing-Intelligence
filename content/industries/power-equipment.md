# China Power & Electrical Equipment Industry

China's power-equipment industry splits between Baoding (transmission, RMB 166B) and Yueqing
(low-voltage, 65% share) — the high-voltage and low-voltage halves of the power chain.

## Industry at a glance

- **China's power-equipment industry has 30,000+ above-scale firms with 90%+ full-chain localization**
  [1].
- **Baoding's Baobian Electric posted RMB 31.73 billion revenue in the first three quarters of 2024
  (+54.85%)** [2].

## National footprint

- [Baoding](/cities/baoding/): transmission and transformation equipment (Baobian, UHV).
- [Yueqing](/cities/wenzhou/): low-voltage electricals (Chint, 65%+ national share).

## Data deep-dive

The industry splits cleanly by voltage layer: **Baoding** owns the high-voltage/transmission layer
(transformers, UHV equipment — Baobian exports to 40+ countries), while **Yueqing** owns the low-voltage
distribution layer [1][2]. The new-energy and grid-upgrade cycle (equipment renewal) is the demand
driver, with localization above 90% across the full chain.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Power equipment is a **voltage-split industry** — high-voltage transmission and low-voltage
distribution are separate clusters in separate cities, unified only by the grid's demand cycle. The 90%+
localization rate makes it one of China's most self-sufficient equipment industries, insulated from
import substitution pressure.

*Opinion:* For buyers, route by layer: **transmission/UHV equipment → Baoding**; **low-voltage
distribution → Yueqing**. The grid-upgrade and new-energy cycle is a multi-year tailwind — expect firm
demand for both layers.

## Buyer notes

- Transmission/UHV transformers → [Baoding](/cities/baoding/) (Baobian).
- Low-voltage distribution → [Yueqing](/cities/wenzhou/) (Chint).
- Time purchases against the grid-upgrade/new-energy cycle.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, power equipment is a voltage-split industry — high-voltage transmission and low-voltage distribution are separate clusters in separate cities, unified only by the grid that connects them.*

So route by layer: transmission and UHV equipment to Baoding, low-voltage distribution to Yueqing. The grid-upgrade and new-energy cycle is a multi-year tailwind across both — a reason to anchor, not to hedge away.
