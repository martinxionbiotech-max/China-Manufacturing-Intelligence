# China Construction Machinery Industry

China's construction-machinery industry splits between Changsha (Sany, Zoomlion) and Xuzhou (XCMG) — the
world's largest construction-machinery production base.

## Industry at a glance

- **XCMG leads 16 machine categories domestically** (truck cranes, rollers, etc.) and holds global #1 in
  cranes, mobile cranes, and horizontal directional drills [1].
- The industry is cyclical: national revenue fell to RMB 797.7 billion (2022, -12%) in a down-cycle, then
  recovered; the sector targets a RMB 1-trillion market scale [2].

## National footprint

- [Changsha](/cities/changsha/): the **multi-brand pole** — five global top-50 firms (Sany, Zoomlion,
  Sunward, CRCHI, Sinoboom), 70% of national product variety.
- [Xuzhou](/cities/xuzhou/): the **single-champion pole** — XCMG's integration and full lineage.

## Data deep-dive

XCMG's 2024 annual report shows the breadth of a single champion: cranes, mobile cranes, and HDD are
global #1; piling and concrete machinery are global first-tier; excavators #6 globally, #2 domestically;
loaders #1 domestically and #1 globally in new-energy loaders [1]. The equipment-renewal policy (25%+
investment growth target by 2027) is expected to drive a RMB 5-trillion annual equipment market [3].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Construction machinery is a **dual-pole oligopoly** — Changsha (competition/variety) and
Xuzhou (integration/full lineage) split the industry's two strategic logics. XCMG's
global-No.1-across-categories breadth shows a *scale-and-lineage* strategy, while Changsha's
five-firm, 70%-variety structure shows a *competitive-ecosystem* strategy.

*Opinion:* For buyers, the split is **multi-vendor negotiation leverage and niche specs → Changsha**
versus **single-account scale and full lineage → Xuzhou**. The equipment-renewal policy is a demand tailwind
worth timing; expect excavator and crane pricing to stay competitive given the multi-brand structure.

## Buyer notes

- Multi-vendor sourcing, niche specs → [Changsha](/cities/changsha/).
- Single-vendor scale, full lineage → [Xuzhou](/cities/xuzhou/).
- Time purchases against the equipment-renewal demand cycle.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, construction machinery is a dual-pole oligopoly — Changsha and Xuzhou split the industry's two strategic logics, and you pick a logic before you pick a city.*

Changsha gives competition and variety; Xuzhou (XCMG) gives integration and a full lineage. Multi-vendor negotiation leverage and niche specs go to Changsha; single-account scale and full-lineage consistency go to Xuzhou.
