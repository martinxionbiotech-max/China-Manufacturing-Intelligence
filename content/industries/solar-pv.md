# China Solar (PV) Industry

China's PV industry spans Yancheng, Changzhou, Wuxi, and Hefei — anchored by Trina Solar's Yancheng base
— the world's dominant PV manufacturer.

## Industry at a glance

- **2024 new PV installations: 277.6 GW (+28.3%); cumulative 885.7 GW** [1].
- **#1 globally in new PV installations for 12 straight years (since 2013)** [1].

## National footprint

- [Yancheng](/cities/yancheng/): PV manufacturing base (Trina Solar, 106 above-scale chain firms).
- [Changzhou](/cities/changzhou/), [Wuxi](/cities/wuxi/), [Hefei](/cities/hefei/): the Jiangsu-Anhui PV belt.

## Data deep-dive

China's PV industry has built a **full-chain global lead** — from polysilicon through wafers, cells, and
modules — with the 2024 installation surge (277.6 GW) driven by domestic grid-scale buildout. The
optimistic 2030 projection is 1,000+ GW of new global PV installations [1]. The industry is
cost-competitive globally but faces grid-absorption and policy-volatility risks.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Solar PV is a **full-chain, cost-leadership industry** — China owns the entire value chain
and sets global module pricing. The 12-year #1 installation streak reflects a policy-supported domestic
market that also subsidizes global cost leadership. The key risk is grid absorption and price
over-competition, not technology.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **PV modules → Yancheng (Trina Solar)** and the Jiangsu-Anhui belt. The
industry's cost leadership means module pricing is globally competitive, but expect price volatility —
lock in contracts and dual-source across the belt. For integrated clean-energy capex, pair PV with
Yancheng's offshore-wind cluster.

## Buyer notes

- PV modules → [Yancheng](/cities/yancheng/) (Trina Solar).
- Pair PV + offshore wind from Yancheng for integrated clean-energy capex.
- Lock contracts against price volatility.
