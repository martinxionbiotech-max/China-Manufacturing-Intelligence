# China AI & Intelligent Voice Industry

China's intelligent-voice industry is anchored by Hefei's "China Speech Valley" — the densest
voice-and-language AI cluster in the country, built around iFlytek (科大讯飞).

## Industry at a glance

- Hefei's intelligent-voice cluster entered the national advanced-manufacturing list in H1 2025,
  gathering iFlytek, Geovis (中科星图), and Amazfit (华米科技) [cls-hefei-intelligent-voice].
- "China Speech Valley" reached RMB 65 billion output and 430 resident firms as early as 2018.

## National footprint

Hefei is the *manufacturing* anchor for voice AI, but the industry's full map is three-cornered:
**Hefei** (voice/algorithm core, iFlytek), **Beijing** (research and platform layer), and **Shenzhen**
(device integration and hardware).

## Data deep-dive

China's AI industry scale broke RMB 700 billion in 2024, growing >20% annually for multiple consecutive
years (CNNIC) [1]. By March 2025, 346 generative-AI services had passed national filing [1]. Note the
metric divergence: the 2,697-billion figure from one 2024 report counts a narrower definition, while the
7,000-billion CNNIC figure is broader — treat any single "AI market size" number as definition-dependent.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Hefei's voice cluster is an **algorithm-plus-institution cluster** — built on research
institutions (USTC) and a national champion, not on low-cost labor or port logistics. That makes it
"sticky" but dependent on one anchor firm's trajectory. The 1,000-firm iFlytek Town is the material
signal of ecosystem widening beyond a single champion.

*Opinion:* For buyers, the actionable distinction is between **software/algorithm licensing** (Hefei,
iFlytek) and **hardware integration** (Shenzhen). "AI" as a sourcing category is too broad; voice, edge
AI, and smart-terminal AI are separate supply chains anchored in different cities. Beware
definition-dependent market-size claims when comparing vendors.

## Buyer notes

- Voice/LLM-related software and voice modules → Hefei (iFlytek ecosystem).
- Edge-AI and smart-terminal hardware integration → Shenzhen's electronics base
  ([electronics](/industries/electronics/)).

## The Author's Take

*In my view, the Hefei voice cluster is an algorithm-plus-institution cluster — built on a research university and a national champion, not on cheap labor or port logistics.*

That matters because "AI" is too broad a sourcing category. Split software and algorithm licensing (Hefei, iFlytek) from hardware integration (Shenzhen), and you stop making a category error that trips up a lot of buyers.
