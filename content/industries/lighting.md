# China Lighting Industry

China's lighting industry anchors on Guzhen — ~70% of domestic and ~50% of the global market from a
sub-50 km² town, the densest single-category concentration in this knowledge base.

## Industry at a glance

- **2024 LED-lighting market: RMB 716.9 billion** (3.64% CAGR) [1].
- **LED = 85.3% of China's general-lighting-source exports (2024)**, up from 79% in 2023 — LED
  substitution of traditional sources is accelerating [2].

## National footprint

- [Guzhen/Zhongshan](/cities/zhongshan/): the manufacturing-and-market core (30,000+ entities).
- [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/): LED components and design.
- Xiamen: export fixtures.

## Data deep-dive

The high-value segments are the growth engine: **UV LED (RMB 2.8 billion in 2024, +23%)**, plant
lighting (US$1.317 billion, +6.8%), and Mini/Micro-LED display backlights [2]. The commodity
general-lighting core is mature and consolidating, while specialty lighting is where margin and growth
now live.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Lighting is a **category-monopoly + technology-upgrade industry** — Guzhen's extreme spatial
density owns the commodity floor, while the LED-substitution (85.3% of exports) and specialty segments
(UV, plant, Mini-LED) drive the value layer. The two are diverging: commodity fixtures commoditize,
specialty lighting concentrates.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **commodity LED fixtures → Guzhen**, but source **UV/plant/Mini-LED
specialty lighting** from the technology layer (Shenzhen components + specialized makers), not the
commodity floor. Treat the "~50% global" figure as magnitude, not audit — and pair Guzhen's manufacturing
with Shenzhen's design layer when specs matter.

## Buyer notes

- Commodity LED fixtures → [Guzhen/Zhongshan](/cities/zhongshan/).
- UV/plant/Mini-LED specialty lighting → technology layer (Shenzhen components).
- Pair Guzhen manufacturing with Shenzhen design/components.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, lighting is a category-monopoly plus technology-upgrade industry — Guzhen's extreme spatial density owns the commodity floor, while the LED-substitution wave is the growth engine.*

So anchor commodity LED fixtures to Guzhen, but source UV, plant, and Mini-LED specialty lighting from the technology layer (Shenzhen components plus specialized makers). The floor and the frontier are different cities.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
