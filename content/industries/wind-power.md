# China Wind Power Equipment Industry

China's wind-power equipment industry anchors on Yancheng — 39% of national offshore wind and ~10% of
global — the world's leading wind-power manufacturer.

## Industry at a glance

- **2024 cumulative grid-connected capacity: 521 GW (+18%); new installations 79.82 GW (~70% of global)**
  [1].
- **2024 wind power generation: 991.6 TWh (+16%); utilization 95.9%** [1].

## National footprint

- [Yancheng](/cities/yancheng/): offshore-wind manufacturing and test farm (Goldwind).
- [Wuxi](/cities/wuxi/): wind-equipment component complement.

## Data deep-dive

China's offshore wind reached 41 GW cumulative (2024), and technology is leaping: **18–20 MW
single-unit turbines installed, 26 MW offshore prototypes built, 10 MW onshore units in batch
operation** [2]. The "three-north" region holds 75% of new installations, while floating offshore wind
made multiple breakthroughs [2].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Wind power is a **resource-plus-manufacturing, scale-leaping industry** — China's ~70% of
global new installations makes it the price-setting node, and the 18–26 MW turbine leap signals a
technology cycle, not just volume growth. Yancheng's test-farm infrastructure (98 turbines, 13.6 MW
class) provides real-world validation that pure-manufacturing clusters lack.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **offshore-wind turbines/systems → Yancheng (Goldwind)** and pair PV +
wind from one geography for integrated clean-energy capex. The 10%-global figure is 2020-vintage (now
understated) — treat it as a floor, and expect the large-turbine (18–26 MW) shift to matter for
project economics.
