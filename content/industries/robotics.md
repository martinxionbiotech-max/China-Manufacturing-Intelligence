# China Robotics Industry

China's robotics industry traces to Shenyang — SIASUN (新松), born from the institute that built China's
first industrial robot — with Shenzhen as the volume-and-deployment pole.

## Industry at a glance

- **2024 industrial-robot sales: 302,000 units — #1 globally for 12 straight years** [1].
- **China's robot patent applications = 2/3 of the global total** [1].
- **2024 industrial-robot market: ~RMB 58 billion; Shenzhen produced 133,900 industrial + 7.11 million
  service robots** [2].

## National footprint

- [Shenyang](/cities/shenyang/): the technology-origin pole (SIASUN, 7-academician research base).
- [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/): volume, deployment, and integration (robot valley, UBTech/Dobot).
- [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/): system integration and AI deployment.

## Data deep-dive

The competitive geography is striking: **Shandong has the most robot firms (2,371), followed by
Guangdong, Jiangsu, Zhejiang** [3] — the manufacturing provinces, not the research origin, hold the
enterprise mass. The market splits into industrial (SIASUN, Estun), service (Ecovacs, Roborock), public
(DJI, Horizon), and specialty robots — four distinct competitive arenas.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Robotics is a **technology-origin + volume-distributed industry** — the technology was born
in Shenyang, but the volume and deployment concentrate in the Delta (Shenzhen's 133,900 industrial units).
The four sub-arenas (industrial/service/public/specialty) are diverging supply chains, not one market.

*Opinion:* For buyers, split **robot arms → Shenyang (SIASUN)** from **system integration/AI deployment →
Shanghai/Shenzhen**. Evaluate "robot + AI" (embodied intelligence) capability separately from classic
arm hardware — they are diverging chains. Shenzhen owns volume and price; Shenyang owns standards and
lineage.
