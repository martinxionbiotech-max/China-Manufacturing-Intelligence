# China Electronics & ICT Manufacturing

China's electronics manufacturing anchors on Shenzhen and Dongguan — the world's densest
electronics-and-smart-terminal corridor.

## Industry at a glance

- **2024 above-scale electronics manufacturing revenue: RMB 16.19 trillion (+7.3%); profit RMB 640.8
  billion (+3.4%)** [1].
- **Phone output: 1.67 billion units (+7.8%), including 1.25 billion smartphones (+8.2%)** [1].

## National footprint

- [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/): components, PCBs, design, distribution (Huaqiangbei).
- [Dongguan](/cities/dongguan/): terminal assembly (1 in 5 phones globally).
- [Chengdu-Chongqing](/cities/chengdu/): cost-optimized inland ICT (RMB 1.3T).

## Data deep-dive

The regional structure is sharply concentrated: **Guangdong alone holds 13,195 above-scale electronics
firms (31.6% of national) and RMB 6.07 trillion revenue (28.7%)**, producing 40.9% of national mobile
phones, 22.7% of microcomputers, and 57.7% of color TVs [2]. East China adds the IC-focused Yangtze
Delta (SMIC, JCET), completing a two-hub structure.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Electronics is a **two-hub, layered industry** — the Pearl River Delta (components + smart
terminals) and the Yangtze Delta (ICs + foundry) divide the work by layer. Guangdong's 40.9% phone share
confirms the Delta is the global smart-terminal factory, while the Yangtze Delta owns the chip layer.

*Opinion:* For buyers, route by layer: **components/PCBs/design → Shenzhen**; **finished smart terminals
→ Dongguan**; **ICs/foundry → Yangtze Delta (Shanghai)**; **cost-optimized inland assembly →
Chengdu-Chongqing**. The 4.0% operating margin is a signal to expect thin, competitive pricing across
the layer.

## Buyer notes

- Components/PCBs/design/distribution → [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/).
- Smart-terminal assembly → [Dongguan](/cities/dongguan/).
- ICs/foundry → [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/) (Yangtze Delta).
- Inland cost-optimized assembly → [Chengdu-Chongqing](/cities/chengdu/).

## The Author's Take

*In my view, electronics is a two-hub, layered industry — the Pearl River Delta and Yangtze Delta divide the work by layer, not by rivalry.*

So route by layer: components, PCBs, and design to Shenzhen; finished smart terminals to Dongguan; ICs and foundry to the Yangtze Delta (Shanghai); cost-optimized inland assembly to Chengdu-Chongqing. The layer tells you the city.
