# China Shipbuilding Industry

China's shipbuilding includes the Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai marine-engineering cluster — Beihai
Shipbuilding, CIMC Raffles, 140+ firms — one of China's most globally competitive heavy industries.

## Industry at a glance

- **2023 above-scale shipbuilding revenue: RMB 623.7 billion (+20%); profit RMB 25.9 billion (+131.7%)**
  [1].
- **2024 H1: revenue RMB 327.35 billion (+23.8%); profit RMB 22.22 billion (+137.3%)** [1].
- **2024 completion: ~45 million DWT; new orders ~50 million DWT** [2].

## National footprint

- [Qingdao](/cities/qingdao/): total assembly (Beihai Shipbuilding).
- [Yantai](/cities/yantai/): marine engineering (CIMC Raffles).
- [Weihai](/cities/weihai/): co-building fabrication.

## Data deep-dive

The industry is in a **high-boom cycle**: profit growth (+131.7% in 2023, +137.3% in H1 2024) far
outpaces revenue growth, signaling pricing power and efficiency gains [1]. Demand is driven by fleet
renewal, green-ship mandates, and the "deep-sea technology" strategic-industry designation (2025
Government Work Report) [3]. Green and intelligent vessels are the new-order engine.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Shipbuilding is a **cyclical, capital-intensive champion industry** — the three-city
Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai division (assembly/marine-engineering/fabrication) is a role-split, not a
competition. The profit-growth-outpacing-revenue pattern is the signature of a supply-constrained upcycle:
yards hold pricing power, which favors buyers who secure slots early.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **total assembly/large vessels → Qingdao (Beihai)**; **marine-engineering
platforms → Yantai (CIMC Raffles)**; **co-building fabrication → Weihai**. In a tight upcycle, secure
yard slots and green-ship specs early — capacity, not price, is the binding constraint.

## Buyer notes

- Total assembly/large vessels → [Qingdao](/cities/qingdao/) (Beihai).
- Marine engineering → [Yantai](/cities/yantai/) (CIMC Raffles).
- Co-building → [Weihai](/cities/weihai/).
- Secure yard slots early (supply-constrained upcycle).

## The Author's Take

*In my view, shipbuilding is a cyclical, capital-intensive champion industry, and the three-city Qingdao-Yantai-Weihai division is a role-split, not a rivalry.*

Anchor total assembly and large vessels to Qingdao (Beihai); marine-engineering platforms to Yantai (CIMC Raffles); co-building fabrication to Weihai. Treat the three as one cluster, and expect the upcycle to move price and lead time.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
