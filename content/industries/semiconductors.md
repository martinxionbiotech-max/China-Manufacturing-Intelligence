# China Semiconductor Industry

China's IC industry anchors on Shanghai's Zhangjiang — SMIC, Hua Hong, and 7 of the world's top-10 chip
designers — the domestic-foundry core.

## Industry at a glance

- **2024 semiconductor market: RMB 1.7567 trillion; ICs = 78%** [1].
- **2023 IC output: 351.44 billion units (+8.41%); 2024 Jan–Aug +26.6%** [2].
- **Global IC market: US$626.8 billion (2024, +19%)** [3].

## National footprint

- [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/): foundry and design (SMIC, Hua Hong, AMEC).
- [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/): design and downstream integration.
- [Wuxi](/cities/wuxi/): IC materials and packaging complement.

## Data deep-dive

The 2024 global IC rebound (+19%) was led by memory (+81%) and AI/auto demand [3]. China's localization
is accelerating: the top-10 competitive firms (SMIC, Naura, Will Semiconductor, Hygon, Hua Hong, etc.)
span design (49 of the top-100), equipment (14), and materials (11) [2]. The structure has matured into
a coordinated design-manufacturing-packaging trio.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Semiconductors is a **foundry-anchored, substitution-driven industry** — its growth is
policy-pulled (import substitution) as much as market-pulled. The memory-led 2024 rebound and the
design-heavy top-100 (49 firms) show a sector strengthening design and equipment, not just assembly.

*Opinion:* For buyers, route by layer: **domestic IC design/foundry → Shanghai Zhangjiang**;
**downstream integration → Shenzhen**; **materials/packaging → Wuxi**. Export controls and the
substitution policy make provenance and end-use verification critical for advanced-node parts.

## Buyer notes

- IC design/foundry → [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai/) (SMIC, Hua Hong).
- Downstream integration → [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/).
- Materials/packaging → [Wuxi](/cities/wuxi/).
- Verify provenance/end-use for advanced-node parts.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, semiconductors is a foundry-anchored, substitution-driven industry — its growth is policy-pulled as much as market-pulled, which shapes what you can realistically source.*

For domestic IC design and foundry, route to Shanghai Zhangjiang. But advanced-node access is externally constrained, so the realistic use is design, packaging, and mature-node work — price that in rather than hoping for leading-edge capacity.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
