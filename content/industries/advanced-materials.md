# China Advanced Materials Industry

China's advanced-materials industry spans carbon fiber (Changzhou, Ningbo) and magnetic materials
(Ningbo, 40%+ of national) — a materials layer that underpins aerospace, new energy, and electronics.

## Industry at a glance

- China's new-materials output grew from ~RMB 1 trillion (2012) to RMB 6.8 trillion (2022), and to an
  estimated RMB 8.48 trillion in 2024 — targeting RMB 10 trillion by 2025 [1][2].
- Over 100 material categories (advanced energy-storage, PV, superhard, new-display) are #1 globally by
  output; carbon fiber, wind blades, graphene, and display glass each form RMB 10B+ industrial groups
  [2].

## National footprint

- **Carbon fiber**: [Changzhou](/cities/changzhou/) anchors the complete chain — precursor, composite
  forming, equipment, end products — with the new carbon-materials cluster past RMB 120 billion
  [cls-changzhou-carbon-fiber].
- **Magnetic materials**: [Ningbo](/cities/ningbo/) converts [Baotou](/cities/baotou/) ore into finished
  NdFeB magnets (40%+ of national) [cls-ningbo-magnetic-materials].

## Data deep-dive

The global new-materials market reached ~US$4.7 trillion in 2024 [1]. China's cluster geography is
structured: Bohai Rim, Yangtze Delta, and Pearl River Delta are the three centers, with Northeast and
central-west as specialty zones [1]. Carbon fiber is on the national *Key New Materials First-Batch
Application Demonstration Catalog* (2024), framed explicitly as core to aircraft and aerospace
supply-chain security.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Advanced materials is a **layer, not a product** — its value is that it sits upstream of
aerospace, NEV, and electronics, so a materials breakthrough compounds across every downstream cluster.
China's 8.48-trillion scale reflects breadth (100+ #1 categories) more than any single dominant material.
The cluster geography (3 centers + specialty zones) mirrors the broader manufacturing map.

*Opinion:* For buyers, advanced materials should be sourced **by application, not by generic category**.
Carbon fiber → Changzhou (aerospace-grade, export-controlled); NdFeB magnets → Ningbo (downstream of
Baotou ore). Verify end-use licensing for aerospace and defense-adjacent grades before committing.

## Buyer notes

- Aerospace-grade carbon fiber and composites → [Changzhou](/cities/changzhou/).
- Finished NdFeB magnets → [Ningbo](/cities/ningbo/).
- Export controls apply to aerospace and defense-adjacent grades — verify end-use.
