# China Leather & Fur Industry

China's leather-and-fur industry anchors on Haining — the Leather City and a high-end autumn-winter
fashion supply chain.

## Industry at a glance

- **2024 above-scale leather/fur/feather + shoemaking revenue: RMB 853.32 billion (+3.7%); profit RMB
  46.09 billion (+1.6%)** [1].
- **Haining "leveraged one piece of leather into a RMB 100B industry cluster"**
  [cls-haining-leather].

## National footprint

- [Haining](/cities/haining/): finished leather/fur fashion garments (the Leather City trading platform).

## Data deep-dive

The national leather industry (leather + fur + feather + shoemaking) is a **stable, low-growth
livelihood sector** — revenue up 3.7% and profit up just 1.6% in 2024 [1]. The value is concentrated in
finished, fashion-tier autumn-winter garments (coats, fur), not commodity hides, where Haining holds
"dominant" influence through its trading-platform model.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Leather is a **seasonal fashion supply chain**, not a commodity industry — its strength is
finished autumn-winter garments where a trading platform (Leather City) anchors the discovery layer and
thousands of small makers supply behind it. The 1.6% profit growth signals a mature, margin-thin sector
where fashion tiering is the only differentiator.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Haining is the correct anchor for **finished, fashion-tier leather/fur garments**,
sourced in season. Commodity leather hides and mass garment blanks are not the strength — match the
fashion tier, not the commodity tier.

## Buyer notes

- Finished autumn-winter leather/fur garments → [Haining](/cities/haining/).
- Source in season (CHIC spring / autumn-winter cycle).
- Match the fashion tier, not the commodity tier.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, leather is a seasonal fashion supply chain, not a commodity industry — its strength is finished autumn-winter garments where a trading platform anchors the whole chain.*

So Haining is the correct anchor for finished, fashion-tier leather and fur garments, sourced in season. Commodity leather hides and mass garment blanks are not Haining's strength — that is a different, lower-value market.
