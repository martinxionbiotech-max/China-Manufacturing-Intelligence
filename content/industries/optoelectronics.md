# China Optoelectronics Industry

China's optoelectronics and display industry anchors on Wuhan's Optics Valley — the world's largest
optical-fiber and cable production base, with Shenzhen as the display-integration complement.

## Industry at a glance

- **2024 optical-communication-systems market: RMB 215.4 billion (+1.07%)** [1].
- **2024 laser market forecast: RMB 145.5 billion** (2022 base RMB 103.7 billion) [2].

## National footprint

- [Wuhan](/cities/wuhan/): fiber, optoelectronic devices, laser equipment (16,000+ firms, 光芯屏端网 chain).
- [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/): display and electronics integration.

## Data deep-dive

The sector is upgrading fast: 400G/800G optical modules, silicon photonics, and G.654.E new-type fiber
are being pulled by the "double-gigabit" network buildout and AI-compute network expansion [1]. Wuhan's
1+8+10+163+525 S&T matrix (1 national lab, 8 large facilities, 10 Hubei labs, 163 national platforms,
525 R&D institutes) is the research backbone [cls-wuhan-optics-valley].

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Optoelectronics is a **research-anchored, compute-driven industry** — its demand now tracks
AI/compute-network buildout as much as telecom. Wuhan's institution depth (42 universities, the S&T
matrix) makes it the intellectual capital of fiber-optics, structurally distinct from labor-density
clusters.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **fiber, optical components, and laser equipment → Wuhan**, and pair Wuhan
with **Shenzhen for display integration** in optoelectronic-system sourcing. The 400G/800G-module and
silicon-photonics upgrade is the growth layer worth watching for forward-looking component sourcing.

## Buyer notes

- Optical fiber, optoelectronic devices, laser equipment → [Wuhan](/cities/wuhan/).
- Display/integration → [Shenzhen](/cities/shenzhen/).
- Watch 400G/800G modules and silicon photonics for forward component needs.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, optoelectronics is a research-anchored, compute-driven industry — its demand now tracks AI and compute-network buildout as much as telecom.*

So anchor fiber, optical components, and laser equipment to Wuhan (Optics Valley), and pair Wuhan with Shenzhen for display integration. The compute cycle, not the telecom cycle, is now the demand driver to watch.
