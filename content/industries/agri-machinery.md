# China Agricultural Machinery Industry

China's agricultural-machinery industry anchors on Luoyang — YTO Group (中国一拖), 300+ linked firms,
RMB 60 billion output — and gave Henan its first national advanced-manufacturing cluster in 2024.

## Industry at a glance

- **Market size: RMB 610 billion (2024)**; above-scale enterprise revenue an estimated RMB 195.8 billion
  [1][2].
- **YTO Group (一拖股份) led with RMB 10.96 billion in 2024 agricultural-machinery revenue** [2].

## National footprint

- [Luoyang](/cities/luoyang/) is the **complete-machine anchor** (YTO tractors, 8568 combine harvester).
- [Weifang](/cities/weifang/) is the adjacent **diesel-engine/powertrain** anchor (Weichai) — the
  engine chain is separate from the tractor chain.

## Data deep-dive

Agricultural-machinery total power has risen steadily, and the industry is consolidating — above-scale
revenue slipped to RMB 188.32 billion in 2023 (-7.6%) before recovering to ~RMB 195.8 billion in 2024
[2]. The sector is upgrading toward smart, high-end, and green machinery, driven by the large-scale
equipment-renewal policy.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Agricultural machinery is a **single-champion industry** — one anchor (YTO), one city
(Luoyang), one national cluster. That concentration produces a deep, stable supply chain but weaker
competitive pricing pressure than multi-brand sectors (construction machinery, electronics). The
sector's small market size (RMB 610B) relative to autos (RMB 3-trillion production) explains why it is a
strategic rather than a scale industry.

*Opinion:* For buyers, the practical split is **complete machines → Luoyang (YTO)** versus
**engines/powertrain → Weifang (Weichai)**. For tractors and combines, YTO is the safe, standards-backed
choice; for powertrain components, route to the engine chain.

## Buyer notes

- Complete tractors and combine harvesters → [Luoyang](/cities/luoyang/) (YTO).
- Engines/powertrain → [Weifang](/cities/weifang/) (Weichai).
- Distinguish the engine chain from the tractor chain.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, agricultural machinery is a single-champion industry — one anchor, one city, one cluster — and that concentration is a trade-off, not a flaw.*

It produces a deep, stable supply chain, but less competitive tension than a multi-champion category. For complete machines, Luoyang (YTO) is the anchor; for engines and powertrain, Weifang (Weichai) is a separate lane.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
