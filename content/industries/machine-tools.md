# China Machine Tool Industry

China's machine-tool industry anchors on East Zhejiang — 2,000+ firms, RMB 280 billion output, ~1/5 of
national — the "industrial mother machine" base.

## Industry at a glance

- **2023 CNC machine-tool industry scale: RMB 409 billion; ~RMB 432.5 billion in 2024** [1].
- **2023 CNC metal-cutting machine-tool output: 279,000 units (+33.68%)** [2].

## National footprint

- [Taizhou](/cities/taizhou/): 2,000+ firms, economical CNC = 1/3 of national (Heidman, Eastern CNC).
- [Ningbo](/cities/ningbo/): co-anchor of the East-Zhejiang cluster.

## Data deep-dive

CNC machine tools recovered strongly — from RMB 326 billion (2020, COVID trough) to RMB 409 billion
(2023) and ~RMB 432.5 billion (2024) [1][2]. The competitive set (Chuangshiji, Huazhong CNC, Qinchuan,
Kede) is diversifying into high-end, multi-axis, high-speed machines, driven by aerospace, auto, and
electronics demand. The equipment-renewal policy adds a multi-year demand tailwind.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Machine tools is a **mother-machine + upgrading industry** — its value is that it makes the
machines that make everything else, so it is the highest-leverage layer in manufacturing. The 33.68%
CNC-cutting output jump signals an accelerated high-end-CNC substitution cycle, not just cyclical
recovery.

*Opinion:* For buyers, Taizhou is the correct anchor for **mid-to-high-end CNC and general machine tools**.
Its mother-machine role makes it a leverage point — coordinate machine-tool sourcing with downstream
category machinery (e.g., Foshan's ceramic presses). Expect the equipment-renewal policy to keep demand
and pricing firm.

## Buyer notes

- CNC and general machine tools → [Taizhou](/cities/taizhou/).
- High-end, multi-axis CNC → evaluate Chuangshiji/Huazhong/Qinchuan tier.
- Coordinate machine tools + category machinery for integrated capex.

## The Author's Take

*In my view, machine tools is a mother-machine plus upgrading industry — it makes the machines that make everything else, which makes it the highest-leverage layer in manufacturing.*

So coordinate machine-tool purchases against the rest of your factory investment; the multiplier runs both ways. Taizhou is the correct anchor for mid-to-high-end CNC and general machine tools, and its mother-machine role makes it a leverage point.

*This is my editorial judgment, not a verified fact — the sourced figures are above.*
