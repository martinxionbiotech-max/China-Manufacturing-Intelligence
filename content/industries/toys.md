# China Toys & Sports Products Industry

China's toys industry anchors on Chenghai (33% of global plastic toys); sports products on Quanzhou — the
world's toy factory and its sports-goods twin.

## Industry at a glance

- **2024 trend-and-collectible toys retail: RMB 55.83 billion** — the sector's new growth engine [1].
- **Global AI-toy market forecast >US$35 billion by 2030** [2].

## National footprint

- [Chenghai/Shantou](/cities/shantou/): plastic toys (33% of global).
- [Quanzhou](/cities/quanzhou/): sports products (Anta/Xtep/361°).
- [Dongguan](/cities/dongguan/): ~85% of China's art toys.
- [Yiwu](/cities/jinhua/): distribution.

## Data deep-dive

The industry is bifurcating: **traditional plastic toys** (Chenghai's volume core) are mature, while
**trend-and-collectible toys** (blind boxes, figures — RMB 55.83 billion) and **AI toys** (voice,
NLP-enabled) are the fast-growth segments [1][2]. The "toy + IP" and "toy + culture" fusion is pulling
the sector from commodity manufacturing toward cultural-creative products.

## Original analysis

*Inference:* Toys is a **volume-to-IP shift industry** — Chenghai's commodity plastic-toy base (33% of
global) is being layered with Dongguan's art-toy (~85% national) and a rising AI-toy segment. The value
is migrating from molded plastic to IP, collectibility, and embedded intelligence.

*Opinion:* For buyers, anchor **commodity plastic toys → Chenghai**; **art/trend toys → Dongguan**;
**sports products → Quanzhou**. The IP-and-AI segments carry the margin — for differentiated products,
source the IP/AI layer, not just the commodity mold.
